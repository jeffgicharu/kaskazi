# Kaskazi Student Account

## About the Kaskazi Student Account

The Kaskazi Student Account is a current account for university and college students aged 18 to 25. It has no monthly fee and no minimum balance, and it comes with a Kaskazi Debit Card.

To keep your money safer while you study, the Kaskazi Student Account has a daily withdrawal limit of KES 20,000. When you turn 26 or stop being a student, it becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account.

## Who qualifies for a Kaskazi Student Account

You can open a Kaskazi Student Account if you are:

- aged 18 to 25; and
- a student at a university or college, with a valid student ID from it.

Your course can be a certificate, diploma, degree or postgraduate course, as long as your institution is a university or college and your student ID is valid.

You can't open a Kaskazi Student Account if you are 26 or older, even if you are still studying, or if you don't have a valid student ID. The Kaskazi Everyday Account is open to everyone aged 18 and over.

## Documents for a Kaskazi Student Account

You need the same documents as for any Kaskazi Bank personal account, plus your student ID:

- your national ID card or passport;
- your KRA PIN;
- a phone number registered in your name;
- a valid student ID from your university or college; and
- a selfie, if you open the account in the app.

Your student ID must show your name, your institution and a date it is valid until, and the name on it must match the name on your national ID card or passport.

## Opening a Kaskazi Student Account

You can open a Kaskazi Student Account in the app in about 10 minutes, or at any Kaskazi Bank branch. In the app, you photograph your student ID along with your other documents.

There is no minimum opening balance. Your Kaskazi Debit Card is ordered when the account opens, and the first card is free.

## No monthly fee on the Kaskazi Student Account

The Kaskazi Student Account has no monthly fee. You pay KES 0 a month whatever your balance, and you don't need a salary paid in or a minimum balance to avoid a fee.

The fees for individual services still apply, such as using another bank's ATM, a replacement card, a printed statement or a transfer to another bank.

## The Kaskazi Debit Card on the Student Account

Your Kaskazi Student Account comes with a Kaskazi Debit Card, on the same terms as for other Kaskazi accounts.

| Annual fee | KES 0 |

On the Kaskazi Student Account, cash withdrawals with the card also count towards the KES 20,000 daily withdrawal limit, explained below.

You can block and unblock your card yourself at any time in the app or through the Kaskazi assistant.

## The Kaskazi Student Account daily withdrawal limit

You can withdraw up to KES 20,000 in cash from your Kaskazi Student Account each day.

The limit covers all cash withdrawals added together: at Kaskazi Bank ATMs, at other banks' ATMs, at Kaskazi agents and at branches. It resets at the start of each new day.

Card payments in shops and online are not cash withdrawals, so they don't count towards the KES 20,000. They have their own daily limit of KES 300,000. Transfers in the app have their own limits too.

The limit is there because students often carry cash on campus and share accommodation. If your card and PIN are stolen, the most a thief can take in cash in a day is KES 20,000.

### Daily withdrawal limit example

In the morning you take KES 15,000 from a Kaskazi Bank ATM. In the afternoon you try to withdraw KES 8,000 at a Kaskazi agent.

The agent withdrawal is declined, because KES 15,000 + KES 8,000 = KES 23,000, which is more than KES 20,000. You can still withdraw up to KES 5,000 that day. A withdrawal of KES 5,000 at an agent costs KES 50.

The next day, the full KES 20,000 is available again.

### If you need more than KES 20,000 in cash

The KES 20,000 daily limit can't be raised on a Kaskazi Student Account. If you need to pay a large amount, such as rent or fees, pay it by transfer or card instead of cash. School fees payments from your Kaskazi account are free.

## Paying money into your Kaskazi Student Account

Anyone can pay money into your Kaskazi Student Account: your family, a sponsor, your employer if you work part-time, or a student loan or bursary scheme. The KES 20,000 daily limit applies only to cash taken out, not to money paid in.

Cash deposits at Kaskazi agents are free. Transfers from other Kaskazi accounts are free for the person sending.

## When there isn't enough money in your Kaskazi Student Account

The Kaskazi Student Account has no overdraft. If there isn't enough money to cover a card payment, withdrawal or transfer and its fee, it is declined and nothing is taken.

## When your Kaskazi Student Account becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account

Your Kaskazi Student Account becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account automatically in either of these cases, whichever comes first:

- the month after your 26th birthday; or
- when you can no longer show a valid student ID.

Kaskazi Bank tells you in the app before the change happens.

### Conversion at 26: example

Your 26th birthday is on 14 March 2027. Your Kaskazi Student Account becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account in April 2027.

March 2027 is still free. From April 2027, the Everyday Account monthly fee of KES 250 applies for any month in which neither waiver applies.

### Conversion when you finish your course

Kaskazi Bank may ask you to show a valid student ID again, for example when the expiry date on the ID you gave runs out. If you can't show one, because you have finished your course or left it, your account becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account, even if you are still 25 or younger.

If you go back to study before you turn 26, you can ask for your account to be changed back to a Kaskazi Student Account by showing your new valid student ID.

## What changes when a Kaskazi Student Account becomes an Everyday Account

When your Kaskazi Student Account becomes a Kaskazi Everyday Account:

- **staying the same:** your account number, your Kaskazi Debit Card and PIN, your balance, your standing orders and your app login;
- **monthly fee:** the Kaskazi Everyday Account fee of KES 250 a month applies, unless your salary is paid into the account that month or your average balance for the month is KES 50,000 or more;
- **daily withdrawal limit:** the KES 20,000 Student Account limit no longer applies. The card's standard daily ATM limit of KES 100,000 applies instead.

The Kaskazi Everyday Account terms then apply to the account.

### Avoiding the Everyday Account fee after you graduate

If you start work, ask your employer to pay your salary into your former Student Account, which is now a Kaskazi Everyday Account. In any month your salary is paid in, the KES 250 Everyday Account fee is waived.

For example, if your first salary is paid into the account in June 2027, you pay no monthly fee for June 2027.

## Kaskazi Student Account statements

You get a monthly statement for your Kaskazi Student Account in the app, free. A printed statement at a branch, for example for a scholarship or an embassy application, costs KES 300 per statement.

## If you stop using your Kaskazi Student Account

If you don't start any transaction on your Kaskazi Student Account for 12 months, it becomes dormant. Money paid in by others, such as a bursary, doesn't count as a transaction started by you.

You can reactivate it at any branch with your ID, or in the app with a selfie check, at no cost.

## Closing your Kaskazi Student Account

You can close your Kaskazi Student Account at any time, at a branch or in the app. Kaskazi Bank pays the balance to another account you choose, or as cash at a branch, and cancels your debit card.

Before you close it, move any regular payments in, such as a sponsor's or bursary payment, to your new account.
