# Frequently asked questions

These are the questions Kaskazi Bank customers ask most often, with short answers. Each answer gives the figures that apply from 1 October 2026. Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration, so every figure is a sample, but the bank's systems use exactly these figures. For the full detail, see the document named in each answer, such as the "Fees and charges (tariff guide)".

## Opening an account and everyday banking

### What do I need to open a Kaskazi account?

To open a personal Kaskazi account you need your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN, a phone number registered in your name, and, if you open it in the app, a selfie taken in the app. For a Kaskazi Student Account you also need a valid student ID. For a Kaskazi Business Account you also need the certificate of registration or incorporation, the business's KRA PIN, and the IDs and KRA PINs of all signatories.

### Can I open a Kaskazi account on my phone?

Yes. You can open a Kaskazi account in the Kaskazi app in about 10 minutes. Have your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN and the phone number registered in your name ready, and the app will ask you to take a selfie so we can match you to your ID.

### How much do I need to open a Kaskazi account?

The minimum opening balance depends on the account:

| Account | Minimum to open |
|---|---|
| Kaskazi Everyday Account | KES 0 |
| Kaskazi Student Account | KES 0 |
| Kaskazi Goal Saver | KES 1,000 |
| Kaskazi Business Account | KES 5,000 |
| Kaskazi Fixed Deposit | KES 50,000 |
| Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account | USD 100, GBP 100 or EUR 100 |

### How much is the Everyday Account monthly fee, and how do I avoid it?

The Kaskazi Everyday Account costs KES 250 a month. You do not pay it in any month when your salary is paid into the account, or when your average balance for the month is KES 50,000 or more. Meeting either condition is enough.

### Does the Everyday Account pay interest or have an overdraft?

No. The Kaskazi Everyday Account does not pay interest and does not offer an overdraft. Payments that would take the balance below zero are declined. To earn interest, move savings into a Kaskazi Goal Saver or a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit; transfers between your own accounts are free.

### How much does a cheque book cost?

A cheque book of 25 leaves costs KES 750 on the Kaskazi Everyday Account.

### Who can open a Kaskazi Student Account?

The Kaskazi Student Account is for students aged 18 to 25 with a valid student ID from a university or college. It has no monthly fee (KES 0), no minimum opening balance, and comes with a debit card. Cash withdrawals are limited to KES 20,000 a day.

### What happens to my Student Account when I turn 26 or finish college?

Your Kaskazi Student Account converts to a Kaskazi Everyday Account the month after your 26th birthday, or when you can no longer show a valid student ID. Your account number stays the same. From then on, the Everyday Account's monthly fee of KES 250 applies, unless your salary is paid in that month or your average balance is KES 50,000 or more.

### How much does a Kaskazi Business Account cost?

The Kaskazi Business Account costs KES 1,000 a month and includes 30 transactions a month. Each extra transaction costs KES 30. Card and mobile money payments collected through your till come in at no charge to you. You need KES 5,000 to open it, and it can have up to 4 signatories. Statements are free in the app; printed monthly statements cost KES 300 a month.

### Can I keep dollars, pounds or euros with Kaskazi?

Yes, in a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account, in USD, GBP or EUR. You need 100 in the account's currency to open it (USD 100, GBP 100 or EUR 100). The monthly fee is USD 5, GBP 4 or EUR 5. Withdrawing foreign cash at a branch costs 1.0% of the amount, so USD 1,000 in cash costs USD 10. The account does not pay interest.

### How is my Kaskazi account number made up?

A Kaskazi account number has 13 digits: a "0", then the 3-digit code of your branch, then an 8-digit serial number, and finally a check digit that helps catch typing mistakes. If you mistype one digit, the app will usually tell you the number is not valid before you send anything.

### My account has gone dormant. How do I reactivate it?

An account becomes dormant after 12 months with no transaction started by you. Reactivating it is free (KES 0): visit any branch with your ID, or reactivate it in the app with a selfie check. After a long period with no contact, the law requires banks to hand unclaimed money to a government authority, from which you can still claim it.

### Why is Kaskazi asking me to update my documents?

We ask every customer to update their identity details every 3 years, so our records stay correct and your account stays protected. You can update them in the app, with a selfie check, or at any branch with your national ID card or passport and KRA PIN.

## Savings and fixed deposits

### How does the Goal Saver interest rate work?

The Kaskazi Goal Saver pays 5.0% a year on the whole balance when it is KES 10,000 or more, and 3.0% a year on the whole balance when it is below KES 10,000. Interest is calculated daily on the closing balance and paid monthly, on the last working day of the month, after 15.0% withholding tax.

### How many savings goals can I have?

You can set up to 5 goals in your Kaskazi Goal Saver, for example school fees, rent and an emergency fund, each with its own target. The interest rate is based on your Goal Saver balance.

### Can I take money out of my Goal Saver whenever I want?

Yes, but you get 1 free withdrawal a month. Each extra withdrawal in the same month costs KES 150, so 3 withdrawals in a month cost KES 300. The Goal Saver does not come with a debit card; you move money to your Everyday Account in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

### What are the fixed deposit rates?

The Kaskazi Fixed Deposit pays 8.0% a year for 3 months, 8.75% a year for 6 months and 9.5% a year for 12 months. The minimum deposit is KES 50,000. The rate is fixed for the term, and interest is paid at maturity after 15.0% withholding tax.

### How much will I earn on KES 100,000 in a fixed deposit for a year?

At 9.5% a year for 12 months, KES 100,000 earns about KES 9,500 before tax. Withholding tax of 15.0% is about KES 1,425, so you receive about KES 8,075 in interest at maturity, plus your KES 100,000. The exact figure depends on the number of days.

### Can I break my fixed deposit before it matures?

Yes, you can withdraw early, but you lose half of the interest earned so far. You always get your full deposit back. Partial withdrawals are not allowed, so you cannot take out part and leave the rest. For example, breaking a KES 200,000 12-month deposit at 9.5% after 6 months means you keep about KES 4,750 of the roughly KES 9,500 earned, before tax.

### What happens when my fixed deposit matures?

Your Kaskazi Fixed Deposit rolls over automatically for the same term at the rate on the rollover date. If you want the money paid out, or a different term, tell us at least 2 working days before the maturity date, in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

### Do you deduct tax from my interest?

Yes. Withholding tax of 15.0% is deducted from Goal Saver and Fixed Deposit interest before it is paid, and Kaskazi pays it to the tax authority for you. On KES 1,000 of interest, KES 150 is deducted and you receive KES 850.

## Debit and credit cards

### Do I pay for the Kaskazi Debit Card?

No. Your first Kaskazi Debit Card costs KES 0 and there is no annual fee. A replacement card costs KES 500. Cards are valid for 4 years.

### How much does it cost to withdraw at another bank's ATM?

With your Kaskazi Debit Card, withdrawals cost KES 0 at Kaskazi ATMs, KES 35 at another bank's ATM in Kenya, and KES 300 at an ATM abroad, plus a 3.0% currency conversion fee abroad. A balance enquiry at another bank's ATM costs KES 25.

### What are the debit card limits?

The Kaskazi Debit Card lets you withdraw up to KES 100,000 a day at ATMs and spend up to KES 300,000 a day in shops and online together. Contactless payments are limited to KES 5,000 per payment; above that, insert the card and enter your PIN. Student Account cards are limited to KES 20,000 a day in cash withdrawals.

### I entered the wrong PIN three times and my card stopped working. What now?

Your Kaskazi Debit Card locks after 3 wrong PIN attempts, to protect you if someone else is trying to guess it. Set a new PIN in the Kaskazi app, or visit a branch with your national ID card or passport. If you did not make the attempts, block the card and tell us.

### I have lost my card. What should I do?

Block it straight away in the Kaskazi app or through the assistant; you can do this yourself at any time. If you think you have just misplaced it, use a temporary block, which you can undo later. If it is lost for good or stolen, report it as lost or stolen: the card is cancelled for good and you order a replacement, which costs KES 500 and is ready at the branch you choose within 5 working days.

### Will my subscriptions still be paid if I block my card?

No. While your card is blocked, all payments on it are declined, including subscriptions and other payments you set up before the block. To unblock the card, use the app or the assistant; you will need a one-time code sent to your registered phone number. A card reported lost or stolen cannot be unblocked.

### Who can get a Kaskazi Credit Card, and what limit will I get?

You need a net monthly pay of at least KES 50,000. Credit limits are from KES 30,000 to KES 500,000, depending on what you can afford. Apply in the app, through the assistant or at a branch. Payments that would take you over your limit are declined, so there is no over-limit fee.

### How much interest does the credit card charge?

The Kaskazi Credit Card charges 2.5% a month on balances you carry over, which is 30% a year. Purchases have up to 50 days without interest: pay the full balance by the due date and you pay no interest on them. Carrying over KES 40,000 for a month costs about KES 1,000 in interest.

### What is the minimum payment on my credit card?

The minimum payment is 5.0% of the balance or KES 1,000, whichever is more, or the full balance if it is less than KES 1,000. On a balance of KES 60,000 that is KES 3,000. If the minimum payment is late, a late payment fee of KES 1,000 is charged.

### Can I withdraw cash with my credit card?

Yes, but it is expensive. A cash advance costs 3.0% of the amount, with a minimum of KES 500, and interest is charged from the day of the withdrawal, with no interest-free period. Withdrawing KES 10,000 costs KES 500 in fees; KES 30,000 costs KES 900.

### Is there an annual fee for the credit card?

The Kaskazi Credit Card's annual fee is KES 2,000, waived for the first year. A replacement card costs KES 500, and card payments in a foreign currency cost 3.0% of the amount.

### There is a card payment I don't recognise. What do I do?

Block your card in the app or through the assistant, then report the payment as a dispute within 60 days of the transaction date. We acknowledge disputes within 24 hours. For disputes on amounts up to KES 20,000, we give a provisional credit within 10 working days while we investigate. Investigations take up to 45 days.

### How much could I lose if someone uses my card fraudulently?

The most you lose for card fraud before you report it is KES 5,000, unless you were careless with your PIN or one-time code. After you report it, you lose nothing (KES 0). That is why it pays to block the card and tell us the moment you notice.

## Loans and salary advances

### How much can I borrow with a Kaskazi Personal Loan?

You can borrow from KES 50,000 to KES 3,000,000, repaid over 6 to 60 months in equal monthly instalments. The amount we offer depends on your pay and your other loan repayments: all your monthly loan repayments, including the new one, must be no more than 40.0% of your net monthly pay.

### What is the interest rate on a personal loan?

The Kaskazi Personal Loan charges 15.5% a year on the reducing balance. That is the Kaskazi Base Rate of 11.5% plus a margin of 4.0 percentage points. Interest is worked out each month on what you still owe.

### What fees are charged on a personal loan?

There is an arrangement fee of 2.5% of the loan (minimum KES 2,000) and credit life insurance of 0.5% of the loan for each year of the term, with part years counting as a full year. Both are deducted from the loan before it is paid to you. There is also a late payment fee of KES 500 for each missed instalment.

### If I borrow KES 500,000, how much will I receive and repay?

On KES 500,000 over 36 months, the arrangement fee is KES 12,500 and the insurance is KES 7,500, so KES 480,000 is paid into your Everyday Account. Your monthly instalment is about KES 17,455.34, worked out on the full KES 500,000 at 15.5% a year.

### Who qualifies for a personal loan?

You must be at least 21, and no older than 65 at the end of the loan. Your net monthly pay must be at least KES 25,000, and we need 3 months of salary history: salary paid into Kaskazi, or statements from another bank. Your loan repayments together must not exceed 40.0% of your net monthly pay.

### What documents do I need for a loan?

You need your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN, your payslips for the latest 3 months with no gaps, and, if your salary goes to another bank, that bank's statements for 6 months with every page included. The names must match on every document and your ID must not have expired.

### How long does a loan decision take?

We aim to decide within 2 working days after we have received all your documents. If you accept the offer, the loan is paid into your Kaskazi Everyday Account the same working day.

### Can I repay my personal loan early?

Yes. During the first 12 months, an early repayment fee of 2.0% of the amount repaid early applies; repaying KES 200,000 early in month 8 costs KES 4,000. After 12 months there is no fee.

### What happens if I miss a loan repayment?

A late payment fee of KES 500 is charged for each missed instalment. If you are struggling to pay, tell us as early as you can, through the assistant or at a branch; the assistant passes money worries to a person so we can talk through your situation.

### What is a Kaskazi Salary Advance and how much does it cost?

A salary advance lets you borrow part of your next salary. You can borrow KES 1,000 to KES 100,000, up to 50.0% of your net pay, for a flat one-off fee of 3.0% with no interest. It is repaid in full from your next salary. You need 3 consecutive monthly salaries paid into your Kaskazi account, and you can have 1 advance at a time.

### What if my salary doesn't arrive to repay my salary advance?

If your salary does not arrive, the advance plus a fee of KES 500 is collected from the account when money next comes in. Also note the one-third rule: after the advance is repaid from your salary, you must still take home at least one third of your basic pay, so we may offer a smaller advance.

## Payments and transfers

### How much is an instant transfer to another bank?

Instant transfers to another bank in Kenya cost KES 30 for KES 1 to KES 10,000, KES 60 for KES 10,001 to KES 100,000, and KES 120 for KES 100,001 to KES 999,999. The most you can send in one instant transfer is KES 999,999, and the money usually arrives within moments.

### What is the difference between an instant transfer, EFT and RTGS?

An instant transfer arrives within moments and costs KES 30 to KES 120. A next-day transfer (EFT) costs KES 100 and arrives the next working day if sent before the 3:00 pm cut-off. A same-day high-value transfer (RTGS) costs KES 500, is for KES 1,000,000 or more, must be made before the 2:00 pm cut-off on a working day, and is made at a branch.

### How much does it cost to send money to mobile money?

Sending to a mobile money wallet costs KES 15 for KES 1 to KES 1,000, KES 35 for KES 1,001 to KES 10,000, KES 55 for KES 10,001 to KES 50,000, and KES 85 for KES 50,001 to KES 150,000. The most per transfer is KES 150,000, and the most in a day is KES 300,000.

### Is it free to send money to another Kaskazi account?

Yes. Transfers between your own Kaskazi accounts and to other Kaskazi customers cost KES 0 and arrive straight away. A transfer out of a Goal Saver counts as a withdrawal, though, so after the first free withdrawal in a month each costs KES 150.

### Why can I only send KES 50,000 to someone new?

When you pay someone for the first time in the app, online banking, USSD or through the assistant, you can send them up to KES 50,000 in the first 24 hours. This protects you from scams and account takeovers, which usually rush money to a new recipient. After 24 hours your normal limits apply. For a bigger payment straight away, visit a branch.

### What is the most I can send in a day from the app?

The daily digital limit is KES 500,000, across the app, online banking, USSD and the assistant together. Mobile money also has its own daily limit of KES 300,000 inside that. For larger payments, visit a branch with your ID.

### How much does it cost to pay bills and school fees?

Bill payments cost KES 30 each. School fees payments made through the school fees option are free (KES 0). Paying a school by ordinary transfer to another bank costs the normal transfer fee instead, so use the school fees option if your school is listed.

### How much is a standing order?

Setting up a standing order costs KES 0, and each payment it makes costs KES 50. A monthly rent standing order therefore costs KES 50 a month. Set up, change or cancel standing orders in the app, online banking, through the assistant or at a branch, before the payment date.

### Can I send money abroad, and how much does it cost?

Yes. Sending money abroad costs KES 2,500, plus any charges by the banks in between, which may be taken from the amount on the way. Receiving money from abroad into your Kaskazi account costs KES 0, although the sender's bank may charge them.

### I sent money to the wrong number. Can I get it back?

Tell us at once through the assistant, the app or a branch, with the date, amount and reference. We contact the recipient, their bank or the mobile money provider and ask for the money to be returned. We cannot take money from someone else's account without their agreement or a lawful order, so recovery is not guaranteed. You get a reference starting KZC-, and we acknowledge it within 24 hours.

### Kwa nini EFT yangu haijafika? Why hasn't my EFT arrived?

An EFT sent before 3:00 pm on a working day normally arrives the next working day. One sent after 3:00 pm, or at the weekend or on a public holiday, goes on the next working day, so it arrives a working day later. If it is later than that, contact us with the transaction reference.

## The app, online banking and USSD

### Does the Kaskazi app cost anything?

No. The Kaskazi app and online banking are free (KES 0), and so are statements in the app. You only pay the normal fee for payments you make, which is the same in every channel.

### My one-time code has expired. What do I do?

One-time codes are valid for 5 minutes and work only once. If yours has expired, ask for a new one on the same screen; only the newest code works. If you get a code you did not ask for, do not share it with anyone and tell us.

### Why does the app keep logging me out?

For your security, the Kaskazi app and online banking sign you out after 5 minutes without activity. A payment you had not confirmed before the timeout is not sent, so sign in and start it again. Payments you had already confirmed go through.

### I'm locked out of the app. How do I get back in?

Access locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts. To unlock it, choose to reset your PIN or password on the sign-in screen, enter your ID number and the one-time code we send to your registered phone, take a selfie, and set a new PIN or password. If you no longer have your registered phone, visit a branch with your ID.

### Can I use Kaskazi without internet?

Yes, with USSD banking from the phone number registered on your account. Kaskazi charges nothing for USSD sessions (KES 0), although your mobile network may charge. You can check your balance, see a mini statement, transfer money, send to mobile money, pay bills and block your card. The USSD code is not available in this demonstration.

### How do I get a bank statement?

Download one in the Kaskazi app or online banking for free (KES 0): open the account, choose statements and pick your dates. A statement printed at a branch costs KES 300.

### Is the Kaskazi assistant a real person?

No. The Kaskazi assistant is an AI assistant in the app and online banking. It answers from the bank's documents and tells you which document each answer comes from. It can check your balances, block a card or prepare a payment, but it always shows you what it is about to do and asks you to confirm first. It never asks for your PIN, password, one-time code or full card number.

### Can I talk to a real person instead of the assistant?

Yes, at any time: just ask the assistant for a person. Our team staffs the chat from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, every day, and aims to reply within 5 minutes during those hours. Outside those hours your conversation waits in the queue until the team is next in. Card blocks never wait, because you can do them yourself at any time.

### Naweza kuongea na assistant kwa Kiswahili?

Yes. You can write to the Kaskazi assistant in English or Swahili, and it replies in the language you use. If you prefer, ask for a person, who can also help you in Swahili.

## Kaskazi agents

### What can I do at a Kaskazi agent?

At a Kaskazi agent you can deposit cash for free (KES 0), withdraw cash up to KES 50,000 at a time, check your balance for KES 10 and get a mini statement for KES 15. Agents cannot open accounts, take loan applications or replace cards.

### How much does it cost to withdraw cash at an agent?

Agent withdrawals cost KES 30 for KES 1 to KES 2,500, KES 50 for KES 2,501 to KES 5,000, KES 80 for KES 5,001 to KES 10,000, KES 120 for KES 10,001 to KES 20,000, and KES 200 for KES 20,001 to KES 50,000. The fee is taken from your account; never pay an agent extra cash.

### What are the limits at an agent?

You can withdraw up to KES 50,000 at a time at a Kaskazi agent, and up to KES 100,000 a day through agents in total. To withdraw KES 70,000 in one day, make two withdrawals, for example KES 50,000 and KES 20,000, which cost KES 200 and KES 120.

### An agent asked for my PIN. Is that normal?

No. A genuine Kaskazi agent will never ask for your PIN, one-time code or app password, to hold your phone, or for extra cash on top of the fee. You type your PIN yourself on your own phone or the card machine. Do not go ahead, and report the agent through the assistant, the app or a branch.

## Security and fraud

### Someone called saying they're from Kaskazi and asked for my code. What should I do?

Hang up. Kaskazi Bank staff, agents and the assistant will never ask for your PIN, password, one-time code or full card number, and will never ask you to move money to a "safe account". If you shared anything, block your card in the app or through the assistant, change your app PIN, and tell us straight away.

### How can I keep my Kaskazi account safe?

Never share your PIN, password or one-time codes. Lock your phone and download the Kaskazi app only from your phone's official app store. Check the recipient's name before you confirm any payment. Be wary of urgent requests to pay, and of messages saying a supplier's bank details have changed. Check your transactions regularly and report anything odd.

### My phone was stolen. What do I do about my Kaskazi app?

Tell us at once through online banking, the assistant on another device, or a branch, so we can stop app access on the stolen phone. Block your cards as well. Ask your mobile network to block the SIM, and update your registered phone number with us once you have a new one.

## Complaints

### How do I make a complaint?

Tell the Kaskazi assistant, use complaints in the app, or speak to anyone at a branch. You get a reference in the form KZC- followed by 6 digits. Keep it and quote it whenever you contact us. Someone else can complain for you with your permission.

### How long will my complaint take?

We acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 7 working days. If it is complex, for example because we need information from another bank, we aim to resolve it within 30 days and will tell you why.

### What if I'm not happy with how my complaint was handled?

You can take it to the next level: first the agent or branch who handled it, then the Customer Experience team, and finally the Head of Customer Experience, who gives the bank's final answer. Quote your KZC- reference and ask to escalate. In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

## Privacy and your data

### Can I get a copy of the information Kaskazi holds about me?

Yes. Ask through the assistant, the app or a branch, and we will respond to your request within 7 days.

### How long do you keep my information after I close my account?

We keep your records for 7 years after your account is closed, and then delete them securely.

### Who can see my conversations with the assistant?

Your conversation with the Kaskazi assistant is part of your records. If the assistant passes you to a person, that member of staff sees the conversation so you do not have to repeat yourself. Account numbers, card numbers and any codes you typed are masked in what staff see.
