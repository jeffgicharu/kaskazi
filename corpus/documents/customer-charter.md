# Our customer charter

This charter sets out the service you can expect from Kaskazi Bank: how quickly we aim to serve you, how we treat you, and what to do if we fall short. It applies in every branch, on the phone, in the Kaskazi app and when you chat with the assistant.

## Kaskazi Bank's promises in brief

- We will be clear about what our products cost, before you sign up and whenever prices change.
- We will serve you quickly and tell you honestly when something will take longer.
- We will treat you fairly and with respect, whoever you are and however much money you have.
- We will protect your money and your personal data.
- We will put things right when we get them wrong, and tell you how to take it further if you are not satisfied.

## Kaskazi Bank's service standards

These are the targets we set ourselves. We measure how often we meet them.

| Service | Our standard |
|---|---|
| Waiting in a branch queue | served within 15 minutes |
| Calling the contact centre | answered within 60 seconds, 24 hours a day, every day |
| Asking the assistant for a person | handed over within 5 minutes, during staffed hours (7:00 am to 10:00 pm, every day) |
| Acknowledging a complaint | within 24 hours |
| Resolving a complaint | within 7 working days |
| Resolving a complex complaint | within 30 days |
| Replacement card | ready within 5 working days, at the branch you choose |
| Blocking a card | at any time, yourself, in the app or through the assistant |
| Opening an account in the app | about 10 minutes |
| Acknowledging a card dispute | within 24 hours |
| Responding to a request for your personal data | within 7 days |

## Service in Kaskazi Bank branches

Our branches are open:

- 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday
- 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday
- closed on Sundays and public holidays

We aim to serve you within 15 minutes of joining the queue. If you are waiting longer, please tell a member of staff. Busy times are usually the start and end of the month, and lunchtime.

Our branches are in Mombasa (Mombasa Head Office), Malindi, Kilifi, Nairobi City Centre, Westlands in Nairobi, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Nyeri and Machakos.

Many things don't need a branch visit at all. You can check balances, get statements, make transfers, block and unblock cards and open some accounts in the Kaskazi app.

## Kaskazi Bank's contact centre

Our contact centre is open 24 hours a day, every day. We aim to answer your call within 60 seconds. The contact centre's number is not available in this demonstration.

When you call, we confirm who you are with questions about you and your account. We will never ask for your PIN, your full card number, a one-time code or your app password.

## The Kaskazi assistant and talking to a person

The Kaskazi assistant answers questions from our own documents, at any time of day, and can help with tasks such as blocking a card.

You can ask to talk to a person at any point. During staffed hours, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm every day, we aim to hand you over within 5 minutes. Outside staffed hours, the conversation waits in the queue and a person replies when the team is next in.

Card blocks never wait, because you can block your card yourself at any time in the app or through the assistant.

The assistant will tell you when it doesn't know the answer, rather than guess. Our privacy notice explains how the assistant handles your data.

## Opening an account in the Kaskazi app

You can open an account in the Kaskazi app in about 10 minutes. You will need:

- your national ID card or passport
- your KRA PIN
- a phone number registered in your name
- a selfie, taken in the app

A Kaskazi Student Account also needs a valid student ID.

If something goes wrong part way through, you can carry on later or finish at any branch.

## Kaskazi cards: blocks and replacements

If your card is lost, stolen or you just can't find it, you can block it yourself straight away in the Kaskazi app or through the assistant, at any time of day or night.

A replacement card is ready within 5 working days, to collect at the branch you choose. The replacement fee is KES 500.

## Complaints: how Kaskazi Bank puts things right

If you are unhappy with anything we have done, or not done, please tell us. You can complain in the app, through the assistant, at any branch or through the contact centre.

- We acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours.
- We aim to resolve it within 7 working days.
- If your complaint is complex, for example it needs information from another bank or business, it may take up to 30 days. We will tell you if this applies and keep you updated.

Every complaint gets a reference starting "KZC-" followed by 6 digits. Quote it whenever you contact us about the complaint.

### Taking a complaint further

If you are not happy with our response, you can ask for your complaint to be reviewed by, in turn:

1. the agent or branch who handled it
2. the Customer Experience team
3. the Head of Customer Experience

In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

## How Kaskazi Bank treats customers fairly

Treating you fairly means:

- **Clear prices.** Our fees, rates and limits are published, and we tell you before any change.
- **Products that suit you.** We only offer you credit we believe you can afford, and we explain the costs, including interest and fees, before you agree.
- **No pressure.** You can take information away, think about it and ask questions before you decide.
- **Help when things are hard.** If you are struggling to repay a loan or card, talk to us early. We would rather agree a plan with you than add fees.
- **Honest answers.** If we can't do something, we will tell you why.
- **Respect.** Every customer is treated with courtesy, whatever their background, age, gender, religion, disability or the size of their balance.

## Accessibility at Kaskazi Bank

We want our services to work for everyone.

- **In branches:** customers who are older, pregnant, have a disability or are unwell can ask to be served without waiting in the main queue. Staff will help you with forms if you ask.
- **If you can't sign:** we can arrange another way for you to confirm instructions.
- **In the app:** the Kaskazi app works with your phone's text size and screen reader settings.
- **Someone to help you:** you can bring a trusted person to a branch, or arrange for someone to act for you, for example through a power of attorney.
- **If you need more time:** tell us, and we will slow down, explain again, or give you information to take away.

If something about our service makes it hard for you to bank with us, please tell us, so that we can fix it.

## Languages: English and Swahili

You can bank with us in English or Swahili. Our branch staff, contact centre and the Kaskazi assistant can all help you in either language. You can switch languages at any time, even part way through a conversation with the assistant.

Our product terms are written in plain English, and staff will explain them in Swahili if you prefer.

## What Kaskazi Bank asks of you

Good service works both ways. To help us serve you well, we ask you to:

- keep your phone number and other details up to date, because one-time codes and important messages go to your registered phone number
- update your identity details when we ask, every 3 years
- keep your PIN, one-time codes and passwords secret, and block your card straight away if you think it is lost or misused
- check your statements and tell us quickly if something looks wrong
- tell us early if you are having trouble repaying a loan or card
- treat our staff with the same courtesy we promise you

## When Kaskazi Bank misses a standard

Our standards are targets, and occasionally we will miss them, for example on very busy days or when a system is down. When that happens we will be honest about it, tell you what we are doing, and give you a realistic time.

If we have missed a standard and it has caused you a problem, tell us. You can raise it as a complaint, and we will look into what happened and put it right where we can.

We review this charter regularly and update it when our services change.
