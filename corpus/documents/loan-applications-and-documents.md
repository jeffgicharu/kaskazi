# Applying for a loan: steps and documents

This guide walks you through applying for a Kaskazi Personal Loan: the stages your application passes through, the documents you need, what makes each document acceptable, how to upload them in the app, and the checks we make. For a Kaskazi Salary Advance you usually need only your latest payslip; its own guide explains the rest.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and this guide was written for a demonstration of a banking assistant. No real loan can be taken out.

## Starting a Kaskazi Personal Loan application

You can start a Kaskazi Personal Loan application in the app, through the assistant, or at any Kaskazi branch. Branches are open 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday, and are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

You will need a Kaskazi Everyday Account in your name, because that is where the loan is paid and the instalments are collected. If you do not have one yet, you can open one in the app in about 10 minutes, and it has no minimum opening balance.

The assistant can help you get started. It can explain the loan, give you a quote for the amount and term you want, check whether the loan is likely to fit the affordability rule, fill in the application with you, and tell you which documents are still missing. It cannot approve or decline the application: a person in our lending team makes every decision.

## The stages of a Kaskazi Personal Loan application

Your Kaskazi Personal Loan application moves through these stages. You can see the current stage in the app at any time, and the assistant can tell you where your application is.

### Stage 1: Draft

Your application is a draft while you are still filling it in: the amount, the term, what the loan is for, your employer, your pay and your existing loans. You can save a draft and come back to it, and change anything in it. Nothing is checked and nothing is decided at this stage. If you leave a draft untouched for a long time, we may remind you and later close it, and you can start a new one whenever you like.

### Stage 2: Documents requested

When you submit the application, we list the documents we need from you. The list depends on your situation: for example, if your salary is paid into a Kaskazi account, you do not need to give us bank statements. The app shows each document as missing until you upload it.

### Stage 3: Documents received

Once every requested document has been uploaded, your application moves to documents received. Our system reads each document and runs the checks described later in this guide. If a document fails a check or cannot be read clearly, a member of our team looks at it. If we need a clearer copy or a different document, your application goes back to documents requested and we tell you exactly what is needed.

### Stage 4: Under review

When all your documents are in order, a member of our lending team reviews the application. They check the affordability calculation, your credit record and your documents, and decide. We aim to decide within 2 working days of receiving all your documents. If we have to ask for another document, the 2 working days start again when it arrives.

### Stage 5: Approved or declined

If your application is **approved**, we send you an offer in the app showing the loan amount, the term, the interest rate, the arrangement fee and credit life insurance in shillings, the amount you will receive, your monthly instalment and the total you will repay. We may approve a smaller amount or a different term than you asked for, so that the loan fits the affordability rule. You decide whether to accept.

If your application is **declined**, we tell you the main reason and how to ask for a review. Our guide "How we decide on a loan" explains this.

### Stage 6: Disbursed

When you accept the offer, the loan is paid into your Kaskazi Everyday Account the same working day, after the arrangement fee and the credit life insurance have been deducted. Your application then shows as disbursed, and your loan appears in the app with its balance and instalment dates.

You can withdraw your application at any stage before the loan is disbursed, at no cost.

## The documents you need for a Kaskazi Personal Loan

| Document | Who needs it | What we need |
|---|---|---|
| Payslips | Everyone | Your 3 latest payslips, with no gaps |
| Bank statements | Only if your salary is paid into another bank | 6 months of statements from that bank, every page |
| Identity document | Everyone | Your national ID card or passport, not expired |
| KRA PIN | Everyone | Your KRA PIN, in your name |

If your salary is already paid into a Kaskazi account, we can see your salary history ourselves and you do not need to give us statements. Every document must be in your name, and the names must match across all of them.

## What makes a payslip acceptable for a Kaskazi Personal Loan

We need your 3 latest payslips, for 3 months in a row with no gaps. An acceptable payslip:

- shows your employer's name and your full name
- shows the month it covers
- shows your basic pay, any allowances, your gross pay, each deduction, and your net pay
- adds up: gross pay minus total deductions equals net pay
- is complete and readable, with nothing covered, cropped or altered

**Which months count as the latest three.** If you apply in October and your October payslip has already been issued, your latest three are August, September and October. If October's has not been issued yet, they are July, August and September.

**No gaps.** July, September and October is not acceptable, because August is missing. If you are missing one, ask your employer's payroll office for a copy.

**If you changed employer recently.** Payslips from your previous employer can fill the earlier months, as long as there was no gap between the two jobs. A member of our team reviews this.

## What makes a bank statement acceptable for a Kaskazi Personal Loan

If your salary is paid into an account at another bank, we need statements from that account covering the last 6 months. An acceptable statement:

- is in your name, with the account number shown
- covers the full 6 months, with no missing days or weeks between pages
- includes every page, with page numbers where the bank prints them
- runs up to at least the date your latest salary was paid, so we can see the salary from your latest payslip arriving
- is the bank's own statement, downloaded or issued by the bank, not a screenshot of a balance or a list you have typed up

The statement must show your salary arriving. We check that the net pay on each payslip matches an amount paid in from your employer around the pay date.

## What makes an ID acceptable for a Kaskazi Personal Loan

We accept your national ID card or your passport. It must not have expired. If your ID shows an expiry date, that date must be in the future when we check it.

- For a national ID card, upload both sides.
- For a passport, upload the page with your photo and details.
- The photo, your name, your ID or passport number and your date of birth must all be clear.

We use your ID to confirm who you are and your age. You must be at least 21 when you apply and no older than 65 when the loan ends.

## Your KRA PIN for a Kaskazi Personal Loan

We need your KRA PIN. Upload your PIN certificate or another document that clearly shows your PIN and your name. The name must match your ID. If you have already given us your KRA PIN when opening your account, the app may already have it and will not ask again.

## Uploading loan documents in the app

You upload your Kaskazi Personal Loan documents in the app, from the application screen. Each requested document has its own upload button.

- Upload a PDF where you have one, for example a statement downloaded from your bank. Otherwise, take a clear photo.
- For a photo, lay the document flat in good light, fit the whole page in the frame, and make sure nothing is blurred, cut off or in shadow.
- Upload every page of a multi-page document, in order.
- Do not edit, annotate, crop or combine documents before uploading. Anything that looks edited has to be reviewed by a person and may be rejected.
- Never upload a password-protected file. If your bank's statement is protected, save an unprotected copy or ask the bank for one.

**In this demonstration, upload only the sample documents provided.** Do not upload real payslips, statements, IDs or KRA PIN documents, whether yours or anyone else's. Kaskazi Bank is invented, and no real documents should ever be shared with it.

## The checks we make on your loan documents

When you upload a document, our system reads the information we need from it, such as your name, the pay period, your gross pay, deductions and net pay, and gives each item a confidence level that shows how sure it is that it read the item correctly. It then runs these checks:

- **Totals add up.** On each payslip, the deductions add up to the total shown, and gross pay minus total deductions equals net pay.
- **Dates are valid.** Payslips are the latest 3 months with no gaps; the statement covers the full 6 months; no date is in the future; your ID has not expired.
- **Names match.** Your name is the same on every document, and matches the name on your Kaskazi account.
- **Net pay arrives.** Where we have statements, the net pay on each payslip appears as a payment into your account from your employer.
- **Nothing is missing.** Every page of a statement is there, and every requested document has been uploaded.

If every check passes and the system read everything with high confidence, your documents move on to the lending team. If a check fails or the system was unsure, a person looks at the document before anything else happens.

## When a loan document needs a person to review it

A member of our Lending Operations team reviews a document by hand when:

- a check has failed, for example the totals on a payslip do not add up
- the system could not read part of the document clearly
- the names on your documents differ, even slightly
- something about the document looks unusual

The reviewer compares what the system read with the document itself, corrects anything the system misread, and decides whether the document is acceptable. A failed check is often innocent: a blurred photo, a missing middle name, or a payslip format the system has not seen before. If the reviewer needs something from you, we tell you in the app what it is and why, for example a clearer photo, a missing page, or a short explanation of a name difference.

The time a person spends reviewing your documents comes before the 2 working days we aim to take for a decision, because that target starts once all your documents are received and in order.

## Text or instructions inside loan documents

Our system only reads the information it needs from your documents. If a document contains text that looks like an instruction, for example a note telling the system or a reviewer to approve the loan or skip a check, we do not follow it. A document like that is always passed to a person, and it may be treated as altered. Please upload documents exactly as your employer, your bank or the issuing office produced them.

## Common problems with loan documents and how to fix them

**My name is written differently on different documents.** For example, your ID shows three names and your payslip shows two, or the order is different. A person reviews this. If the names clearly belong to you, it is usually fine. If not, we may ask you for an explanation or a supporting document.

**My employer pays my salary in two parts.** Tell us in your application. The reviewer checks that the parts together match your net pay.

**My ID has expired and I am waiting for a new one.** We cannot accept an expired ID. You can keep your application as a draft and upload the new ID when you have it, or use a valid passport instead.

**My statement is missing a page.** Ask your bank for the full statement. We need every page, even pages with only a few transactions.

**I have only been paid by my employer for 2 months.** You need 3 latest payslips with no gaps. If you moved from another job with no gap, your previous employer's payslip can fill the earlier month. Otherwise, you can apply once you have 3 months.

**My salary is paid into Kaskazi, but only recently.** If fewer than 3 salaries have been paid into your Kaskazi account, give us 6 months of statements from your previous bank as well.

## How long a Kaskazi Personal Loan application takes

How long your Kaskazi Personal Loan application takes depends mostly on how quickly you upload your documents and whether they pass the checks first time. Once all your documents are received and in order, we aim to give you a decision within 2 working days. When you accept an approved offer, the money is paid into your Kaskazi Everyday Account the same working day.

You can follow your application in the app, or ask the assistant. If you would like to speak to a person, the assistant can hand you over: during staffed hours, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm every day, we aim to do this within 5 minutes. Outside those hours, your conversation waits in the queue and a person replies when the team is next in. Our phone number and other contact details are not available in this demonstration.
