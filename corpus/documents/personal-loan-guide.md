# Kaskazi Personal Loan: product guide

This guide explains the Kaskazi Personal Loan in plain terms: what it is for, how much you can borrow, what it costs, and what happens if you want to pay it off early. It is a summary. The full rules are in the Kaskazi Personal Loan terms and conditions, which you accept before any money is paid to you. If anything in this guide seems to differ from the terms, the terms apply.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and this guide was written for a demonstration of a banking assistant. No real loan can be taken out.

## What the Kaskazi Personal Loan is for

The Kaskazi Personal Loan is an unsecured loan for people who earn a regular salary. Unsecured means you do not pledge a title deed, a logbook or any other asset. We lend on the strength of your salary and your record of repaying what you owe.

People use a Kaskazi Personal Loan for things they would rather pay for over time than all at once, for example:

- school or college fees for a full year
- building, finishing or repairing a home
- a wedding, a funeral or another family obligation
- medical bills
- paying off more expensive borrowing, such as several mobile loans, so that you have one instalment at a lower rate

You do not have to prove what you spend the money on. We do ask what the loan is for, because it helps us check that the amount and the term make sense for you.

The Kaskazi Personal Loan is not a mortgage, a car loan or a business loan. Kaskazi Bank does not offer loans secured on property or vehicles, and the personal loan is for individuals, not for a business. If you only need a small amount until your next pay day, the Kaskazi Salary Advance may suit you better; it has its own guide.

## How much you can borrow with a Kaskazi Personal Loan, and for how long

| | |
|---|---|
| Smallest loan | KES 50,000 |
| Largest loan | KES 3,000,000 |
| Shortest term | 6 months |
| Longest term | 60 months |
| Repayments | Equal monthly instalments |

You choose the amount and the term within those limits. The amount we can actually offer depends on your net monthly pay and the other loans you are already repaying. All your monthly loan repayments, including the new Kaskazi Personal Loan instalment, must come to no more than 40% of your net monthly pay. Our guide "How we decide on a loan" explains this rule with worked examples, including how to work out the largest loan that fits.

A longer term gives you a lower instalment but means you pay more interest overall. A shorter term costs less in total but needs a bigger instalment each month. The worked examples below show the difference.

Your age also limits the term. You must be no older than 65 when the loan ends. If you are close to that age, the longest term open to you may be shorter than 60 months.

## The Kaskazi Personal Loan interest rate: base rate plus margin

The Kaskazi Personal Loan interest rate is 15.5% a year. It is made of two parts:

- the **Kaskazi Base Rate**, which is currently 11.5% a year and is set by Kaskazi Bank, and
- a **margin** of 4.0 percentage points, which stays the same for the whole life of your loan.

11.5% plus 4.0 percentage points gives 15.5% a year.

Interest is charged on the **reducing balance**. Each month we work out interest on what you still owe, not on the amount you first borrowed. The monthly rate is 15.5% divided by 12. Early in the loan, more of each instalment goes to interest; later, more goes to paying off the loan itself. Your instalment stays the same throughout, unless the Kaskazi Base Rate changes.

If the Kaskazi Base Rate goes up or down, your rate moves by the same amount, because the margin is fixed. We tell you in writing before the change takes effect, and we recalculate your instalment so that the loan still ends on the same date. The terms and conditions set out exactly how this works.

## Kaskazi Personal Loan fees and credit life insurance

Two one-off charges apply to every Kaskazi Personal Loan. Both are deducted from the loan before the money is paid to you, so you do not need cash in hand to pay them.

| Charge | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangement fee | 2.5% of the loan, with a minimum of KES 2,000 | Deducted before payout |
| Credit life insurance | 0.5% of the loan for each year of the term; a part year counts as a full year | Deducted before payout |

Because the charges come out of the loan, the amount that reaches your account is less than the amount you borrow. You repay, with interest, the full amount you borrowed.

**Part years count as full years.** A 6-month loan is charged one year of insurance. An 18-month loan is charged two years. A 36-month loan is charged three years.

**Credit life insurance** pays off what you still owe on your Kaskazi Personal Loan if you die before it is repaid, so the debt does not pass to your family. It does not cover losing your job. The terms and conditions explain how a claim works. In this demonstration no real insurer is involved.

Other charges apply only if something goes wrong or you choose to repay early:

| Charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Late payment fee | KES 500 for each missed instalment |
| Early repayment fee | 2.0% of the amount you repay early, during the first 12 months only |

There is no fee to apply, no fee for a decision and no fee if you are declined.

## Worked example: KES 200,000 over 24 months

This example shows a Kaskazi Personal Loan of KES 200,000 repaid over 24 months at 15.5% a year.

| | |
|---|---|
| Loan amount | KES 200,000 |
| Term | 24 months |
| Interest rate | 15.5% a year, reducing balance |
| Arrangement fee (2.5% of KES 200,000) | KES 5,000 |
| Credit life insurance (0.5% × 2 years) | KES 2,000 |
| **Amount paid into your account** | **KES 193,000** |
| **Monthly instalment** | **KES 9,745** |
| Total repaid (24 × KES 9,745) | KES 233,880 |
| Interest over the term | KES 33,880 |
| **Total cost of credit** (interest, fee and insurance) | **KES 40,880** |

The total cost of credit is what you pay back minus what you receive: KES 233,880 minus KES 193,000 gives KES 40,880.

On affordability, an instalment of KES 9,745 fits within 40% of any net monthly pay from KES 24,363 upwards. That is below the KES 25,000 minimum net monthly pay for every Kaskazi Personal Loan, so if you have no other loans and meet the minimum pay, this loan would fit.

## How the worked examples are calculated

Every Kaskazi Personal Loan instalment is worked out with the standard reducing-balance formula. With a loan amount P, a term of n months and a monthly rate r of 15.5% divided by 12:

instalment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r) to the power of −n)

The result is rounded to the nearest shilling. Because of that rounding, your last instalment may be slightly higher or lower than the others, so that the loan is cleared exactly; the totals in the examples assume equal instalments. Your offer shows your own figures before you accept, and the assistant can work out a quote for any amount and term within the limits.

## Kaskazi Personal Loan eligibility at a glance

You can apply for a Kaskazi Personal Loan if all of these are true:

- you are at least 21 years old when you apply
- you will be no older than 65 when the loan ends
- your net monthly pay is at least KES 25,000
- you have at least 3 months of salary history: either your salary has been paid into a Kaskazi account for at least 3 months, or you can give us 6 months of statements from the bank where your salary is paid
- all your monthly loan repayments, including this one, would be no more than 40% of your net monthly pay
- you have, or will open, a Kaskazi Everyday Account to receive the loan and pay the instalments

You will also need these documents: your 3 latest payslips with no gaps, your national ID card or passport, and your KRA PIN, plus the 6 months of statements if your salary is paid elsewhere. The guide "Applying for a loan: steps and documents" explains what makes each document acceptable.

Meeting all of these does not guarantee a loan. We also look at your record with a credit reference bureau and at how you have managed any accounts or loans with us.

## How long a Kaskazi Personal Loan decision takes

We aim to give you a decision within 2 working days of receiving all your documents. The clock starts when the last document arrives and passes our checks, not when you start the application. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

A person in our lending team makes every decision. The assistant can explain the loan, work out a quote, check whether a loan is likely to fit the affordability rule and prepare your application, but it cannot approve or decline a loan, and nothing it says is an offer.

If we approve your application, we send you an offer showing the amount, the term, the rate, the fees and insurance, the amount you will receive and your monthly instalment. When you accept the offer, the money is paid into your Kaskazi Everyday Account the same working day.

## How you repay a Kaskazi Personal Loan

Your Kaskazi Personal Loan instalments are collected each month from your Kaskazi Everyday Account. We set the instalment date to fall shortly after your usual pay day, so the money is there when the instalment is due. Your first instalment is due about one month after the loan is paid out.

Keep enough in your account on the due date. If an instalment is missed, a late payment fee of KES 500 is charged for each missed instalment, and interest continues on what you owe. If you think you will have trouble paying, talk to us before the instalment is due. We would much rather agree a plan with you early than chase a missed payment later.

If your salary is paid into your Kaskazi Everyday Account, its monthly fee of KES 250 is waived for that month.

## Repaying a Kaskazi Personal Loan early

You can repay part or all of your Kaskazi Personal Loan early, at any time.

- **During the first 12 months** after the loan is paid out, an early repayment fee of 2.0% of the amount you repay early applies.
- **After the first 12 months**, there is no early repayment fee.

Repaying early saves you the interest you would otherwise have paid on that amount. The arrangement fee and the credit life insurance are not refunded.

**Example within the first 12 months.** You borrowed KES 500,000 over 36 months, with an instalment of KES 17,455. You decide to clear the loan on the day your 6th instalment is paid. You still owe KES 431,852. The early repayment fee is 2.0% of that, KES 8,637, so you pay KES 440,489 in total. Had you kept paying, the remaining 30 instalments would have come to KES 523,650, so settling early saves you KES 83,161 even after the fee.

**Example after 12 months.** Same loan, but you clear it on the day your 14th instalment is paid. You owe KES 332,429 and there is no fee. The remaining 22 instalments would have come to KES 384,010, so you save KES 51,581.

**Paying off part of the loan.** If you pay a lump sum during the first 12 months, the fee is 2.0% of that lump sum: for example, KES 2,000 on a part payment of KES 100,000. Afterwards you can choose either to keep your instalment and finish sooner, or to keep your end date and pay a lower instalment.

To repay early, ask in the app, at a branch or through the assistant for a settlement figure. The figure includes interest up to the day you repay.
