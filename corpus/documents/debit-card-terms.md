# Kaskazi Debit Card: terms and conditions

These terms explain how the Kaskazi Debit Card works, what it costs, the limits that apply and what you and Kaskazi Bank each agree to do. They apply from 1 October 2026. Please read them alongside the terms for the account your card is linked to.

## About the Kaskazi Debit Card

The Kaskazi Debit Card lets you spend and withdraw the money in your Kaskazi account. Every payment and withdrawal comes straight out of the linked account, so you are using your own money, not borrowing.

The Kaskazi Debit Card comes with these accounts:

- the Kaskazi Everyday Account
- the Kaskazi Student Account

The Kaskazi Goal Saver does not come with a debit card. If you want to spend money held in a Goal Saver, move it to your Everyday or Student account first.

With the Kaskazi Debit Card you can:

- take out cash at ATMs in Kenya and abroad
- pay in shops by inserting the card and entering your PIN
- tap to pay for smaller amounts (contactless)
- pay online and in apps
- pay in other currencies when you travel or shop with businesses abroad

The Kaskazi Everyday Account has no overdraft. If there isn't enough money in your account to cover a payment or withdrawal, together with any fee for it, the Kaskazi Debit Card payment is declined. We don't let a card payment take your account below zero.

## Fees for the Kaskazi Debit Card

These are all the fees Kaskazi Bank charges for the Kaskazi Debit Card.

| What you do | Fee |
|---|---|
| Your first Kaskazi Debit Card | KES 0 (free) |
| Annual fee | KES 0 (none) |
| Replacement card (lost, stolen, damaged or cancelled) | KES 500 |
| Cash withdrawal at a Kaskazi ATM | KES 0 (free) |
| Cash withdrawal at another bank's ATM in Kenya | KES 35 for each withdrawal |
| Balance enquiry at another bank's ATM in Kenya | KES 25 for each enquiry |
| Cash withdrawal at an ATM outside Kenya | KES 300 for each withdrawal |
| Any payment or withdrawal in a currency other than Kenya shillings | 3% of the amount, as a currency conversion fee |

Kaskazi Bank does not charge a fee for paying in a shop or online in Kenya shillings.

Fees are taken from your linked account when the transaction happens. They appear as separate lines in your statement, so you can see exactly what you paid.

Another bank or ATM owner may charge its own fee for using its machine, separate from Kaskazi Bank's fee. The ATM must show you that fee on screen before you go ahead, so you can cancel if you don't want to pay it.

### Example: using other banks' ATMs in a month

Suppose that in one month you take cash out four times at other banks' ATMs in Kenya and check your balance twice at those ATMs.

- Withdrawals: 4 × KES 35 = KES 140
- Balance enquiries: 2 × KES 25 = KES 50
- Total Kaskazi fees: KES 190

If you had used a Kaskazi ATM for the same six transactions, you would have paid nothing. You can also check your balance free in the Kaskazi app.

## Daily limits on the Kaskazi Debit Card

The Kaskazi Debit Card has two daily limits.

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash from ATMs | up to KES 100,000 a day |
| Card payments, in shops and online together | up to KES 300,000 a day |

The ATM limit covers cash from all ATMs, whether they belong to Kaskazi Bank or another bank, in Kenya or abroad. The card payment limit covers everything you pay by card, in shops and online, added together. Contactless payments count towards the card payment limit.

For payments and withdrawals in another currency, the limit is checked against the amount in Kenya shillings after conversion.

### Example: how much you can still use today

You take out KES 60,000 at ATMs in the morning. For the rest of that day you can take out up to KES 40,000 more in cash.

You spend KES 250,000 on furniture in a shop. For the rest of that day you can pay up to KES 50,000 more by card, whether in shops or online.

These two limits are separate. Spending on the card does not reduce how much cash you can take out, and taking out cash does not reduce how much you can spend.

### Kaskazi Debit Card on a Kaskazi Student Account

If your Kaskazi Debit Card is linked to a Kaskazi Student Account, the account has its own daily withdrawal limit of KES 20,000. That lower limit applies to cash you take out with the card, so on a Student Account you can take out up to KES 20,000 in cash a day, not KES 100,000.

When a Kaskazi Student Account converts to a Kaskazi Everyday Account, the standard Kaskazi Debit Card ATM limit of KES 100,000 a day applies from then on.

### Paying more than the Kaskazi Debit Card limits allow

You can't raise the Kaskazi Debit Card's daily limits above these amounts. For a large payment, such as rent, school fees or a car, use a transfer from the app instead. Transfers have their own, higher daily limits, explained in our payments documents.

Some ATMs, especially other banks' machines, give out less cash in one withdrawal than our daily limit allows. If that happens, you can make more than one withdrawal, but each one at another bank's ATM in Kenya costs KES 35.

## Contactless payments with the Kaskazi Debit Card

You can tap your Kaskazi Debit Card on a contactless reader to pay up to KES 5,000 in a single payment without entering your PIN.

- A payment of KES 5,000 or less: tap to pay.
- A payment of more than KES 5,000: insert the card and enter your PIN.

For example, a KES 4,500 supermarket shop can be paid by tapping. A KES 6,000 shop cannot. The till will ask you to insert the card and enter your PIN instead.

Now and then, the card reader will ask for your PIN even for a small contactless payment. This is a security check that confirms the card is still in your hands, usually after several contactless payments in a row. Insert the card, enter your PIN, and tapping will work again afterwards.

Contactless payments count towards the Kaskazi Debit Card's card payment limit of KES 300,000 a day.

Because contactless payments don't need a PIN, a lost card can be used by someone else for small amounts. If you can't find your Kaskazi Debit Card, block it straight away in the app or through the assistant. You can unblock it later if it turns up.

## Your Kaskazi Debit Card PIN

Your PIN is the secret number that proves a Kaskazi Debit Card payment or withdrawal is really yours. These sections explain how to choose it, what happens if you get it wrong, and how to keep it safe.

### Choosing and changing your Kaskazi Debit Card PIN

You choose your Kaskazi Debit Card PIN yourself when you collect the card at the branch, on a secure keypad that only you can see. No member of staff will ever see or ask for it.

You can change your PIN at any time at any Kaskazi ATM or in the Kaskazi app.

Choose a PIN that other people can't guess. Avoid your birth year, the last digits of your phone number or ID number, simple sequences and repeated digits.

### Three wrong PIN attempts lock the Kaskazi Debit Card

If you enter the wrong PIN 3 times in a row, your Kaskazi Debit Card is locked. While it is locked you can't use the card for cash withdrawals or payments.

The count of wrong attempts is shared across every ATM and card machine. Two wrong attempts at an ATM and one wrong attempt in a shop make 3, and the card locks. Entering the correct PIN before you reach 3 sets the count back to zero.

To unlock the Kaskazi Debit Card, reset your PIN:

- in the Kaskazi app, confirming with a one-time code sent to your registered phone number (the code works for 5 minutes), or
- at any Kaskazi branch, with your national ID card or passport.

A PIN lock is not the same as blocking your card. A PIN lock happens automatically after wrong attempts and is cleared by resetting your PIN. A block is something you choose to do, explained in our document on blocking, unblocking and replacing a card.

### If an ATM keeps your Kaskazi Debit Card

Some ATMs, particularly those of other banks, keep a card after too many wrong PIN attempts or if you don't take it back quickly. If a machine keeps your Kaskazi Debit Card, treat the card as lost: block it as lost in the app or through the assistant and order a replacement. The replacement fee is KES 500, and the new card is ready to collect within 5 working days at the branch you choose.

### Keeping your Kaskazi Debit Card PIN safe

- Never write your PIN on the card or keep it with the card.
- Never tell anyone your PIN, including family, friends and anyone who says they work for Kaskazi Bank.
- Cover the keypad with your other hand when you type your PIN.
- Don't let anyone stand close behind you at an ATM. If someone offers to "help" you, cancel and walk away.

If you think someone knows your PIN, change it straight away.

## Paying online with the Kaskazi Debit Card

You can use the Kaskazi Debit Card to pay on websites and in apps. You will usually be asked for the card number, the expiry date and the security code on the back of the card. These details are for entering on a checkout page you opened yourself. They are never for reading out to someone who calls or messages you.

For many online payments, we send a one-time code to your registered phone number to confirm the payment. The message tells you the business's name and the amount. Check both before you enter the code. A one-time code works for 5 minutes. If it runs out, ask for a new one on the checkout page.

If you receive a one-time code for a payment you are not making, do not enter it or share it with anyone. Someone may be trying to use your card details. Block your card in the app or through the assistant and tell us.

Online payments count towards the Kaskazi Debit Card's card payment limit of KES 300,000 a day, together with payments in shops.

### Online payments in other currencies

What matters is the currency the payment is processed in, not where you are. If you buy from a website or app that charges in US dollars, euros or any other currency, the 3% currency conversion fee applies, even though you are at home in Kenya.

### Saved card details and subscriptions

If you save your Kaskazi Debit Card on a website or use it for a subscription, the business can take payments without asking you each time. Keep a list of where your card is saved. If your card is replaced, you will need to give each of these businesses your new card details. While your card is blocked, these regular payments are declined.

## Using the Kaskazi Debit Card abroad

You can use the Kaskazi Debit Card in other countries wherever the card network shown on your card is accepted.

You don't need to tell us before you travel. Make sure your registered phone number works while you are away, so that you can receive one-time codes for online payments, PIN resets and unblocking.

### Fees for using the Kaskazi Debit Card abroad

- Every payment or withdrawal in a foreign currency has a currency conversion fee of 3% of the amount.
- Every cash withdrawal at an ATM outside Kenya also costs KES 300.
- The ATM owner abroad may charge its own fee as well, shown on screen before you go ahead.

The foreign amount is converted to Kenya shillings at the card network's exchange rate on the day the payment is processed. The 3% conversion fee is then added.

### Example: taking cash out abroad

You take out cash at an ATM abroad. At the card network's rate on the day, the amount comes to KES 20,000.

- Cash: KES 20,000
- Currency conversion fee: 3% of KES 20,000 = KES 600
- ATM abroad fee: KES 300
- Total taken from your account: KES 20,900, plus any fee charged by the ATM owner

### Example: paying in a shop abroad

You pay for a meal abroad. At the card network's rate, the bill comes to KES 8,000.

- Payment: KES 8,000
- Currency conversion fee: 3% of KES 8,000 = KES 240
- Total taken from your account: KES 8,240

### When a shop or ATM abroad offers to charge you in Kenya shillings

Some shops and ATMs abroad offer to charge you in Kenya shillings instead of the local currency. If you choose Kenya shillings, the payment reaches us already in shillings, so Kaskazi Bank's 3% conversion fee does not apply. But the shop or ATM uses its own exchange rate, which is often less favourable than the card network's rate plus our fee. Compare the two amounts on screen before you choose.

### Refunds from businesses abroad

When a business abroad refunds you, the refund is converted to Kenya shillings at the rate on the day the refund is processed. Exchange rates change, so the amount you get back may be slightly more or less than you paid. We also refund the 3% conversion fee on the amount refunded.

## Validity and renewal of the Kaskazi Debit Card

Each Kaskazi Debit Card is valid for 4 years. The expiry month and year are printed on the card. The card works until the last day of that month.

Before your card expires, we prepare a renewed Kaskazi Debit Card and send you a message when it is ready to collect at your branch. You choose a new PIN when you collect it. Once you start using the renewed card, cut the old card through the chip and the card number and throw it away.

If you have saved your card on websites or use it for subscriptions, update the expiry date and security code with each business once you have your renewed card.

### Kaskazi Debit Card on a dormant account

If you don't start any transaction on your account for 12 months, the account becomes dormant, and the Kaskazi Debit Card is declined until you reactivate it. You can reactivate the account at any branch with your ID, or in the app with a selfie check. There is no fee for reactivation.

## Lost, stolen or damaged Kaskazi Debit Card

If your Kaskazi Debit Card is lost or stolen, block it as lost or stolen in the Kaskazi app or through the assistant straight away. You can do this at any time of day or night. Card blocks never wait for staff, because you do them yourself.

A Kaskazi Debit Card blocked as lost or stolen is cancelled for good and can't be unblocked. Order a replacement in the app, through the assistant or at any branch.

- Replacement fee: KES 500, taken from your linked account
- Ready to collect within 5 working days
- Collected at the branch you choose

If you're not sure whether the card is lost, for example you think it may be at home, use a temporary block instead. You can unblock it yourself with a one-time code sent to your registered phone number.

If your Kaskazi Debit Card is damaged or worn out and no longer works in machines, order a replacement in the same way. The fee is KES 500.

Our document on blocking, unblocking and replacing a card explains each step in more detail.

## What Kaskazi Bank will never ask you for

Kaskazi Bank will never ask you, by phone, message, email, social media or in person, for:

- your Kaskazi Debit Card PIN
- your full card number
- the security code on the back of your card
- a one-time code sent to your phone
- your password for the Kaskazi app or online banking

We will also never:

- ask you to move money to a "safe account" to protect it
- send anyone to your home or office to collect your card
- ask you to install an app that lets someone else control your phone or computer

If anyone asks for any of these, it is a scam, even if they know your name, your account number or details of recent payments, and even if the caller ID or message appears to come from Kaskazi Bank. End the conversation, block your card in the app or through the assistant if you have shared anything, and tell us. Our guide to fraud and scams has more on spotting and reporting scams.

## If something goes wrong with a Kaskazi Debit Card payment

If you see a payment you don't recognise, a payment taken twice, or an ATM withdrawal where the cash didn't come out, you can dispute it.

- Report it within 60 days of the transaction date.
- We acknowledge your dispute within 24 hours.
- For disputes on amounts up to KES 20,000, we give you a provisional credit within 10 working days while we investigate.
- We aim to finish the investigation within 45 days.

### Your liability for fraud on the Kaskazi Debit Card

If someone else uses your Kaskazi Debit Card or card details without your permission:

- Before you report it, the most you lose is KES 5,000, unless you were careless with your PIN or a one-time code.
- After you report it, you lose nothing (KES 0).

So the sooner you block the card and tell us, the better. Our document on disputing a card payment explains what counts as carelessness and gives worked examples.

## Your responsibilities as a Kaskazi Debit Card holder

By using the Kaskazi Debit Card, you agree to:

- keep the card safe and keep your PIN secret
- use the card only yourself; the card is in your name and nobody else may use it, even with your permission
- check your transactions regularly; statements in the app are free

- update your personal details when we ask; we review identity details every 3 years
- block the card as soon as you think it is lost, stolen or being misused

If you let someone else use your card or PIN, payments they make are treated as payments you authorised.

## Cancelling the Kaskazi Debit Card

You can cancel your Kaskazi Debit Card at any time in the app, through the assistant or at a branch. Cancelling the card does not close your account.

When a card is cancelled:

- payments already approved before you cancelled will still go through
- subscriptions and other regular payments on the card are declined, so arrange another way to pay them
- cut the card through the chip and the card number

If you close your linked account, the Kaskazi Debit Card is cancelled at the same time.

We may stop or cancel your Kaskazi Debit Card if we reasonably suspect fraud or misuse, if you break these terms, or if the law requires it. We will tell you why, and what you can do, unless the law prevents us from telling you.
