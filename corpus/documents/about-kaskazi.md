# About Kaskazi Bank

## Who Kaskazi Bank is

Kaskazi Bank is an invented Kenyan retail bank. It exists only for a public demonstration of an AI banking assistant. It has no real customers, money or staff, and you cannot open a real account with it.

Every product, fee, rate, limit and rule in Kaskazi Bank's documents is sample data written for the demonstration. None of it is an offer, and none is taken from a real bank.

In the demonstration, the bank's full name is Kaskazi Bank Limited and its head office is in Mombasa. The name comes from the kaskazi, the north-east monsoon wind on the Kenyan coast.

Because the bank is invented, it has no licence, partners or history. In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

## What Kaskazi Bank offers

Kaskazi Bank's sample products are for individuals and businesses:

- **Everyday banking:** the Kaskazi Everyday Account, the Kaskazi Student Account and the Kaskazi Business Account. The Everyday and Student accounts come with a Kaskazi Debit Card.
- **Saving:** the Kaskazi Goal Saver and the Kaskazi Fixed Deposit.
- **Foreign currency:** the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account, in USD, GBP or EUR.
- **Borrowing:** the Kaskazi Personal Loan, the Kaskazi Salary Advance and the Kaskazi Credit Card.
- **Payments and digital banking:** transfers, bill payments, mobile money wallet transfers, agent banking, and the app and web banking.

Each product has its own terms and conditions document. The Kaskazi Base Rate is 11.5% a year.

## Kaskazi Bank branch opening hours

Every Kaskazi Bank branch keeps the same hours:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 8:30 am to 4:30 pm |
| Saturday | 9:00 am to 12:00 noon |
| Sundays and public holidays | Closed |

Kaskazi Bank aims to serve you within 15 minutes of your arrival at a branch. When branches are closed, many things can be done in the app instead.

## Your Kaskazi Bank account number and branch code

Every Kaskazi Bank account number has 13 digits. It starts with "0", followed by the 3-digit code of the branch where the account was opened, then an 8-digit serial number, then a single check digit.

For example, an account opened at Kisumu starts "0301". The check digit lets the bank's systems catch most mistyped account numbers before a payment is sent.

## Kaskazi Bank contact centre hours

The Kaskazi Bank contact centre is open 24 hours a day, every day, including Sundays and public holidays. Kaskazi Bank aims to answer calls within 60 seconds.

The contact centre's phone number is not available in this demonstration.

## Getting help from the Kaskazi assistant

The Kaskazi assistant answers questions from Kaskazi Bank's own documents and tells you which document each answer comes from. It can also help with some tasks, such as blocking your card if you have misplaced it.

If the assistant cannot answer, or you would rather talk to a person, ask it to hand you over to the Kaskazi Bank team.

## When the Kaskazi assistant hands you to a person

People on the Kaskazi Bank team answer handed-over conversations during staffed hours: 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, every day.

During staffed hours, Kaskazi Bank aims to have a person pick up your conversation within 5 minutes of the handover.

Outside staffed hours, the conversation waits in the queue and a person replies when the team is next in. You don't need to start again or repeat yourself; the person sees the whole conversation.

Card blocks never wait for the team. You can block your card yourself at any time, in the app or through the assistant, whether or not anyone is staffing the queue.

## Making a complaint to Kaskazi Bank

If something has gone wrong, tell the assistant, a branch or the contact centre. Kaskazi Bank acknowledges complaints within 24 hours, gives you a reference that starts "KZC-" followed by 6 digits, and aims to resolve them within 7 working days, or within 30 days if they are complex.

If you are not happy with the answer, you can ask for your complaint to go to the Customer Experience team and then to the Head of Customer Experience. In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.
