# Blocking, unblocking and replacing a card

This guide explains how to block a Kaskazi card, how to unblock it, and how to get a replacement. It applies to both the Kaskazi Debit Card and the Kaskazi Credit Card.

## Two kinds of card block

There are two ways to block a Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card. Choosing the right one matters, because one can be undone and the other can't.

| | Temporary block | Blocked as lost or stolen |
|---|---|---|
| When to use it | You've misplaced the card, or you want to pause it | You know the card is lost or has been stolen, or someone has your card details |
| Can you undo it? | Yes, yourself, with a one-time code | No. The card is cancelled for good |
| Do you need a new card? | No | Yes. Order a replacement (KES 500) |
| Where to do it | In the Kaskazi app or through the assistant | In the Kaskazi app or through the assistant |

If you're not sure, use a temporary block first. It protects you straight away, and you can block the card as lost or stolen later if it doesn't turn up.

## Blocking your Kaskazi card temporarily

You can block your Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card yourself, at any time of day or night, in the Kaskazi app or by asking the assistant.

In the app, open the card and choose to block it. With the assistant, just say you want to block your card, and say which one if you have more than one. The block takes effect at once.

You don't need to wait for a member of staff. Even outside staffed hours, when other conversations wait in a queue for a person, card blocks never wait, because you do them yourself.

### What a temporary block stops

While a Kaskazi card is temporarily blocked:

- ATM withdrawals are declined
- payments in shops, including contactless payments, are declined
- online and in-app payments are declined
- subscriptions and other regular card payments you set up before the block are declined

### What a temporary block doesn't stop

A temporary block on a Kaskazi card doesn't affect the rest of your banking:

- your account stays open and your money stays where it is
- you can still use the Kaskazi app, make transfers and pay bills from your account
- refunds from businesses can still reach your card
- payments that were already approved before you blocked the card can still go through, because the business already has permission for them

If a payment was approved before the block and you didn't make it, dispute it. Our document on disputing a card payment explains how.

## Unblocking your Kaskazi card

To unblock a Kaskazi card you blocked temporarily, choose to unblock it in the app or ask the assistant. We then send a one-time code to your registered phone number. Enter the code to confirm, and the card works again straight away.

- The one-time code works for 5 minutes. If it runs out, ask for a new one.
- The code only goes to the phone number registered on your account. We can't send it anywhere else.
- Never share the code with anyone. A real Kaskazi member of staff will never ask you for it.

If you have changed your phone number and haven't told us, update your registered phone number at a branch first, with your national ID card or passport. You will then be able to receive the code.

A card blocked as lost or stolen can't be unblocked, with or without a code. See the section on blocking a card as lost or stolen.

### Example: a card you thought you'd lost

You can't find your Kaskazi Debit Card after a trip to the market, so you put a temporary block on it in the app. That evening you find it in your other bag. You choose to unblock it in the app, receive a one-time code on your registered phone, enter it within 5 minutes, and the card works again. There is no fee for blocking or unblocking.

## Blocking a Kaskazi card as lost or stolen

If you know your Kaskazi card is lost or has been stolen, or you think someone has copied your card details, block it as lost or stolen in the app or through the assistant.

A card blocked as lost or stolen is cancelled for good and can't be unblocked. Any later attempt to use it is declined. You'll need to order a replacement card.

You can do this even if the card is already temporarily blocked. If a temporary block turns into a confirmed loss, change it to lost or stolen and order your replacement.

If your card was stolen, you may also want to report the theft to the police. Keep any reference they give you, in case you need to dispute payments.

### Why reporting quickly matters

If someone else uses your Kaskazi card before you report it, the most you lose is KES 5,000, unless you were careless with your PIN or a one-time code. After you report it, you lose nothing (KES 0). A block in the app or through the assistant counts as reporting it, and it takes seconds.

## Ordering a replacement Kaskazi card

You can order a replacement Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card in the app, through the assistant or at any branch.

- Replacement fee: KES 500. For a debit card it is taken from your linked account. For a credit card it is added to your card balance.
- Ready within 5 working days.
- Collected at the branch you choose.

When you order, choose the branch that is easiest for you. You don't have to use the branch where you opened your account. We send you a message when the card is ready.

### Collecting your replacement Kaskazi card

Bring your national ID card or passport. You choose a new PIN at the branch on a secure keypad that only you can see.

Your replacement card has a new card number, expiry date and security code. Your account and your balance don't change.

### When you need a replacement Kaskazi card

Order a replacement, at the same fee of KES 500, if your card:

- has been blocked as lost or stolen
- was kept by an ATM
- is damaged, bent or worn and no longer works in machines

A card that is simply reaching its expiry date is renewed instead. Our debit card and credit card terms explain renewals.

## Subscriptions and regular payments while your Kaskazi card is blocked

Payments you set up before the block, such as subscriptions, are declined while your Kaskazi card is blocked. This includes streaming and music services, app subscriptions, gym memberships and any business that has saved your card to charge you regularly.

For example, if your monthly video streaming subscription is due on a day your card is temporarily blocked, the payment is declined. The business may try again later or pause your service.

- After a temporary block, once you unblock the card, businesses can charge it again. Some will retry a declined payment by themselves.
- After a lost or stolen block, the old card number no longer works at all. Give each business your new card details once you have your replacement.

If a bill is important, such as insurance or school fees, pay it another way while your card is blocked, for example by a transfer from the app.

## If you find your Kaskazi card again

What to do depends on how you blocked it.

- **Temporary block:** unblock it in the app or through the assistant with a one-time code, and carry on using it.
- **Blocked as lost or stolen:** the card is cancelled for good and won't work again, even if it looks fine. Don't try to use it. Cut it through the chip and the card number, and throw it away. If you have already ordered a replacement, collect it from the branch you chose.

You can't cancel a replacement or get the fee back because you found the old card. That is why, if you're not sure a card is gone, a temporary block is the better first step.

## If an ATM keeps your Kaskazi card

If an ATM keeps your Kaskazi card, block it as lost or stolen straight away in the app or through the assistant, and order a replacement. Don't accept help from strangers standing near the machine, and don't enter your PIN again for anyone. Someone may have tampered with the machine.

If the ATM kept your card during a withdrawal and you didn't get your cash, dispute the withdrawal as well.

## If you can't use the Kaskazi app

If your phone has been lost or stolen along with your card, you can still block the card:

- through the assistant, from another device
- through our contact centre, open 24 hours a day, every day (number not available in this demonstration)
- at any branch, during opening hours

Tell us about the phone as well. If you think someone has your phone and your SIM card, ask your mobile network to block the SIM straight away, so they can't receive your one-time codes. Our guide to fraud and scams explains what to do after a phone theft.

## Examples of blocking and replacing a Kaskazi card

### A card stolen on the way to work

Your bag is stolen on a matatu, with your Kaskazi Debit Card inside. At the next stop you open the Kaskazi app on your phone, block the card as lost or stolen, and order a replacement to collect at the Nairobi City Centre branch. KES 500 is taken from your account. Within 5 working days you get a message that the card is ready, and you collect it with your ID.

### A credit card you think you left at a restaurant

You think you left your Kaskazi Credit Card at a restaurant last night. You ask the assistant to block it temporarily while you call the restaurant. They have it. You collect it, unblock it in the app with a one-time code, and nothing else changes. If they hadn't found it, you would have changed the block to lost or stolen and ordered a replacement.

### A card number used online by someone else

Your Kaskazi Debit Card is still in your wallet, but you see an online payment you didn't make. Someone has your card details, so block the card as lost or stolen, even though you still have it, and order a replacement. Then dispute the payment within 60 days of the transaction date.
