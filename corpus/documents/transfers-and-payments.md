# Transfers and payments: limits, fees and timings

## About this guide to transfers and payments

This guide explains every way to move money out of a Kaskazi Bank account: to your own accounts, to other Kaskazi customers, to other banks in Kenya, to mobile money wallets, to billers and schools, by standing order and abroad. For each one it gives the fee, the limits and when the money arrives. It also explains the daily digital limit, the lower limit for new recipients, and what to do if you send money to the wrong person.

Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration, and every figure here is a sample, but the bank's systems use exactly these figures. The fees are the same as in the "Fees and charges (tariff guide)", and every fee includes any government levy.

In this guide, a **working day** is Monday to Friday, except public holidays. Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays are not working days for payments, even though some branches open on Saturday mornings.

Two limits sit across all digital payments: the **daily digital limit** of KES 500,000, and the **new-recipient limit** of KES 50,000 in the first 24 hours after you add someone. Both are explained below.

## Transfers between your own accounts and to other Kaskazi accounts

Moving money between your own Kaskazi accounts, or to another person's or business's Kaskazi account, is free (KES 0) and arrives straight away, at any time of day, every day.

- **Your own accounts.** You can move money between your Everyday, Goal Saver, Student, Business and other Kaskazi accounts in the app, online banking, USSD, through the assistant or at a branch.
- **Goal Saver withdrawals.** The transfer itself is free, but moving money out of a Goal Saver counts as a withdrawal. You have 1 free withdrawal a month, and each extra one costs KES 150.
- **Business Account.** Transfers from a Business Account count towards its 30 free transactions a month; after that, each transaction costs KES 30.
- **Other Kaskazi customers.** You need their 13-digit Kaskazi account number. Before you confirm, the app shows the account holder's name so you can check it is the right person.
- **Limits.** Payments to other Kaskazi customers count towards your daily digital limit of KES 500,000, and the new-recipient limit applies the first time you pay someone.

## Instant transfers to another bank

An instant transfer sends money to an account at another bank in Kenya, usually within moments, on any day, including weekends and public holidays.

| Amount sent | Fee |
|---|---|
| KES 1 to KES 10,000 | KES 30 |
| KES 10,001 to KES 100,000 | KES 60 |
| KES 100,001 to KES 999,999 | KES 120 |

The most you can send in a single instant transfer is KES 999,999.

**Examples.** Sending KES 10,000 costs KES 30. Sending KES 10,001 costs KES 60. Sending KES 45,000 costs KES 60. Sending KES 250,000 costs KES 120.

**Things to know about instant transfers:**

- **They cannot be recalled by us once sent.** Because the money moves at once, check the account number and the name shown before you confirm.
- **Digital limits apply.** In the app, online banking and the assistant, your daily digital limit of KES 500,000 also applies. To send a single instant transfer above KES 500,000, up to the KES 999,999 maximum, visit a branch.
- **If the receiving bank cannot accept the payment,** for example because the account is closed, the money comes back to your Kaskazi account. Contact us if it has not come back.

## Next-day transfers (EFT) and the 3:00 pm cut-off

A next-day transfer (EFT) sends money to another bank in Kenya for a flat fee of KES 100, whatever the amount. It is slower than an instant transfer but costs less for larger amounts.

**The cut-off is 3:00 pm on a working day.** An EFT you send before 3:00 pm on a working day goes that day and normally reaches the other bank the next working day. Instructions received after 3:00 pm, or on a weekend or public holiday, go on the next working day.

**Timing examples:**

| You send the EFT | It goes | It normally arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Monday at 11:00 am | Monday | Tuesday |
| Monday at 4:00 pm | Tuesday | Wednesday |
| Friday at 2:30 pm | Friday | Monday |
| Friday at 5:00 pm, or on Saturday | Monday | Tuesday |

If a public holiday falls in between, add a working day.

**When an EFT is the better choice.** For amounts up to KES 100,000, an instant transfer (KES 30 or KES 60) is both faster and cheaper than an EFT (KES 100). From KES 100,001 upwards, an instant transfer costs KES 120, so an EFT saves KES 20 if you do not need the money to arrive the same day.

**Cancelling an EFT.** If you change your mind, contact us straight away. We can only stop an EFT that has not yet been sent to the other bank.

## Same-day high-value transfers (RTGS)

A same-day high-value transfer (RTGS) is for large, urgent payments to another bank in Kenya, such as buying land, a vehicle or paying a large supplier.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Fee | KES 500 |
| Minimum amount | KES 1,000,000 |
| Cut-off | 2:00 pm on a working day |
| Arrival | The same working day, if sent before the cut-off |

**Making an RTGS payment.** Because the RTGS minimum of KES 1,000,000 is above the daily digital limit of KES 500,000, RTGS payments are made at a branch. Branches are open 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday. Come before 2:00 pm on a working day so the payment can go the same day; instructions given after the cut-off go on the next working day.

**What to bring.** Your national ID card or passport, and the recipient's full name, bank, branch and account number. For a property or vehicle purchase, check the recipient's details directly with them, not from an email or message alone.

**Choosing between RTGS and EFT for large amounts.** An EFT costs KES 100 and arrives the next working day. An RTGS costs KES 500 and arrives the same working day. Choose RTGS when the money must arrive today.

## Mobile money transfers

You can send money from your Kaskazi account to any mobile money wallet in Kenya, including your own. The fee depends on how much you send.

| Amount sent | Fee |
|---|---|
| KES 1 to KES 1,000 | KES 15 |
| KES 1,001 to KES 10,000 | KES 35 |
| KES 10,001 to KES 50,000 | KES 55 |
| KES 50,001 to KES 150,000 | KES 85 |

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Maximum per transfer | KES 150,000 |
| Daily limit for mobile money transfers | KES 300,000 |

**Examples.**

- Sending KES 500 costs KES 15. Sending KES 1,001 costs KES 35. Sending KES 25,000 costs KES 55.
- Sending KES 150,000 costs KES 85, the highest mobile money fee.
- To send KES 200,000 in a day, make two transfers, for example KES 150,000 and KES 50,000: the fees are KES 85 and KES 55, so KES 140.
- The most you can send to mobile money in a day is KES 300,000, for example two transfers of KES 150,000 for KES 170 in fees.

**Things to know about mobile money transfers:**

- **Check the name.** Before you confirm, the app shows the name registered to the wallet. If it is not the person you expect, stop.
- **The mobile money daily limit sits inside the daily digital limit.** Mobile money transfers count towards both the KES 300,000 mobile money limit and the KES 500,000 daily digital limit.
- **The recipient's charges.** The fee above is everything Kaskazi charges you. The mobile money provider may charge the recipient to withdraw cash from their wallet, under its own terms.

## Bill payments and school fees

You can pay bills and school fees from your Kaskazi account in the app, online banking, USSD, through the assistant or at a branch.

| Payment | Fee |
|---|---|
| Bill payment | KES 30 each |
| School fees payment | KES 0 |

**Bill payments.** Choose the biller, enter your customer or account number with the biller, and confirm. Each bill payment costs KES 30. When the biller credits your account depends on the biller, so pay a little before the due date.

**School fees.** Paying school fees through the school fees option is free. Enter the school, the student's name and admission number, and the term. Paying a school by an ordinary transfer to another bank is charged as that transfer (for example, KES 60 for an instant transfer of KES 45,000), so use the school fees option where your school is listed.

**Keep your confirmation.** Every bill and school fees payment has a confirmation with a reference in the app. Keep it until the biller or school confirms they have received the money.

## Standing orders

A standing order pays a fixed amount from your Kaskazi account on dates you choose, for example rent on the 1st of each month or a weekly transfer to a family member.

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Setting up a standing order | KES 0 |
| Each payment the standing order makes | KES 50 |

**Example.** A monthly rent standing order costs nothing to set up and KES 50 each month when it pays. Over a year of monthly payments, that is 12 payments at KES 50.

**Things to know about standing orders:**

- **Set up, change or cancel** a standing order in the app, online banking, through the assistant or at a branch. Changes must be made before the payment date to take effect for that payment.
- **Have the money ready.** Keep enough in the account the day before the payment date. If there is not enough money, the payment is not made and we let you know.
- **Where it can pay.** A standing order can pay your own accounts, other Kaskazi accounts or accounts at other banks. Any fee for the transfer type it uses applies as well as the KES 50 standing order fee.

## The daily digital limit

The daily digital limit is KES 500,000. It is the most you can pay out in one day through the Kaskazi app, online banking, USSD and the assistant, added together.

**What counts towards it.** Payments to other people and businesses, including other Kaskazi accounts, other banks, mobile money wallets, bills, school fees and international payments. Transfers between your own Kaskazi accounts do not leave the bank and do not count.

**How it works with other limits.**

- Mobile money transfers also have their own daily limit of KES 300,000, inside the KES 500,000.
- An instant transfer can be up to KES 999,999, but in digital channels the KES 500,000 daily limit caps it.
- An RTGS payment starts at KES 1,000,000, so it is always made at a branch.

**Why we have it.** If someone ever got into your app, the daily limit caps how much they could take in a day while you and we act.

**Paying more than the limit.** For larger payments, visit a branch with your ID. Branch payments are not part of the daily digital limit.

**Example.** On one day you pay KES 150,000 to a supplier by instant transfer, send KES 200,000 to mobile money and pay KES 30,000 in school fees. That is KES 380,000, so you can still pay out KES 120,000 digitally that day.

## Limit for new recipients in the first 24 hours

When you pay someone for the first time from the app, online banking, USSD or the assistant, you can send them up to KES 50,000 in total during the first 24 hours after you add them. After 24 hours, your normal limits apply.

**Why this limit exists.** Most scams and account takeovers need money to go quickly to a new account or wallet. A fraudster who tricks you, or who gets into your app, will almost always add a new recipient and try to empty the account at once. Capping new recipients at KES 50,000 for 24 hours limits the harm and gives you time to notice and tell us. It also gives you time to think if someone is pressuring you to pay quickly.

**Who counts as a new recipient.** Any account, at Kaskazi or another bank, or mobile money wallet that you have not paid before from your Kaskazi profile.

**Example.** You add your new landlord on Tuesday at 9:00 am and pay them KES 30,000. Until 9:00 am on Wednesday you can send them only KES 20,000 more. From Wednesday at 9:00 am, the rest of your daily digital limit is available for them.

**If you need to pay more straight away,** for example a deposit on a house, visit a branch with your ID.

## Sending money abroad and receiving it

Kaskazi Bank can send money to, and receive money from, bank accounts outside Kenya.

| Payment | Fee |
|---|---|
| Sending money abroad | KES 2,500, plus any charges by the banks in between |
| Receiving money from abroad | KES 0 |

**Sending money abroad.** You can send from a Kenya shilling account or from a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account (USD, GBP or EUR). You need the recipient's full name and address as their bank holds it, their account number, their bank's name and country, and the bank's international identifier code. We may ask what the payment is for and for supporting documents, such as a school invoice.

**Charges by banks in between.** International payments often pass through other banks before arriving. Those banks may take their own charges, which Kaskazi does not set. They may be taken from the amount on the way, so your recipient can receive less than you sent.

**Exchange rate.** If the payment involves changing currency, the exchange rate is shown before you confirm.

**Timing.** International payments take longer than payments within Kenya. The time depends on the countries and banks involved.

**Receiving money from abroad.** Kaskazi charges nothing (KES 0) to receive an international payment. Give the sender your full name, your 13-digit account number and your branch. The sender's bank, or banks on the way, may take their own charges before the money reaches us.

## If you sent money to the wrong person

If you sent money to the wrong account or wallet, act at once. The sooner you tell us, the better the chance of getting it back.

**Step 1: tell us straight away.** Use the assistant (ask for a person if you prefer), the app or a branch. Have the date, amount, the account or wallet number you paid and the transaction reference. The assistant hands conversations about wrong payments and possible fraud to a person; during staffed hours, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm every day, we aim to reply within 5 minutes.

**Step 2: we log it and give you a reference.** You get a reference in the form KZC- followed by 6 digits. We acknowledge it within 24 hours and aim to resolve it within 7 working days, or within 30 days if it is complex.

**Step 3: we try to recover the money.**

- **Another Kaskazi account.** We contact the account holder and ask them to agree to return the money.
- **Another bank.** We ask the receiving bank to contact its customer and return the money.
- **A mobile money wallet.** We ask the mobile money provider to contact the wallet holder and return the money.

**What we cannot do.** We cannot take money out of another person's account without their agreement or a lawful order. If the recipient refuses or has already spent the money, recovery may not be possible, and we will explain your options.

**If you were tricked into paying,** tell us it was a scam. We will treat it as fraud, help protect your account and give you a reference you can use if you report it to the police.

**Before you pay next time:** check the name the app shows before you confirm, and for a new recipient, consider sending a small amount first.

## Keeping your payments safe

These habits protect your money when you send transfers and payments.

- **Kaskazi will never ask for your PIN, password or one-time code.** No member of staff, agent or the assistant will ask for them. Anyone who does is not genuine.
- **One-time codes** are valid for 5 minutes. Only enter them in the Kaskazi app or online banking, never into a link or for a caller.
- **Check the name** shown before you confirm any payment.
- **Be wary of urgency.** A message saying you must pay now, or a "Kaskazi" caller asking you to move money to a "safe account", is a scam.
- **Changed payment details.** If a supplier, landlord or school says their bank details have changed, confirm it with them in person or on a number you already know before you pay.
- **Report anything odd at once** through the assistant, the app or a branch. You can block your card yourself at any time in the app or through the assistant.
