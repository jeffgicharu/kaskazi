# Banking at a Kaskazi agent

## About Kaskazi agents

A Kaskazi agent is a shop or business, such as a pharmacy, a hardware store or a general shop, that Kaskazi Bank has appointed to offer basic banking on its behalf. Agents let you deposit and withdraw cash close to home, often outside branch hours, without a trip to town.

Agents act for Kaskazi Bank, so a deposit made at an agent is the same as a deposit at a branch: it is in your account as soon as you get the confirmation. Each agent sets its own opening hours, usually the hours of the shop.

Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration, and every fee and limit here is a sample figure, but the bank's systems use exactly these figures. Every fee includes any government levy.

## What you can do at a Kaskazi agent

At a Kaskazi agent you can:

- **Deposit cash** into your own Kaskazi account, or into someone else's, free of charge.
- **Withdraw cash** from your Kaskazi account, up to KES 50,000 at a time.
- **Check your balance** for KES 10.
- **Get a mini statement** of your recent transactions for KES 15.

**What agents cannot do.** Agents cannot open accounts, take loan or credit card applications, issue or replace cards, change your details, reset your PIN or handle complaints. For those, use the Kaskazi app, the assistant or a branch.

## Depositing cash at a Kaskazi agent

Depositing cash at a Kaskazi agent is free (KES 0), whatever the amount, and the money is in the account straight away.

**How to deposit:**

1. Give the agent the cash and the 13-digit Kaskazi account number you are paying into.
2. The agent enters the deposit on their Kaskazi agent device.
3. Before the agent confirms, check the account holder's name they read back or show you.
4. You, or the account holder, receive a confirmation message from Kaskazi with the amount, the account and a reference. Keep it until the deposit shows in the account.

**Depositing for someone else.** You can deposit into a family member's or a business's Kaskazi account. You only need their account number; their confirmation goes to their own registered phone.

**If you do not get a confirmation,** do not leave until the agent shows you that the deposit went through. A receipt written by hand is not a confirmation.

## Withdrawing cash at a Kaskazi agent

You can withdraw up to KES 50,000 at a time at a Kaskazi agent. The fee depends on the amount and is taken from your account, not paid in cash.

**How to withdraw:**

1. Tell the agent how much you want and give your Kaskazi account number or registered phone number.
2. The agent starts the withdrawal on their device.
3. A request appears on your own phone, showing the agent's name, the amount and the fee.
4. Check the details, then approve on your phone by entering your PIN. You type the PIN yourself; the agent never sees it.
5. The agent receives confirmation and counts the cash in front of you. You receive a confirmation message from Kaskazi.

**Using your debit card.** If the agent has a card machine, you can use your Kaskazi Debit Card instead. Type your PIN yourself, cover the keypad, and never hand your card over out of sight.

**Withdrawal fee examples:**

- Withdrawing KES 2,500 costs KES 30. Withdrawing KES 3,000 costs KES 50.
- Withdrawing KES 15,000 costs KES 120.
- Withdrawing KES 50,000, the most in one go, costs KES 200.

The amount plus the fee must be in your account. For example, to withdraw KES 10,000, you need KES 10,080.

## Daily and per-withdrawal limits at agents

Two limits apply to cash withdrawals at Kaskazi agents:

- **Maximum per withdrawal: KES 50,000.** For more, make a second withdrawal, which pays its own fee.
- **Daily agent limit: KES 100,000.** This is the most you can withdraw through agents in a day, at one agent or several.

**Example.** To withdraw KES 70,000 in one day, make two withdrawals, for example KES 50,000 (KES 200) and KES 20,000 (KES 120), so KES 320 in fees. Two withdrawals of KES 50,000 reach the KES 100,000 daily limit and cost KES 400.

**Student Account holders.** The Kaskazi Student Account has a daily withdrawal limit of KES 20,000, which also applies to cash taken at agents.

**Agents' own cash.** An agent can only pay out the cash they have in the shop. For a large withdrawal, ask the agent before you start whether they have enough.

## Balance enquiries and mini statements at agents

At a Kaskazi agent, a balance enquiry costs KES 10 and a mini statement costs KES 15. The agent starts the request and you approve it on your phone with your PIN, as with a withdrawal.

**A free alternative.** Your balance and recent transactions are free in the Kaskazi app and online banking. Kaskazi charges nothing for USSD sessions either, although your mobile network may charge. If you have your phone with you, these are usually the cheaper choice.

## Saving money on agent fees

The agent withdrawal fee is set per withdrawal, so how you withdraw affects what you pay.

- **Withdraw once rather than several times.** Withdrawing KES 5,000 at once costs KES 50; two withdrawals of KES 2,500 cost KES 60. Withdrawing KES 20,000 at once costs KES 120; two withdrawals of KES 10,000 cost KES 160.
- **Watch the band edges.** KES 2,500 costs KES 30 but KES 2,501 costs KES 50. KES 20,000 costs KES 120 but KES 20,001 costs KES 200.
- **Use a Kaskazi ATM where you can.** Cash withdrawals at Kaskazi ATMs cost KES 0 with your debit card.
- **Check your balance in the app** for free instead of paying KES 10 at the agent.

## How to spot a genuine Kaskazi agent

Genuine Kaskazi agents follow the same steps every time. Before you hand over cash or approve a withdrawal, check for these signs:

- **The agent uses a Kaskazi agent device**, and every transaction produces a confirmation message from Kaskazi to your phone.
- **Withdrawal requests show the agent's name** on your phone, and it matches the shop you are standing in.
- **The agent displays Kaskazi signage and their agent details** where you can see them.
- **The fee matches this guide.** The fee shown on your phone is the fee in the tables above, and nothing extra is asked for in cash.

**Warning signs of an impostor agent:**

- They ask for your PIN, one-time code or app password, or offer to "help" by entering it.
- They ask to hold your phone or your card out of your sight.
- They ask for a cash "service charge", "registration fee" or "tax" on top of the fee.
- There is no confirmation message, or they say "the system is down, I'll send it later".
- They ask you to send money to a mobile money wallet or a personal account to "verify" or "activate" your account.
- They count the cash out of sight, or rush you.

If you see any of these, stop, keep your PIN and phone to yourself, and use a branch, a Kaskazi ATM or another agent.

## What a Kaskazi agent must never ask for

A genuine Kaskazi agent will never ask you for:

- **Your PIN**, card PIN or app PIN. You type it yourself, on your own phone or on the card machine, covering the keypad.
- **A one-time code.** Codes are only for you to enter in the Kaskazi app or online banking.
- **Your app password** or sign-in details.
- **Your phone**, to "complete" a transaction for you.
- **Your card**, out of your sight.
- **Extra money in cash**, beyond the amount you are depositing. Agent fees are taken by Kaskazi from your account.
- **A transfer to them or anyone else** to "unlock", "verify" or "upgrade" your account.

Kaskazi staff and the Kaskazi assistant will never ask for these either. If an agent asks, do not go ahead, and tell us.

## If something goes wrong at an agent

If a transaction at a Kaskazi agent goes wrong, tell us as soon as you can, and keep your confirmation message.

**Common problems:**

- **Your account was debited but you did not get the cash.** Do not make another withdrawal. Note the agent's name and the time, and tell us.
- **Your deposit is not showing.** Check the confirmation message for the account number. If it is wrong, tell us straight away.
- **You were charged extra in cash, or asked for your PIN.** Report the agent; we take this seriously.
- **You think you were tricked.** Block your card in the app or through the assistant, change your app PIN, and tell us.

**How to report it.** Use the Kaskazi assistant (you can ask for a person at any time), complaints in the app, or a branch. You get a reference in the form KZC- followed by 6 digits. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 7 working days, or within 30 days if they are complex.
