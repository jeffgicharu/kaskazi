# Kaskazi Business Account: terms and conditions

## About the Kaskazi Business Account

The Kaskazi Business Account is Kaskazi Bank's current account for sole traders, partnerships and registered companies. It keeps your business money separate from your personal money, lets you collect card and mobile money payments from your customers at no charge to you, and lets up to 4 people sign for the business.

For a monthly fee of KES 1,000, you get 30 free transactions every month. Each transaction after that costs KES 30.

These terms explain who can open a Kaskazi Business Account, the documents you need, fees, collections, statements, signatories and mandates, and closing the account.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and every figure here is sample data for a demonstration.

## Who can open a Kaskazi Business Account

You can open a Kaskazi Business Account for:

- **a sole trader:** one person trading under a registered business name;
- **a partnership:** people trading together as partners under a registered name; or
- **a registered company:** a limited company incorporated in Kenya.

The business must be registered. If you trade informally without a certificate of registration, you can't open a Kaskazi Business Account yet; you can use a Kaskazi Everyday Account in your own name until the business is registered.

Groups that are not businesses, such as savings groups, welfare groups and churches, are not covered by these terms.

## Documents for a Kaskazi Business Account

To open a Kaskazi Business Account, you need:

- the business's certificate of registration (for a sole trader or partnership) or certificate of incorporation (for a company);
- the business's KRA PIN; and
- the national ID card or passport and the KRA PIN of every signatory.

Each signatory also needs a phone number registered in their own name, because Kaskazi Bank sends one-time codes to it when they approve payments in the app.

All names must match across the documents. If a signatory's ID has expired, they need to renew it before the account can be opened.

## Opening a Kaskazi Business Account

Kaskazi Business Accounts are opened at a branch. You can use any Kaskazi Bank branch.

At the branch, you complete the account form and the mandate, which records who the signatories are and how they sign (see "Kaskazi Business Account signatories and mandates" below). Kaskazi Bank checks the documents and the identity of every signatory.

You need to pay in at least KES 5,000 to open the account. The KES 5,000 minimum applies only at opening. Afterwards, the balance can go lower without any charge for doing so.

Your Kaskazi Business Account has a 13-digit account number starting with "0" and the 3-digit code of the branch where it was opened.

## The Kaskazi Business Account monthly fee

The Kaskazi Business Account monthly fee is KES 1,000. It covers running the account, app and web banking for your signatories, your monthly statement in the app, and your first 30 transactions each month.

The fee is taken from the account at the end of each calendar month. Unlike the Kaskazi Everyday Account, the Business Account fee has no waiver: it is charged every month the account is open, whatever the balance.

Over 12 months, the monthly fee comes to KES 12,000, before any charges for extra transactions.

## Kaskazi Business Account transactions: 30 free, then KES 30 each

Your monthly fee includes 30 transactions each calendar month. Each transaction after the 30th in the same month costs KES 30.

Unused free transactions don't carry over. If you make 20 transactions in November, you still get 30 free in December, not 40.

The KES 30 charge for each extra transaction is added up and taken at the end of the month, together with the monthly fee.

### What counts as a transaction on the Kaskazi Business Account

A transaction is any payment or withdrawal out of the Kaskazi Business Account, including:

- transfers to other Kaskazi accounts and to other banks;
- transfers to mobile money wallets;
- bill payments and standing order payments;
- cheques paid from the account; and
- cash withdrawals at a branch or agent.

These do not count as transactions:

- money paid into the account, including card and mobile money collections, cash deposits and transfers in;
- Kaskazi Bank's own fees and charges.

### Extra transaction charges are on top of payment fees

On the Kaskazi Business Account, the KES 30 charge for a transaction after the first 30 is on top of any fee for the payment itself. For example, a bill payment has its own fee of KES 30. If it is your 35th transaction of the month, you pay KES 30 for the bill payment and KES 30 for the extra transaction.

## Kaskazi Business Account fee examples

### Example 1: a quiet month

Your business makes 25 payments from its Kaskazi Business Account in November. All 25 are within the 30 free transactions.

You pay the monthly fee of KES 1,000 and no extra transaction charges.

### Example 2: a busy month

Your business makes 42 payments from its Kaskazi Business Account in December.

- Free transactions: 30
- Extra transactions: 42 − 30 = 12
- Extra transaction charges: 12 × KES 30 = KES 360
- Monthly fee: KES 1,000
- Total account charges for December: KES 1,360

### Example 3: a busy month with transfer fees

Your business makes 42 payments from its Kaskazi Business Account in December, and 8 of them are instant transfers to other banks of KES 10,000 or less, each with a transfer fee of KES 30.

- Monthly fee and extra transaction charges (KES 1,000 + 12 × KES 30): KES 1,360
- Instant transfer fees: 8 × KES 30 = KES 240
- Total for December: KES 1,600

Hundreds of customer payments coming in through your till in December add nothing to these totals, because money paid in is free and doesn't count as a transaction.

## Collecting payments from your customers into a Kaskazi Business Account

You can collect card and mobile money payments from your customers straight into your Kaskazi Business Account. Kaskazi Bank makes no charge to you for these collections, however many you receive.

Collections are money paid in, so they don't count towards your 30 free transactions.

Each collection shows on your statement with its own reference, so you can match payments to your sales. Your customers' own banks or mobile money wallet providers may charge them under their own terms.

To set up card or mobile money collections, ask at any branch.

## Paying from your Kaskazi Business Account

Payments from your Kaskazi Business Account have these fees, as well as counting towards your monthly transactions:

| Payment | Fee |
|---|---|
| To other Kaskazi accounts | KES 0 |
| Instant transfer to another bank, up to KES 10,000 | KES 30 |
| Instant transfer to another bank, over KES 10,000 and up to KES 100,000 | KES 60 |
| Instant transfer to another bank, over KES 100,000 and up to KES 999,999 | KES 120 |
| Next-day transfer to another bank | KES 100 |
| Same-day high-value (RTGS) transfer, KES 1,000,000 or more | KES 500 |
| Bill payment | KES 30 |
| Each standing order payment | KES 50 |
| Payment to an account abroad | KES 2,500, plus any charges by the banks in between |

Next-day transfers instructed by 3:00 pm on a working day go that day; later instructions go the next working day. Same-day high-value transfers must be instructed by 2:00 pm on a working day.

Payments in the app and web banking are limited to KES 500,000 a day in total. Larger payments, including same-day high-value transfers of KES 1,000,000 or more, are made at a branch with the signatures your mandate requires.

Mobile money wallet transfer fees depend on the amount, up to KES 150,000 per transfer. They are set out in Kaskazi Bank's tariff.

## Cash and Kaskazi agents on the Kaskazi Business Account

You can pay cash into your Kaskazi Business Account at any branch or at a Kaskazi agent. Agent deposits are free, and cash paid in doesn't count towards your 30 free transactions.

A signatory can also withdraw cash from the Business Account at a Kaskazi agent. Agent withdrawals have their own fee, and each one counts as a transaction.

| Agent withdrawal | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to KES 2,500 | KES 30 |
| Over KES 2,500 and up to KES 5,000 | KES 50 |
| Over KES 5,000 and up to KES 10,000 | KES 80 |
| Over KES 10,000 and up to KES 20,000 | KES 120 |
| Over KES 20,000 and up to KES 50,000 | KES 200 |

The most you can withdraw at an agent is KES 50,000 at a time, and KES 100,000 a day in total. A balance enquiry at an agent costs KES 10, and a mini statement KES 15.

Agent withdrawals are only possible if your mandate lets one signatory act alone for that amount, because an agent can't collect a second signatory's approval. For larger amounts, or if your mandate needs more than one signatory, withdraw cash at a branch.

### Agent withdrawal example

A signatory withdraws KES 30,000 at a Kaskazi agent to pay casual workers. The agent fee is KES 200. If it is the business's 31st transaction of the month, a further KES 30 applies, so the withdrawal costs KES 230 in total.

## Protecting your Kaskazi Business Account

Every signatory has their own app login. Never share logins between signatories or with other staff: the mandate only protects the business if each approval really comes from the person named.

Some protections are built in:

- each approval needs a one-time code sent to the signatory's registered phone number, valid for 5 minutes;
- a login is locked after 5 wrong attempts; and
- the app logs you out after 5 minutes without activity.

Kaskazi Bank staff and the Kaskazi assistant will never ask a signatory for a password, PIN or one-time code.

A common fraud against businesses is a message that appears to come from a supplier, saying their bank details have changed. Before paying new details, confirm the change with the supplier using contact details you already have, not the ones in the message. For the first 24 hours after you add a new recipient, you can send them up to KES 50,000, which limits the loss if the details turn out to be false.

## Kaskazi Business Account statements

You get a monthly statement for your Kaskazi Business Account, free in the app. Every signatory can see and download it.

If you need printed statements, for example for your accountant or a tender application, they cost KES 300 a month.

## Kaskazi Business Account signatories and mandates

A signatory is a person who can give Kaskazi Bank instructions on the business's behalf, such as making payments, writing cheques or changing details. A Kaskazi Business Account can have up to 4 signatories.

Signatories are usually:

- for a sole trader, the owner, plus any trusted staff the owner chooses;
- for a partnership, some or all of the partners;
- for a company, directors or staff that the company appoints.

Every signatory must give Kaskazi Bank their national ID card or passport and KRA PIN.

### How a Kaskazi Business Account mandate works

The mandate is the business's written instruction to Kaskazi Bank about who can sign and how. You choose:

- **who the signatories are**, up to 4 people;
- **how many signatories must approve a payment**, from one up to all of them; and, if you want,
- **different rules above an amount you choose**, so that small payments need fewer approvals than large ones.

Kaskazi Bank follows the mandate for every instruction, at a branch and in the app.

### Mandate example

A company's Kaskazi Business Account has 4 signatories: two directors and two senior staff. Its mandate says any one signatory can approve payments up to an amount the company sets, and above that amount, one director and one other signatory must both approve.

A member of staff can pay a supplier's small invoice on their own. A large payment waits until a director has also approved it.

### Approving payments in the app

In the Kaskazi app, one signatory of the Kaskazi Business Account sets up a payment and the others approve it with their own login. A payment is not sent until it has every approval the mandate requires. Each signatory confirms with a one-time code sent to their registered phone number, valid for 5 minutes.

If the approvals aren't given, the payment is never sent. Any signatory who is allowed to approve can also decline it.

### Changing signatories or the mandate

To add or remove a signatory, or change the mandate, give Kaskazi Bank an instruction signed in line with your current mandate. For a company, Kaskazi Bank may also ask for a resolution of the directors.

A new signatory needs to provide their ID and KRA PIN before they can sign. A signatory you remove can no longer give instructions from the moment Kaskazi Bank processes the change.

If a signatory leaves the business, remove them straight away. Until you do, Kaskazi Bank continues to follow the mandate you gave.

## When there isn't enough money in your Kaskazi Business Account

If there isn't enough money in the Kaskazi Business Account to cover a payment and its fee, the payment is not made. A cheque that the balance can't cover is returned unpaid, and a standing order that can't be paid is not made that day.

If the balance is lower than the monthly fee or other charges when they are due, Kaskazi Bank takes what is there, and the rest is taken as soon as money next comes in, for example from your next card or mobile money collection. The balance never goes below zero.

## Keeping your Kaskazi Business Account details up to date

Kaskazi Bank asks every business to confirm its details every 3 years. You also need to tell Kaskazi Bank promptly about any change, such as:

- new owners, partners or directors;
- a new business name or registration;
- a change of signatories; or
- a signatory's renewed ID or new phone number.

If the business's details are out of date, Kaskazi Bank may limit what the account can do until they are updated, for example by stopping payments out while still accepting money in.

## If your Kaskazi Business Account becomes dormant

If no signatory starts a transaction on the Kaskazi Business Account for 12 months, it becomes dormant. Money paid in by others, including card and mobile money collections, doesn't count as a transaction started by you.

The monthly fee of KES 1,000 still applies while the account is dormant. A signatory can reactivate it at any branch with their ID, or in the app with a selfie check, at no cost.

## Closing your Kaskazi Business Account

The business can close its Kaskazi Business Account at any time. The closing instruction must be signed in line with your mandate. For a company, Kaskazi Bank may ask for a resolution of the directors to close the account; for a partnership, all partners must agree.

Before you close it:

- tell your customers and collection providers to stop paying into the account;
- cancel or move your standing orders;
- make sure cheques you have written have been paid, because cheques presented after closing are returned unpaid; and
- return or destroy unused cheques.

When the account closes, Kaskazi Bank takes any monthly fee and charges due, then pays the remaining balance to another account the business chooses.

Kaskazi Bank keeps the records of a closed account for 7 years after it closes.

### When Kaskazi Bank may close a Kaskazi Business Account

Kaskazi Bank may close the account by giving the business notice in writing first. Kaskazi Bank may close it straight away if it reasonably believes the account is being used for fraud or another crime, or if the business gave false information to open it.

## Changes to the Kaskazi Business Account terms

Kaskazi Bank may change these terms, including the monthly fee, the number of free transactions and the charge for extra transactions.

If a change is to the business's disadvantage, Kaskazi Bank will tell the signatories in the app before it takes effect, giving reasonable notice. If the business doesn't accept the change, it can close the account before the change takes effect.
