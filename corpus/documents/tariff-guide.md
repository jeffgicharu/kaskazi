# Fees and charges (tariff guide)

## About this tariff guide

This guide lists every fee and charge Kaskazi Bank makes on its accounts, cards, loans, payments, agent services and digital banking. It applies from 1 October 2026. All amounts are in Kenya shillings (KES) unless a line says otherwise, for example the Foreign Currency Account, whose fees are charged in the account's own currency.

Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration, and every fee here is a sample figure. The figures are still exact: the bank's systems charge from the same list, so the fee you see here is the fee you would pay.

A few rules apply to the whole guide:

- **Government levies are included.** Every fee in this guide already includes any government levy or tax on it. Nothing is added on top at the till or in the app.
- **If a service is not listed, we do not charge for it.** Before we introduce a new fee or change one, we will tell you.
- **You see the fee before you confirm.** In the app, online banking and the assistant, the fee for a payment is shown on the confirmation screen before you approve it.
- **Other banks and providers set their own charges.** Where a payment passes through another bank or a mobile money provider, any charge they make is theirs, not ours. The notes under each table say where this can happen.
- **Interest is not a fee.** Interest rates are in the separate "Interest rates" document. Withholding tax on interest is a tax on your interest, not a bank fee, and is explained there.

## Kaskazi Everyday Account fees

The Kaskazi Everyday Account is our current account for individuals aged 18 and over. Its fees are:

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Minimum opening balance | KES 0 |
| Monthly account fee | KES 250 |
| Debit card | Included (first card KES 0) |
| Cheque book, 25 leaves | KES 750 |
| Overdraft | Not available on this account |
| Interest on your balance | Not paid on this account |

**Notes on the Everyday Account fees**

- **When the monthly fee is waived.** You do not pay the KES 250 monthly fee in any month when either of these is true: your salary is paid into the account that month, or your average balance for the month is KES 50,000 or more. Only one condition needs to be met.
- **Average balance, not the lowest balance.** The KES 50,000 test uses your average balance across the month. A balance that dips below KES 50,000 for a few days can still average KES 50,000 or more.
- **No overdraft, so no overdraft fees.** Payments that would take the account below zero are declined rather than charged.
- **Debit card fees** are the same for every account that comes with a card; see "Kaskazi Debit Card fees" below.

## Kaskazi Goal Saver fees

The Kaskazi Goal Saver is our savings account. It has no monthly fee, but it limits free withdrawals so that your savings keep growing.

| Item | Fee or rule |
|---|---|
| Minimum opening balance | KES 1,000 |
| Monthly account fee | KES 0 |
| Free withdrawals each month | 1 |
| Each extra withdrawal in the same month | KES 150 |
| Number of goals you can set | Up to 5 |
| Debit card | Not included |

**Notes on the Goal Saver fees**

- **What counts as a withdrawal.** Any time you move money out of the Goal Saver, for example to your Everyday Account, it counts as a withdrawal. Your first withdrawal in a calendar month is free; each one after that in the same month costs KES 150.
- **Example.** If you make 3 withdrawals in one month, the first is free and the other two cost KES 150 each, so you pay KES 300.
- **Tip.** Moving one larger amount instead of several small ones keeps you within the free allowance.
- **Withholding tax** of 15.0% is deducted from Goal Saver interest before it is paid. It is a tax, not a fee; see "Interest rates".

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit charges

The Kaskazi Fixed Deposit has no fees. The only cost to watch for is what happens if you take your money out before the term ends.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Minimum deposit | KES 50,000 |
| Terms | 3, 6 or 12 months |
| Account fee | None |
| Early withdrawal | Allowed; you lose half of the interest earned so far |
| Partial withdrawal | Not allowed |
| Rollover | Automatic for the same term at the rate on the rollover date, unless you tell us otherwise at least 2 working days before maturity |

**Notes on the Fixed Deposit charges**

- **Breaking a deposit early** is not charged as a fee. Instead, you keep only half of the interest earned up to the day you withdraw. Your full deposit is always returned.
- **No partial withdrawals.** You cannot take part of the money out and leave the rest. If you need some of it, the whole deposit is closed early.
- **To stop an automatic rollover,** tell us at least 2 working days before the maturity date, in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

These are the fees and charges on the Kaskazi Credit Card. The interest rate on balances you carry over is 2.5% a month (30% a year); the "Interest rates" document explains it in full.

## Kaskazi Personal Loan fees

The Kaskazi Personal Loan lends KES 50,000 to KES 3,000,000 over 6 to 60 months at 15.5% a year on the reducing balance. Its fees are:

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Arrangement fee | 2.5% of the loan amount, minimum KES 2,000, deducted from the loan before it is paid to you |
| Credit life insurance | 0.5% of the loan amount for each year of the term (part years count as a full year), deducted from the loan before it is paid to you |
| Early repayment fee | 2.0% of the amount repaid early, during the first 12 months only |
| Early repayment after 12 months | No fee |
| Late payment fee | KES 500 for each missed instalment |

**Notes on the personal loan fees**

- **Fees come out of the loan.** You do not pay the arrangement fee or insurance in cash. They are deducted before the money reaches your Everyday Account, and your instalments are worked out on the full loan amount.
- **Worked example.** On a loan of KES 500,000 over 36 months, the arrangement fee is KES 12,500 and the insurance is KES 7,500 (0.5% for each of 3 years). You receive KES 480,000. The monthly instalment is about KES 17,455.34.
- **Small loans.** On a KES 50,000 loan, 2.5% is KES 1,250, so the KES 2,000 minimum applies.
- **Part years.** A 30-month term counts as 3 years for insurance, so the insurance is 1.5% of the loan.
- **Early repayment.** Repaying KES 200,000 early in month 8 costs KES 4,000. From month 13 onwards, there is no fee.

## Fees for transfers to other banks in Kenya

There are three ways to send money to an account at another bank in Kenya: an instant transfer, a next-day transfer (EFT) and a same-day high-value transfer (RTGS).

**Instant transfer to another bank**

| Amount sent | Fee |
|---|---|
| KES 1 to KES 10,000 | KES 30 |
| KES 10,001 to KES 100,000 | KES 60 |
| KES 100,001 to KES 999,999 | KES 120 |

The most you can send in one instant transfer is KES 999,999.

**Next-day transfer (EFT) and same-day high-value transfer (RTGS)**

| Transfer | Fee | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Next-day transfer (EFT) | KES 100 | Cut-off 3:00 pm on a working day; later instructions go the next working day |
| Same-day high-value transfer (RTGS) | KES 500 | Minimum KES 1,000,000; cut-off 2:00 pm on a working day |

**Notes on transfers to other banks**

- **Bands are inclusive.** Sending exactly KES 10,000 by instant transfer costs KES 30; sending KES 10,001 costs KES 60.
- **One fee per transfer.** The fee depends only on the amount of that transfer, not on how many transfers you made that day.
- **The receiving bank.** The fee is what Kaskazi charges you. We do not deduct anything from the amount your recipient receives for a transfer within Kenya. Whether the receiving bank charges its own customer is between them.
- **Daily limits** for digital banking also apply; see "Transfers and payments: limits, fees and timings".

## How and when fees are taken

Kaskazi takes fees from the account they relate to.

- **Payment and withdrawal fees** are taken at the same time as the payment, and are shown on the confirmation screen before you approve.
- **Monthly account fees** are taken once a month, after the month ends, when we know whether the waiver conditions were met.
- **Loan fees** (arrangement fee and credit life insurance) are deducted from the loan before it is paid out.
- **Credit card fees** appear on your card statement.
- **Foreign Currency Account fees** are taken in the account's currency.
- **If you think a fee was charged wrongly,** tell us through the assistant, the app or a branch. We will look into it under our complaints process and refund any fee charged in error.
