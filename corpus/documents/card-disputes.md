# Disputing a card payment

This guide explains how to dispute a payment on your Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card, what happens while we investigate, when you get money back, and how much you could lose if someone uses your card without permission.

## What a card dispute is

A card dispute is when you tell Kaskazi Bank that a payment on your Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card is wrong, and ask us to get the money back. We then investigate, which may include asking the business and the card network for evidence.

You can dispute a card payment if, for example:

- you didn't make it, and you don't know who did
- you were charged twice for the same thing
- you were charged the wrong amount
- you paid for goods or services that never arrived
- an ATM took the money from your account but didn't give you the cash
- a business kept charging you after you cancelled a subscription
- a business promised a refund that never arrived

A card dispute is for card payments only. Problems with transfers, mobile money or bill payments are handled through our complaints process.

## The time limit for a card dispute: 60 days

Report a disputed Kaskazi card payment within 60 days of the transaction date. The transaction date is the day the payment was made, shown on your statement.

For example, a payment made on 5 March can be disputed up to 4 May. A payment made on 10 August can be disputed up to 9 October.

After 60 days, we may not be able to recover the money, because the rules for getting money back from businesses through the card network have time limits too. Don't wait for your statement if you can see the payment in the app already. Report it as soon as you notice.

## Before you dispute a card payment: things to check

Some payments look wrong at first but aren't. A quick check can save you time.

- **A business name you don't recognise.** Many businesses use a different name on statements from the one over their door, such as a parent company's name. Look at the date, amount and place before deciding you didn't make it.
- **A pending payment that looks like a double charge.** Hotels, fuel stations and car hire companies often hold an amount when you start and then take the final amount later. Holds usually drop off by themselves. If both amounts are still there once both payments show as completed, dispute the extra one.
- **A family member's purchase.** Check whether anyone in your household used your card or card details, for example for an online game or app.
- **A subscription that renewed.** A free trial may have turned into a paid subscription.
- **A refund in progress.** If a business has told you a refund is on its way, give it a reasonable time to arrive before you dispute.

For goods or services that didn't arrive, contact the business first and ask it to put things right. If it doesn't, dispute the payment and tell us what you asked for and what it said. Keep your messages with the business.

## How to raise a card dispute

You can dispute a Kaskazi card payment:

- in the Kaskazi app, by choosing the payment and selecting the option to dispute it
- through the assistant, by telling it which payment and what went wrong
- at any branch

Tell us:

- which card, and which payment (date, amount and business name)
- what went wrong, in your own words
- whether you still have the card
- anything you have already done, such as contacting the business

Evidence helps, such as receipts, order confirmations, messages with the business, delivery tracking or a police report. You can add evidence later if you don't have it to hand.

If you didn't make the payment, block the card as lost or stolen first, in the app or through the assistant. Someone may have your card details, and blocking the card stops more payments.

## What happens after you report a card dispute

### Acknowledgement within 24 hours

We acknowledge every Kaskazi card dispute within 24 hours. The acknowledgement gives you a reference so you can check progress in the app or with the assistant.

### Provisional credit for disputes up to KES 20,000

For a disputed amount of up to KES 20,000, we put a provisional credit into your account within 10 working days while we investigate. On a Kaskazi Credit Card, the provisional credit is applied to your card balance.

- A disputed payment of KES 18,500 qualifies for a provisional credit.
- A disputed payment of exactly KES 20,000 qualifies.
- A disputed payment of KES 35,000 doesn't qualify, because it is more than KES 20,000. You don't get part of it as a provisional credit either. You get the full amount back if the investigation finds in your favour.

A provisional credit is money we give you while we investigate. If the investigation shows the payment was correct, we will take it back. We will tell you before we do.

### Investigation of up to 45 days

We aim to finish investigating a Kaskazi card dispute within 45 days. Many are finished sooner. While we investigate, we may ask you for more information. Answering quickly helps us finish sooner.

### The outcome of a card dispute

When we finish, we tell you what we found and why.

- **In your favour:** we refund the payment, or confirm that the provisional credit is now yours to keep. If the payment was in another currency, we also refund the currency conversion fee charged on it.
- **Not in your favour:** we explain why, with the evidence. If you had a provisional credit, we take it back, after telling you.

If you are unhappy with the outcome, you can raise a complaint. See the section on complaints about a card dispute.

## Your liability for fraud on a Kaskazi card

Card fraud is when someone uses your Kaskazi Debit Card or Kaskazi Credit Card, or its details, without your permission.

### Before you report it: at most KES 5,000

For fraudulent payments made before you report the problem, the most you lose is KES 5,000. Kaskazi Bank covers the rest. This limit does not apply if you were careless with your PIN or a one-time code. See the section on what counts as carelessness.

### After you report it: KES 0

For fraudulent payments made after you report the problem, you lose nothing (KES 0). Reporting includes blocking the card in the app or through the assistant.

### Worked example: a stolen card

Your Kaskazi Debit Card is stolen from your pocket. Before you notice, the thief makes three contactless payments of KES 4,000 each, a total of KES 12,000. You block the card as lost or stolen and dispute the payments. You had not shared your PIN or any code.

- Fraudulent payments before you reported: KES 12,000
- The most you lose: KES 5,000
- Refunded to you: KES 7,000

If the thief had tried to make another payment after you blocked the card, it would have been declined. Even if a payment had somehow gone through after your report, you would get all of it back.

### Worked example: when you were careless

A caller says he is from Kaskazi Bank and asks for the one-time code that has just arrived on your phone. You read it out. He uses it to approve a KES 30,000 online payment on your card. Because you shared a one-time code, the KES 5,000 limit doesn't apply, and you may have to bear the loss. We will still try to recover the money from the business, and we will look at every case on its facts.

## What counts as carelessness

You are careless with your PIN or code if, for example, you:

- write your PIN on the card, or keep it with the card, such as on a note in the same wallet
- tell anyone your PIN, including family or someone claiming to be from Kaskazi Bank
- share a one-time code with anyone, by reading it out, forwarding the message or typing it into a page someone sent you
- let someone else use your card and PIN
- choose a PIN that is easy to link to you, such as the digits of your birth year printed on a document you carry with the card

It isn't carelessness if someone sees your PIN by watching closely while you cover the keypad, if a card machine was tampered with, or if your card details were stolen from a business you shopped with.

## Examples of card disputes

### A payment taken twice

You pay KES 3,200 at a pharmacy with your Kaskazi Debit Card, and two payments of KES 3,200 appear, both completed. Dispute the second one. Because the amount is under KES 20,000, you get a provisional credit of KES 3,200 within 10 working days while we check with the pharmacy.

If the duplicate payment was in another currency, for example a payment abroad that came to KES 6,000, we refund the KES 6,000 and the 3% currency conversion fee of KES 180 charged on it, KES 6,180 in all.

### Goods you paid for but didn't receive

You order a phone online for KES 28,000 with your Kaskazi Credit Card. It never arrives, and the seller stops answering your messages. Dispute the payment and send us your order confirmation and the messages. The amount is over KES 20,000, so there is no provisional credit, but we investigate within 45 days. If the investigation finds in your favour, we refund the KES 28,000 and any interest and fees charged on it.

### A payment you don't recognise

You see a payment of KES 1,850 to a business name you don't know. First check the date and place, and whether anyone in your household used your card. If you still don't recognise it, block the card as lost or stolen and dispute the payment. If it was fraud and you had not shared your PIN or a code, you get the money back. A payment you don't recognise is often a sign someone has your card details, so we treat it urgently.

### Cash not given out by an ATM

You ask a Kaskazi or other bank's ATM for KES 5,000. The machine makes a noise but no cash comes out, and the money is taken from your account. Dispute the withdrawal, telling us the ATM's location and the time. ATMs keep records that show whether cash was given out. If the machine didn't pay out, we refund the KES 5,000 and any fee charged for the withdrawal.

### A subscription you cancelled

You cancelled a gym membership but the gym charged your card again the next month. Send us proof that you cancelled, such as the cancellation message, and dispute the payment. To stop future charges straight away, you can also temporarily block the card. Remember that a block stops all regular payments on the card, not just the gym's.

## Card disputes on the Kaskazi Credit Card

While a Kaskazi Credit Card payment is in dispute:

- keep paying at least your minimum payment on the rest of your balance, so you don't pay a late payment fee
- if the dispute is decided in your favour, we refund the disputed amount and any interest and fees charged on it

## Complaints about a card dispute

If you are unhappy with how we handled your card dispute or with the outcome, raise a complaint in the app, through the assistant, at a branch or through our contact centre (number not available in this demonstration).

- We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours.
- We aim to resolve them within 7 working days, or within 30 days if the complaint is complex.
- Each complaint gets a reference starting "KZC-" followed by 6 digits.

If you aren't satisfied, you can ask for your complaint to go to the agent or branch who handled it, then the Customer Experience team, then the Head of Customer Experience.
