# Interest rates

## About these interest rates

This document sets out the interest rates Kaskazi Bank pays on savings and charges on borrowing. The rates apply from 1 October 2026, the effective date of this document. Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration, so every rate here is a sample figure, but the bank's systems use exactly these rates.

All rates are a year unless the line says otherwise. Fees, such as the personal loan arrangement fee or the credit card cash advance fee, are not interest; they are listed in the "Fees and charges (tariff guide)".

The worked examples below are approximate, because interest is worked out on the exact number of days. They show the size of the amounts, not the precise figure on your statement.

## Summary of Kaskazi interest rates

| Product | Rate |
|---|---|
| Kaskazi Base Rate | 11.5% a year |
| Kaskazi Personal Loan | 15.5% a year on the reducing balance (Base Rate plus 4.0 percentage points) |
| Kaskazi Credit Card, balances carried over | 2.5% a month (30% a year) |
| Kaskazi Goal Saver, balance of KES 10,000 or more | 5.0% a year on the whole balance |
| Kaskazi Goal Saver, balance below KES 10,000 | 3.0% a year on the whole balance |
| Kaskazi Fixed Deposit, 3 months | 8.0% a year |
| Kaskazi Fixed Deposit, 6 months | 8.75% a year |
| Kaskazi Fixed Deposit, 12 months | 9.5% a year |
| Kaskazi Everyday, Student, Business and Foreign Currency Accounts | No interest paid |
| Withholding tax on savings interest | 15.0%, deducted before interest is paid |

## The Kaskazi Base Rate

The Kaskazi Base Rate is 11.5% a year. It is the bank's reference rate for lending: the personal loan rate is set as the Base Rate plus a fixed margin.

The Base Rate is set by Kaskazi Bank. If it changes, this document will be updated with the new rate and its effective date, and we will tell personal loan customers how the change affects them before it applies.

## Personal loan interest rate

The Kaskazi Personal Loan charges 15.5% a year. This is the Kaskazi Base Rate of 11.5% plus a margin of 4.0 percentage points.

Interest is charged on the reducing balance, and you repay in equal monthly instalments. "Reducing balance" means each month's interest is worked out on what you still owe, not on the original loan. Early instalments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly the loan itself.

**Example.** On a loan of KES 500,000 over 36 months at 15.5% a year:

- The monthly instalment is about KES 17,455.34.
- The first month's interest is about KES 6,458.33, because you owe the full KES 500,000.
- Over the whole term you repay about KES 628,392.26, of which about KES 128,392.26 is interest.

The arrangement fee (2.5% of the loan, minimum KES 2,000) and credit life insurance (0.5% of the loan for each year of the term) are fees, not interest. They are deducted from the loan before it is paid to you and are explained in the tariff guide.

## Credit card interest rate

The Kaskazi Credit Card charges 2.5% a month on balances you carry over, which is 30% a year (2.5% for each of 12 months).

**When you pay no interest.** Purchases have up to 50 days without interest. If you pay the full statement balance by the due date, you pay no interest on those purchases.

**When interest is charged.** If you pay less than the full balance, interest at 2.5% a month is charged on the balance carried over. For example, carrying over KES 40,000 for a month costs about KES 1,000 in interest.

**Cash advances are different.** Interest on cash withdrawn with the credit card is charged from the day of the withdrawal, with no interest-free period, and the cash advance fee (3.0%, minimum KES 500) is charged as well.

The minimum payment is 5.0% of the balance or KES 1,000, whichever is more, or the full balance if it is below KES 1,000. Paying only the minimum keeps the card in good standing but means interest is charged on the rest.

## Kaskazi Goal Saver interest rates

The Kaskazi Goal Saver pays interest at one of two rates, depending on your balance:

| Balance | Rate |
|---|---|
| KES 10,000 or more | 5.0% a year on the whole balance |
| Below KES 10,000 | 3.0% a year on the whole balance |

**How the tiers work.** The rate applies to the whole balance, not just the part above KES 10,000. A balance of exactly KES 10,000 earns 5.0%.

**How interest is calculated and paid.** Interest is calculated daily on the closing balance, so each day earns the rate that matches that day's closing balance. It is paid monthly, on the last working day of the month, after withholding tax of 15.0% has been deducted.

**Examples for one month (approximate):**

| Balance all month | Rate | Interest before tax | Withholding tax (15.0%) | Interest paid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KES 9,500 | 3.0% | KES 23.75 | KES 3.56 | KES 20.19 |
| KES 20,000 | 5.0% | KES 83.33 | KES 12.50 | KES 70.83 |
| KES 100,000 | 5.0% | KES 416.67 | KES 62.50 | KES 354.17 |

Topping up a KES 9,500 balance by KES 500 moves the whole balance to 5.0%.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest rates

The Kaskazi Fixed Deposit pays a fixed rate for the term you choose. The minimum deposit is KES 50,000.

| Term | Rate |
|---|---|
| 3 months | 8.0% a year |
| 6 months | 8.75% a year |
| 12 months | 9.5% a year |

**Fixed for the term.** The rate on the day your deposit starts stays the same until it matures, even if our published rates change.

**Paid at maturity.** Interest is paid at the end of the term, after withholding tax of 15.0%.

**Examples on KES 100,000 (approximate):**

| Term | Interest before tax | Withholding tax (15.0%) | Interest paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 months at 8.0% | KES 2,000.00 | KES 300.00 | KES 1,700.00 |
| 6 months at 8.75% | KES 4,375.00 | KES 656.25 | KES 3,718.75 |
| 12 months at 9.5% | KES 9,500.00 | KES 1,425.00 | KES 8,075.00 |

**Rollover.** At maturity, the deposit rolls over automatically for the same term at the rate on the rollover date, unless you tell us otherwise at least 2 working days before maturity. The new rate may differ from the old one.

**Withdrawing early.** You can withdraw before maturity, but you lose half of the interest earned so far. For example, on KES 200,000 for 12 months at 9.5%, withdrawing after 6 months means about KES 9,500 has been earned; you keep half, about KES 4,750, before withholding tax. Partial withdrawals are not allowed.

## Withholding tax on interest

Withholding tax of 15.0% is deducted from the interest on the Goal Saver and the Fixed Deposit before the interest is paid to you. Kaskazi Bank pays the tax to the tax authority on your behalf.

- The rates in this document are **before** tax. The amount you receive is the interest earned less the 15.0% tax.
- On KES 1,000 of interest, KES 150 is deducted and you receive KES 850.
- Withholding tax is a tax, not a bank fee. It does not apply to accounts that pay no interest.

## Accounts that pay no interest

These Kaskazi accounts do not pay interest on your balance:

- Kaskazi Everyday Account
- Kaskazi Student Account
- Kaskazi Business Account
- Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account (USD, GBP and EUR)

If you want your money to earn interest, you can move savings into a Goal Saver or a Fixed Deposit in the app, through the assistant or at a branch. Transfers between your own Kaskazi accounts are free.

## Changes to interest rates

The rates in this document are sample figures, effective from 1 October 2026. When a rate changes, we publish a new version of this document with its new effective date.

- **Fixed Deposits** already running keep their rate until maturity. A rollover takes the rate on the rollover date.
- **Goal Saver** balances earn the published rate for each day.
- **Personal loans** are linked to the Kaskazi Base Rate. We will tell you before a change to the Base Rate affects your loan.
- **Credit card** customers will be told before any change to the card's interest rate applies.
