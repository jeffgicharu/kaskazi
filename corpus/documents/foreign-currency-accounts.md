# Foreign currency accounts

## About the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

The Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account lets you hold money in US dollars (USD), British pounds (GBP) or euros (EUR), instead of Kenya shillings. It suits you if you receive money from abroad, pay for things in a foreign currency, such as school fees or suppliers abroad, or want to keep some savings in another currency.

The Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account is a current account. It does not pay interest. Receiving money from abroad into it is free; sending money abroad has a fee.

## Which currencies the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account comes in

Kaskazi Bank offers the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account in three currencies: US dollars (USD), British pounds (GBP) and euros (EUR). It is not available in any other currency.

Each Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account holds one currency. If you want to hold US dollars and euros, you open two accounts, one in each currency. You can have a USD, a GBP and a EUR account at the same time, and each has its own minimum opening balance and monthly fee.

## Opening a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can open a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account at any branch or in the app. You need the same documents as for any Kaskazi Bank account: your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN and a phone number registered in your name. A business opens one with the documents it gave for its Kaskazi Business Account.

### Minimum opening balance

To open a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account, you pay in at least:

- USD 100 for a USD account;
- GBP 100 for a GBP account; or
- EUR 100 for a EUR account.

You can pay in the opening amount by converting Kenya shillings from your Kaskazi account, by receiving a transfer from abroad, or with foreign cash at a branch.

The minimum applies only when you open the account. Afterwards the balance can go lower, but the monthly fee keeps being charged, so a very small balance is slowly used up by fees.

## Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account monthly fees

Each Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account has a monthly fee, charged in the account's own currency:

| Account | Monthly fee | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|
| USD account | USD 5 | USD 60 |
| GBP account | GBP 4 | GBP 48 |
| EUR account | EUR 5 | EUR 60 |

The fee is taken from the account at the end of each calendar month. There is no waiver: the fee is charged every month the account is open, whatever the balance.

If you hold accounts in more than one currency, each one is charged its own fee. Someone with a USD and a EUR account pays USD 5 and EUR 5 each month.

### If the balance is lower than the monthly fee

If the balance is lower than the monthly fee when it is due, Kaskazi Bank takes what is there, and the rest is taken when money next comes into the account. The balance never goes below zero.

### Monthly fee example

You open a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account in USD with USD 100 and never use it. After 12 months of USD 5 fees, USD 60 has been taken and USD 40 is left. If you don't need a foreign currency account any more, it is cheaper to close it than to leave it unused.

## No interest on the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

The Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account does not pay interest in any currency, whatever your balance.

If you want interest, Kaskazi Bank's interest-paying accounts, the Kaskazi Goal Saver and the Kaskazi Fixed Deposit, are in Kenya shillings only. Moving money into them means converting it to shillings at Kaskazi Bank's exchange rate.

## Converting money to and from a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can move money between your Kenya shilling account and your Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account in the app or at a branch. Kaskazi Bank converts it at its exchange rate at the time.

Kaskazi Bank sets its exchange rates every working day, and they can change during the day. The rate for buying a currency is different from the rate for selling it. Before you confirm a conversion, the app or the branch shows you the rate and the exact amount you will get.

Transfers between your own Kaskazi accounts have no transfer fee. The cost of a conversion is in the exchange rate.

## Taking foreign cash from a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can withdraw cash in the account's currency at a Kaskazi Bank branch. The fee is 1.0% of the amount, taken from the account in the same currency.

| You withdraw | Fee at 1.0% |
|---|---|
| USD 1,000 from a USD account | USD 10 |
| GBP 400 from a GBP account | GBP 4 |
| EUR 2,500 from a EUR account | EUR 25 |

You need enough in the account for the cash and the fee together. To withdraw USD 1,000, you need at least USD 1,010 in the account.

Branches keep limited stocks of foreign notes, and the choice of note sizes can vary. If you need a large amount of foreign cash, ask the branch in advance so that it can order it for you.

If you want Kenya shillings instead, move the money to your shilling account first, at the exchange rate, and withdraw shillings from there.

## Paying foreign cash into a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can pay USD, GBP or EUR notes into the account of the same currency at a Kaskazi Bank branch.

Kaskazi Bank checks every note. Notes that are damaged, badly worn, heavily marked or no longer in circulation may be refused. Coins are not accepted.

## Debit cards and the Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

The Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account does not come with its own debit card.

When you travel, you can use your Kaskazi Debit Card on your Kenya shilling account. Paying or withdrawing in a foreign currency with the debit card has a currency conversion fee of 3.0% of the amount, and cash at an ATM abroad costs KES 300 per withdrawal.

## Receiving money from abroad into a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

Kaskazi Bank does not charge you for receiving money from abroad. The Kaskazi fee for an incoming international payment is KES 0.

To receive money, give the person sending it:

- your name, exactly as it appears on the account;
- your 13-digit Kaskazi account number;
- Kaskazi Bank's international bank identifier code (not available in this demonstration); and
- the name of your branch.

### Charges by other banks on money you receive

Kaskazi Bank charges nothing, but the sender's bank, and any bank the payment passes through on the way, may take their own charges. These are often taken out of the payment itself, so the amount that arrives can be less than the amount sent.

For example, if a relative abroad sends you USD 2,000, the payment may arrive as slightly less than USD 2,000 because a bank along the way took its charge. Kaskazi Bank takes nothing from it. If you need an exact amount to arrive, the sender can ask their bank whether they can pay all the charges at their end.

### Money that arrives in a different currency

If money arrives in a currency different from your account's, for example euros sent to your USD account, Kaskazi Bank converts it into the account's currency at its exchange rate on the day. To avoid a conversion, give the sender the details of the account in the currency they are sending.

## Sending money abroad from a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can send money to a bank account abroad from your Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account or from your Kenya shilling account. The Kaskazi Bank fee is KES 2,500 per payment, plus any charges by the banks in between.

If you pay from a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account, the KES 2,500 fee is taken from that account as the equivalent amount in its currency, at Kaskazi Bank's exchange rate on the day.

### What you need to send money abroad

- the recipient's full name and address;
- their account number;
- their bank's name, country and international bank identifier code; and
- the reason for the payment.

For large payments, Kaskazi Bank may ask for a document that shows what the payment is for, such as an invoice or a fees letter.

Payments up to the app's daily payment limit of KES 500,000 in total can be sent in the app. Larger payments are made at a branch.

### Charges by other banks on money you send

The banks a payment passes through, and the recipient's bank, may deduct their own charges. Kaskazi Bank doesn't control these and can't tell you in advance exactly what they will be. The recipient may therefore receive less than you sent.

If an exact amount must arrive, for example a school fees invoice, ask the recipient's bank about its charges and allow for them.

### International payment example

You send USD 3,000 from your USD account to pay a university abroad.

- Kaskazi Bank's fee: KES 2,500, taken from your USD account as its equivalent in US dollars at the exchange rate on the day.
- Charges by the banks in between: taken by those banks, usually from the payment on the way.
- The university may receive slightly less than USD 3,000, so check whether it needs the full amount.

### If an international payment is returned

If a payment is returned, for example because the account details were wrong, the money comes back to your account less any charges taken by other banks. Kaskazi Bank's KES 2,500 fee covers sending the payment and is not refunded if the details you gave were wrong.

Check the recipient's details carefully before you confirm.

## Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account statements

You get a monthly statement for each Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account in the app, free. It shows each payment in and out, every conversion and its exchange rate, and the monthly fee.

A printed statement at a branch costs KES 300 per statement.

## If you stop using your Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

If you don't start any transaction on your Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account for 12 months, it becomes dormant. Money sent to you from abroad doesn't count as a transaction started by you.

The monthly fee still applies while the account is dormant. You can reactivate the account at any branch with your ID, or in the app with a selfie check, at no cost.

## Closing a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account

You can close a Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account at any time, at a branch or in the app. Before it closes, you choose what happens to the balance:

- convert it into your Kenya shilling account at the exchange rate on the day, with no transfer fee;
- send it abroad, with the KES 2,500 fee plus any charges by the banks in between; or
- take it as foreign cash at a branch, with the 1.0% cash withdrawal fee.

The monthly fee for the month you close is taken first.
