# Privacy notice: how we use your personal data

This notice explains what personal data Kaskazi Bank holds about you, why we hold it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It also explains how our AI assistant handles your messages. It applies from 1 October 2026.

## Who we are and what this privacy notice covers

Kaskazi Bank Limited ("Kaskazi Bank", "we", "us") is responsible for the personal data described in this notice. Our head office is in Mombasa.

This privacy notice covers everyone whose personal data we hold, including:

- customers with accounts, cards or loans
- people who apply for a product, even if they don't go ahead or are turned down
- signatories and directors of business customers
- people who chat with our assistant, call our contact centre or visit a branch

"Personal data" means any information that identifies you or could be linked to you, such as your name, ID number, phone number, account number or the record of your payments.

## The personal data Kaskazi Bank holds about you

### Identity and contact details

When you open an account, we collect the details we need to confirm who you are:

- your name, date of birth and national ID card or passport details
- your KRA PIN
- a phone number registered in your name
- a selfie, if you open your account or verify yourself in the Kaskazi app
- your address and email, if you give them to us
- for a Kaskazi Student Account, your student ID
- for a business account, the business's registration or incorporation certificate and KRA PIN, and the IDs and KRA PINs of all signatories

We ask you to update these details every 3 years, or sooner if they change.

### Account, card and transaction data

- the accounts, cards and loans you have with us, and their balances
- every payment in and out, including card payments, transfers, mobile money payments, bill payments, agent and ATM transactions
- the people and businesses you pay and receive money from
- your statements, disputes and complaints

### Financial and credit information

When you apply for a loan or a Kaskazi Credit Card, we collect:

- your payslips and employer's details
- bank statements from other banks, if your salary isn't paid into Kaskazi
- the information credit reference bureaus hold about you
- other loans and repayments you tell us about

### Digital and device information

When you use the Kaskazi app, online banking or the assistant, we collect:

- your login times and the device you use
- the messages you send to the assistant, and photos of documents you choose to share
- information that helps us spot fraud, such as unusual logins or a new device

### Calls, visits and security recordings

We may record calls to our contact centre, to check quality and resolve disputes. Our branches and ATMs have security cameras.

## Where Kaskazi Bank gets your personal data from

Most of the personal data we hold comes from you: what you tell us when you apply, the documents you give us, and the way you use your accounts.

We also receive personal data from:

- **licensed credit reference bureaus,** when you apply for credit
- **your employer,** indirectly, through the salary payments made into your account and the payslips you give us
- **other banks and mobile money providers,** about payments you send or receive, and in fraud investigations
- **the card network,** about card payments and disputes
- **the people who pay you,** such as the name they give when sending you money
- **fraud prevention and screening checks,** including lists of people who are subject to financial restrictions under the law
- **a business customer,** about its signatories and directors

## If you don't give Kaskazi Bank the personal data we need

Some personal data is required by law or is needed to provide a product. For example, we can't open an account without your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN and a phone number registered in your name, and we can't consider a loan without proof of your income.

If you don't give us what we need, we may not be able to open your account, approve your application or carry out an instruction. We will always tell you which details are required and which are optional.

If you don't update your details when we ask, every 3 years, we may limit what you can do on your account until you do.

## Personal data about other people that you give Kaskazi Bank

Sometimes you give us personal data about someone else, for example:

- the name and account or phone number of a person you send money to
- the details of the other signatories on a business account
- the name of someone you want to act for you

Please only give us someone else's details if you have the right to, and let them know that we will hold them in line with this notice. We use these details only for the purpose you gave them to us, such as making the payment.

## Marketing messages from Kaskazi Bank

We only send you marketing messages about our products if you have agreed. You can choose how you hear from us, for example in the app or by text message, and you can change your mind at any time in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

Even if you have said no to marketing, we will still send you messages we need to send to run your account, such as one-time codes, payment alerts, statements, and notice of changes to fees or terms.

We never pass your details to other companies for them to market their own products to you.

## Why Kaskazi Bank uses your personal data

We use your personal data to:

- **open and run your accounts, cards and loans:** making payments, sending statements, charging fees and paying interest
- **confirm who you are** when you open an account, log in, or ask us to do something, such as unblocking a card with a one-time code
- **meet our legal duties,** including checks to prevent money laundering and financing of crime, and keeping records the law requires
- **protect you and the bank from fraud,** for example by spotting unusual payments, applying the new-recipient limit, and investigating disputed card payments
- **decide whether to lend to you,** and how much, using your income, other commitments and credit record
- **answer your questions and complaints,** including through the assistant
- **improve our service,** for example by checking how well the assistant answers questions
- **tell you about products,** but only if you have agreed to this

## Our legal reasons for using your personal data

The law only lets us use your personal data when we have a good reason. In plain terms, these are the reasons we rely on.

### Because we have an agreement with you

We need your data to provide the accounts, cards and loans you have asked for. For example, we can't make a transfer for you without knowing who it is going to, or send you a statement without your contact details.

### Because the law requires it

Banks must check their customers' identity, keep records of transactions, report certain suspicious transactions to the authorities, and keep records for a set time. We do these things because the law requires them, not because you've agreed.

### Because it is in our legitimate interests, fairly balanced against yours

Some uses are needed to run the bank well and safely, such as preventing fraud, recording calls for quality, and improving our services. We only rely on this reason when our interest doesn't outweigh your rights. You can object (see the section on your rights).

### Because you have agreed

For some uses we ask for your consent first, for example sending you marketing messages, or letting an AI service read a photo of your document. You can withdraw your consent at any time, and it won't affect anything we did before.

## Credit checks with credit reference bureaus

When you apply for a Kaskazi Personal Loan or a Kaskazi Credit Card, we check your credit record with licensed credit reference bureaus. These are independent companies that collect information on how people across the country repay loans and cards.

- **What we receive:** your loans and cards with other lenders, how you've repaid them, and any defaults.
- **What we share:** your application, and then how you repay what you borrow from us, including missed payments.

Lenders use this information when you apply for credit, so missed payments with us can make it harder to borrow elsewhere, and paying on time helps your record.

You can ask a credit reference bureau for a copy of what it holds about you. If something is wrong, ask the bureau to correct it, and tell us if it relates to a Kaskazi account.

## Who Kaskazi Bank shares your personal data with

We only share your personal data when we need to, and only as much as we need to. We never sell your personal data.

We share it with:

- **other banks, mobile money providers and the card network,** to make the payments you ask for, and to investigate disputes and fraud
- **licensed credit reference bureaus,** as explained in the section on credit checks
- **service providers who work for us,** such as companies that host our systems, produce cards, send text messages with one-time codes, and provide AI services for the assistant. They may only use your data to provide that service to us.
- **our auditors and professional advisers**
- **government authorities, courts and the police,** when the law requires us to, for example for tax, crime prevention or a court order
- **the government authority for unclaimed money:** after a long period with no contact, the law requires banks to hand unclaimed money to a government authority, from which you can still claim it. We share the details needed for that.
- **people you authorise,** such as someone holding a power of attorney for you

### Sending personal data outside Kenya

Some of our service providers, including AI services, may process data outside Kenya. When they do, we put safeguards in place so that your data is protected to the same standard as in Kenya.

## How long Kaskazi Bank keeps your personal data

We keep your personal data while you are a customer, and for 7 years after you close your account. If you have more than one product, the 7 years run from when your last account with us is closed.

We keep it for that long because the law requires banks to keep records, and because we may need them to answer questions, complaints or legal claims about your account after it closes.

After 7 years, we delete your personal data or make it anonymous, so it can no longer be linked to you.

## How our AI assistant handles your data

The Kaskazi assistant answers your questions using our own documents and can help with tasks such as blocking a card. It uses an AI service to understand your messages and write its replies. This section explains how we protect your data when you use it.

### Identifiers are masked before any AI service sees your message

Before any AI service sees a message you send to the assistant, we mask these identifiers:

- national ID card and passport numbers
- phone numbers
- account numbers
- card numbers
- email addresses

Masking means we replace each one with a placeholder, so the AI service sees, for example, "[account number]" instead of the number itself. Our own systems keep the real details, so the assistant can still act on your request, such as checking the right account.

For example, if you type "Has my salary come in? My account number is" followed by your account number, the AI service sees "Has my salary come in? My account number is [account number]".

Even with masking, please don't type your PIN, a one-time code or a password into the assistant. It will never ask for them. If you do type one by mistake, change it.

### Photos of documents: the one exception

Photos of documents are the one case where an AI service reads the image itself. This happens, for example, when you share a photo of your ID or a payslip as part of an application, and the assistant reads the details from it.

A photo can't be masked in the same way as text, so an AI service reads it only after you agree. The assistant asks you first, every time. If you'd rather not agree, you can bring the document to any branch for our staff to check instead. Saying no doesn't affect the outcome of your application.

### A person can always take over

You can ask for a person at any time in a conversation with the assistant, and the assistant will also pass you to a person when a question needs one.

- During staffed hours, 7:00 am to 10:00 pm every day, we aim to hand you over within 5 minutes.
- Outside staffed hours, the conversation waits in the queue and a person replies when the team is next in. Card blocks never wait, because you can do them yourself at any time.

When a person takes over, they see the conversation so far, so you don't need to repeat yourself.

### Conversations are kept in an audit trail

We keep a record of your conversations with the assistant in an audit trail. It shows what you asked, what the assistant replied, which of our documents it relied on, any actions taken (such as a card block) and when a person took over.

We use the audit trail to:

- check the assistant is giving correct answers
- investigate complaints, disputes and fraud
- show what was done, and when, if there is any question about it later

The audit trail is part of your customer records and is kept for the same period: while you are a customer, and for 7 years after you close your account.

## Your rights over your personal data

You have the following rights. To use any of them, ask in the app, through the assistant or at any branch. We may ask you to confirm who you are first, to protect your data from people pretending to be you.

### Seeing your personal data (access)

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We respond to requests for your data within 7 days. If we can't provide something, we explain why.

### Correcting your personal data

If something we hold about you is wrong or out of date, ask us to correct it. Some changes, such as a new name, need a document to support them.

### Objecting to how we use your personal data

You can object to us using your data where we rely on our legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have a strong reason to continue, such as preventing fraud. You can object to marketing at any time, and we will always stop.

### Deleting your personal data

You can ask us to delete your personal data where the law allows it. We can't delete records the law requires us to keep, such as your identity checks and transactions, until the 7-year period after you close your account has passed. We will explain what we can delete and what we must keep.

### Withdrawing consent and stopping marketing

Where we rely on your consent, for example for marketing messages or for an AI service to read a document photo, you can withdraw it at any time in the app or through the assistant.

### Decisions made automatically

If a decision about you is made automatically, for example a payment stopped by our fraud checks, you can ask for a person to review it and explain the decision.

## Keeping your personal data secure

We protect your data with security measures including:

- giving staff access only to the data they need for their job
- one-time codes sent to your registered phone number to confirm important actions; each code works for 5 minutes
- logging you out of the app after 5 minutes without use
- locking the app after 5 wrong login attempts
- masking identifiers before AI services see your messages

If a security problem affects your personal data in a way that puts you at risk, we will tell you and explain what you should do.

Our personal accounts are for people aged 18 and over.

## The Data Protection Officer and complaints about your data

Our Data Protection Officer oversees how Kaskazi Bank uses personal data. Contact details for the Data Protection Officer are not available in this demonstration. You can also raise any privacy question in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, you can complain to us. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 7 working days, or within 30 days if the complaint is complex. Each complaint gets a reference starting "KZC-" followed by 6 digits.

In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

## Changes to this privacy notice

We may update this privacy notice, for example when we offer new services or the law changes. We will tell you about important changes in the app before they take effect. The version and date at the top of this notice show when it was last updated.
