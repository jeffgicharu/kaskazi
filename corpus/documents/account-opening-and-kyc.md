# Opening an account and keeping your details up to date

## About opening a Kaskazi Bank account

This guide explains what you need to open a Kaskazi Bank account, whether you are an individual, a student or a business, and how to open it in the app or at a branch. It also explains why Kaskazi Bank checks who you are, why it asks you to confirm your details every 3 years, and what happens if your details are out of date.

The rules for each account, such as fees and interest, are in that account's own terms and conditions.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and every figure here is sample data for a demonstration.

## Why Kaskazi Bank checks who you are

Before Kaskazi Bank opens an account, it checks that you are who you say you are. Real banks are required to know their customers, and Kaskazi Bank follows the same practice in this demonstration. The checks protect you as much as the bank:

- **They stop someone else opening an account in your name.** Without a check, a fraudster with a photo of your ID could open an account and borrow or receive stolen money in your name.
- **They stop accounts being used for crime.** Accounts opened under false names are used to move money from fraud and other crimes. Checking every customer makes that much harder.
- **They let Kaskazi Bank reach you safely.** Your registered phone number receives the one-time codes that approve payments and unblock cards, so it must really be yours.
- **They get your tax right.** Your KRA PIN is needed so that withholding tax on interest, and any other tax matters, are recorded against the right person.

Kaskazi Bank uses your documents only to open and run your accounts. Staff and the Kaskazi assistant will never ask you for your PIN, your password or a one-time code, whatever reason they give.

## Documents for individuals

To open any Kaskazi Bank personal account, including the Kaskazi Everyday Account, Kaskazi Goal Saver, Kaskazi Fixed Deposit and Kaskazi Foreign Currency Account, you need:

- your national ID card or passport;
- your KRA PIN;
- a phone number registered in your name; and
- a selfie, if you open the account in the app.

### National ID card or passport

Your national ID card or passport must be the original and must not have expired. Kaskazi Bank can't open an account with a photocopy, a police abstract for a lost ID, or an expired document.

If you are not a Kenyan citizen, use your passport.

### KRA PIN

To open a Kaskazi Bank account, your KRA PIN must be in your own name, and the name must match your ID. If you don't have a KRA PIN yet, you need to get one before opening the account.

### A phone number registered in your name

The phone number you give Kaskazi Bank must be registered in your own name with your mobile network. A number registered to a parent, spouse or employer can't be used, even if you are the person who uses it.

This matters because Kaskazi Bank sends one-time codes to that number to confirm payments and to unblock your card. A one-time code is valid for 5 minutes. If the number belonged to someone else, they could receive your codes.

### A selfie, for the app

When you open a Kaskazi Bank account in the app, it asks you to take a selfie. The app compares it with the photo on your ID to confirm that the person opening the account is the person on the ID. You don't need a selfie at a branch, because a member of staff checks your face against your ID in person.

## Extra document for students

To open a Kaskazi Student Account, you need everything an individual needs, plus a valid student ID from your university or college.

The Kaskazi Student Account is for students aged 18 to 25. Your student ID must show your name, your institution and a date it is valid until, and the name must match your national ID card or passport. An expired student ID can't be used.

## Documents for businesses

To open a Kaskazi Business Account, the business needs:

- its certificate of registration (for a sole trader or partnership) or certificate of incorporation (for a company);
- the business's KRA PIN; and
- the national ID card or passport and KRA PIN of every signatory.

A Kaskazi Business Account can have up to 4 signatories, and Kaskazi Bank checks the identity of each one in the same way as for an individual. Each signatory also needs a phone number registered in their own name for one-time codes.

The business's name must be the same on the certificate and on the KRA PIN.

## Opening a Kaskazi Bank account in the app

Opening an account in the Kaskazi app takes about 10 minutes if you have your documents ready. The app's download location is not available in this demonstration.

1. Enter your phone number. The app sends a one-time code to it, valid for 5 minutes, to check that the phone is in your hands.
2. Photograph the front and back of your national ID card, or the photo page of your passport.
3. Enter your KRA PIN.
4. Take a selfie when the app asks.
5. If you are opening a Kaskazi Student Account, photograph your student ID.
6. Choose the account you want and the branch it should belong to.
7. Read and accept the account's terms and conditions.

When the checks are complete, the app shows your new 13-digit account number. It starts with "0" and the 3-digit code of the branch you chose.

### What can slow down opening an account in the app

Opening a Kaskazi Bank account in the app usually takes about 10 minutes, but it can take longer if:

- a photo of your ID is blurred, has glare on it or cuts off an edge;
- the names on your ID and KRA PIN don't match exactly;
- the app can't match your selfie to your ID photo, for example in poor light; or
- your phone number turns out not to be registered in your name.

If the app can't finish the checks, it tells you why. You can try again, or take your documents to any branch to finish opening the account there.

## Opening a Kaskazi Bank account at a branch

You can open any Kaskazi Bank account at any branch. Bring the original documents listed above and your phone. A member of staff checks your documents, confirms your phone number with a one-time code and opens the account.

Branches are open from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday. They are closed on Sundays and public holidays. Kaskazi Bank aims to serve you within 15 minutes of your arrival.

## Opening an account for someone else

Each person opens their own Kaskazi Bank account, with their own documents, phone number and selfie or branch visit. You can't open a personal account in another adult's name, even a family member's, and you can't use your documents to open one for them.

Kaskazi Bank's personal accounts are for people aged 18 and over. If you want to share an account with someone, a joint Kaskazi Everyday Account can be opened at a branch, with every holder present and bringing their own documents.

## If you lose your ID after opening an account

Your Kaskazi Bank accounts keep working if you lose your national ID card or passport. You don't need to tell the bank straight away unless you think someone may try to use it, in which case tell Kaskazi Bank so that it can watch your accounts more closely.

When your replacement arrives, update it in the app or at a branch. Until then, anything that needs a valid ID can't be done, such as reactivating a dormant account at a branch, changing your name, or applying for a loan.

## When the names on your documents don't match

To open and use a Kaskazi Bank account, your name must be the same on your ID, your KRA PIN and, for students, your student ID. Small differences cause problems later, for example when you apply for a loan, because loan documents are only accepted if the names match on every document.

Common causes and what to do:

- **Names in a different order** on two documents: usually acceptable if all the names are the same. Staff at a branch can confirm.
- **A spelling difference**, such as one letter: get the incorrect document corrected by the office that issued it before opening the account.
- **A change of name**, for example after marriage: bring the document that shows the change, such as a marriage certificate, along with your ID in your current name.

## Keeping your Kaskazi Bank details up to date

Kaskazi Bank asks you to confirm your details every 3 years. The app reminds you when it is time. You also need to tell Kaskazi Bank whenever something changes, without waiting for the reminder.

The details Kaskazi Bank keeps up to date are:

- your ID, when you renew it or get a new one;
- your registered phone number;
- your name, if it changes;
- the town where you live;
- your occupation and employer; and
- for students, a valid student ID.

Confirming your details takes a few minutes in the app. If nothing has changed, you simply confirm that everything is still correct.

## How to update your Kaskazi Bank details

You can update most of your details in the app or at any branch.

### Changing your registered phone number

Because one-time codes go to your registered phone number, changing it needs extra care. Fraudsters sometimes try to take over a victim's phone number so that the codes go to them.

You can change your phone number at any branch with your ID, or in the app with a selfie check. The new number must be registered in your name. Kaskazi Bank tells you on your old number that the change has been made, so that you can act quickly if you didn't ask for it.

### Renewing your ID

When your national ID card or passport is renewed or replaced, update it in the app by photographing the new document, or bring it to a branch. If your document number changes, Kaskazi Bank updates it on all your accounts.

### Changing your name

To change your name on your Kaskazi Bank accounts, bring your ID in the new name and the document that shows the change to any branch. Name changes can't be made in the app.

## Business details: keeping them up to date

A business with a Kaskazi Business Account confirms its details every 3 years, like an individual. It must also tell Kaskazi Bank promptly when its owners, partners, directors, signatories, registered name or KRA PIN change.

Changes to signatories are made with an instruction signed in line with the business's mandate. Each new signatory provides their ID and KRA PIN.

## What happens if your details are out of date

If your 3-year confirmation is overdue, or Kaskazi Bank knows that a document has expired, it reminds you in the app first.

If you still don't update your details, Kaskazi Bank may limit what you can do with your accounts until you do. For example, it may stop payments out, card payments or applications for new products, while still letting money come in. Your money stays safe in the account, and the limits are lifted as soon as your details are updated.

Out-of-date details can also hold you up elsewhere:

- **Loans:** a Kaskazi Personal Loan or Salary Advance application needs an ID that has not expired.
- **Unblocking your card:** if your registered phone number is no longer yours, you won't receive the one-time code you need to unblock your card.
- **Reactivating a dormant account:** you need a valid ID at a branch, or a selfie check in the app that matches your ID.

## How Kaskazi Bank looks after your details

Kaskazi Bank keeps your details and records for as long as you have an account, and for 7 years after your account closes.

You can ask Kaskazi Bank for a copy of the information it holds about you. Kaskazi Bank responds to a request for your data within 7 days.

## Getting help with opening an account

If you are not sure which documents you need, or the app can't finish your checks, ask the Kaskazi assistant or visit any branch. The assistant can hand you to a person between 7:00 am and 10:00 pm, every day. Contact details for the bank are not available in this demonstration.
