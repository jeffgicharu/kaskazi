# How to make a complaint

## About making a complaint to Kaskazi Bank

If Kaskazi Bank has let you down, we want to hear about it. A complaint is any time you tell us you are unhappy with our service, a product, a fee, a decision, a member of staff, an agent or the Kaskazi assistant, and you want us to put it right. You do not have to use the word "complaint"; if you tell us something has gone wrong, we treat it as one.

Making a complaint is free. It will never affect how we treat you or any application you make. This document explains how to complain, what happens next, how long it takes, and what to do if you are not happy with our answer.

Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration. The timelines in this document are sample figures, but the bank's systems use exactly these figures.

## What you can complain about

You can complain about anything to do with your banking at Kaskazi, for example:

- **Fees and charges**, such as a fee you think was charged wrongly or that does not match the "Fees and charges (tariff guide)".
- **Payments**, such as a transfer that has not arrived, money sent to the wrong person, or a standing order that did not run.
- **Cards**, such as a payment you do not recognise, a card that was declined, or a late replacement.
- **Loans**, such as how an application was handled or how a repayment was applied.
- **Accounts**, such as a delay in opening an account, or a monthly fee charged when it should have been waived.
- **Digital banking**, such as the app, online banking, USSD, or an answer from the Kaskazi assistant.
- **Agents**, such as an agent who asked for extra cash or for your PIN.
- **Service**, such as long queues, rude treatment or a promise not kept.
- **Your data**, such as how we have used your personal information.

## How to make a complaint

You can complain in whichever way suits you. Every route leads to the same complaints system, so your complaint is handled the same way whichever you choose.

- **Through the Kaskazi assistant.** In the app or online banking, tell the assistant what went wrong. It logs the complaint, gives you a reference and, if you prefer, passes you to a person. You can ask for a person at any time. Our team staffs the chat from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, every day, and aims to reply within 5 minutes during those hours.
- **In the app.** Open help, choose to make a complaint, and fill in the short form. You can attach screenshots or photos of receipts.
- **At any branch.** Tell any member of staff. Branches are open 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday. They are closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- **By phone.** Our contact centre is open 24 hours a day, every day. The phone number is not available in this demonstration.
- **In writing.** The postal and email addresses are not available in this demonstration.

**Someone can complain for you.** A family member, friend or adviser can complain on your behalf if you give us your permission. We will check with you before sharing account details with them.

## What to tell us when you complain

The more we know at the start, the faster we can put things right. When you complain, please tell us:

- **What went wrong**, in your own words.
- **When it happened**: the date and, if you know it, the time.
- **The amount**, if money is involved, and the transaction reference from the app or your confirmation message.
- **Who was involved**: the branch, the agent's name, or that it was the app or the assistant.
- **What you would like us to do**, for example refund a fee, return a payment, or explain a decision.

If you have screenshots, receipts or confirmation messages, keep them and attach them in the app or bring them to a branch. Never send us your PIN, password or one-time code; we will never ask for them.

## Your complaint reference number

Every complaint gets a reference number in the form "KZC-" followed by 6 digits, for example KZC-123456 (an illustration, not a real reference). You get it as soon as the complaint is logged, in the chat, in the app or on a slip at the branch.

Keep your reference. Quote it whenever you contact us about the complaint, so whoever you speak to can see its full history straight away. You can also follow the complaint's progress in the app using the reference.

## We acknowledge your complaint within 24 hours

We acknowledge every complaint within 24 hours of receiving it. The acknowledgement confirms your reference, what we have understood the complaint to be, and who is looking into it.

If you complained through the assistant, the app or a branch, you usually get your reference straight away; the acknowledgement follows within 24 hours. If we have misunderstood the complaint, reply to the acknowledgement and tell us.

## How long it takes to resolve a complaint

We aim to resolve complaints within 7 working days. A working day is Monday to Friday, except public holidays.

**Complex complaints.** Some complaints take longer, for example when we need information from another bank, a mobile money provider or an agent, or when several transactions are involved. We aim to resolve complex complaints within 30 days. If yours is complex, we will tell you why, give you a date to expect our answer, and keep you updated.

**Example.** If you complain on Monday 2 November 2026, we acknowledge it by Tuesday 3 November. We aim to resolve it by Wednesday 11 November (7 working days). If it is complex, we aim to resolve it by Wednesday 2 December 2026 (30 days).

**Many complaints are fixed at once.** If a fee was charged in error and we can see it straight away, we refund it while you are still talking to us.

## Card disputes and fraud: separate timelines

Complaints about card payments you do not recognise, or that went wrong, are handled as card disputes. They have their own timelines, because we may need to contact the merchant and the card network.

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Report a card payment you dispute | Within 60 days of the transaction date |
| We acknowledge the dispute | Within 24 hours |
| Provisional credit while we investigate, for disputes on amounts up to KES 20,000 | Within 10 working days |
| Investigation | Up to 45 days |

**Card fraud.** The most you lose for card fraud before you report it is KES 5,000, unless you were careless with your PIN or one-time code. After you report it, you lose nothing (KES 0). So report straight away, and block your card yourself in the app or through the assistant at any time.

If you are unhappy with how a dispute was handled, that is a complaint, and the complaint timelines and escalation steps in this document apply to it.

## Our answer to your complaint

When we have looked into your complaint, we give you our answer in writing, in the app or by message, and explain it in the chat or at the branch if you prefer. Our answer tells you:

- what we found,
- whether we agree that we got something wrong,
- what we are doing to put it right, and
- what you can do if you are not happy.

**Putting things right.** If we made a mistake, we aim to put you back in the position you would have been in. That can mean refunding a fee, correcting interest, reversing a charge, or fixing a record. We also tell you what we are changing so it does not happen again.

**Closing your complaint.** We close a complaint when you agree it has been resolved. If you do not agree, it stays open and you can take it further.

## If you are not happy with our answer: escalation

If you are not happy with how your complaint is being handled, or with our answer, you can ask for it to be reviewed at the next level. There are three levels:

1. **The agent or branch who handled it.** This is where most complaints are resolved. Ask the person handling your complaint to look again.
2. **The Customer Experience team.** If you are still not happy, ask for your complaint to go to the Customer Experience team. A different person reviews it from the start.
3. **The Head of Customer Experience.** If you are still not happy after the Customer Experience team's answer, ask for it to go to the Head of Customer Experience, who gives the bank's final answer.

To escalate, quote your reference number in the chat, in the app or at a branch, and say you want to take it to the next level. You do not have to give a reason, but telling us what you still disagree with helps.

## Taking your complaint outside the bank

In a real bank, details of the regulator's complaints service would be here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

If you have taken your complaint through all three escalation levels and are still not satisfied, our final answer will confirm that the bank's own process has ended.

## Keeping records of your complaint

Good records help resolve your complaint faster and help you if you need to escalate it.

**What to keep:**

- your complaint reference (KZC- followed by 6 digits),
- the date you complained and the dates of any replies,
- screenshots, confirmation messages, receipts and statements related to the complaint, and
- the names of anyone you spoke to at a branch.

**What we keep.** We record every complaint, the steps we took, our answer and the outcome, and link them to your reference. You can see the history of your complaint in the app. We keep your records for 7 years after your account is closed.

**Getting a copy of your information.** You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, including complaint records. We respond to these requests within 7 days.

## Complaints about the Kaskazi assistant

The Kaskazi assistant answers from the bank's documents and cites them, and it always asks you to confirm before changing anything. If it gives you a wrong answer, misunderstands you, or does something you did not expect, you can complain in the same way as about anything else.

- Tell the assistant, or ask it for a person, and say the answer was wrong.
- Our team can see the conversation, the documents the assistant used and what it did, so you do not have to repeat everything.
- If you acted on a wrong answer from the assistant and lost money, we look at it as a complaint and put things right where the bank was at fault.

## Getting help with a complaint

We want complaining to be easy for everyone.

- **Language.** You can complain in English or Swahili.
- **Accessibility.** If you have a disability or need help, for example with reading or writing, tell us and we will adjust how we handle your complaint, such as explaining our answer in person at a branch.
- **Difficult times.** If your complaint is linked to a bereavement, financial hardship or fraud, tell us. The assistant passes these to a person, and we handle them with extra care.
