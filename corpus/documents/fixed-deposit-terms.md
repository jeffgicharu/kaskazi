# Kaskazi Fixed Deposit: terms and conditions

## About the Kaskazi Fixed Deposit

The Kaskazi Fixed Deposit lets you put a sum of money away for a fixed term of 3, 6 or 12 months at a fixed interest rate. You know from the start what rate you will earn, and Kaskazi Bank pays all the interest when the term ends.

In return, the money is meant to stay put for the whole term. You can take it out early, but you lose half of the interest earned so far, and you can't take out only part of it.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and every figure here is sample data for a demonstration.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit key facts

| Feature | Kaskazi Fixed Deposit |
|---|---|
| Minimum deposit | KES 50,000 |
| Terms | 3, 6 or 12 months |
| Interest rate, 3 months | 8.0% a year |
| Interest rate, 6 months | 8.75% a year |
| Interest rate, 12 months | 9.5% a year |
| When interest is paid | At maturity |
| Withholding tax on interest | 15.0% |
| At maturity | Rolls over automatically for the same term, unless you tell Kaskazi Bank otherwise at least 2 working days before maturity |
| Early withdrawal | Allowed; you lose half of the interest earned so far |
| Partial withdrawal | Not allowed |

## Opening a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit

You can open a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit in the app or at any branch. The money comes from your Kaskazi account, so you need a Kaskazi account with enough money in it first.

The minimum deposit is KES 50,000. You choose the amount and the term (3, 6 or 12 months) when you open it, and both are fixed from then on.

You can have more than one Kaskazi Fixed Deposit at the same time, each with its own amount, term and maturity date.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit terms and interest rates

| Term | Interest rate |
|---|---|
| 3 months | 8.0% a year |
| 6 months | 8.75% a year |
| 12 months | 9.5% a year |

The rates are yearly rates. A 3-month deposit earns 8.0% a year for 3 months, not 8.0% for the 3 months.

The rate on the day you open your Kaskazi Fixed Deposit is fixed for the whole term. If Kaskazi Bank's rates go up or down during the term, your deposit's rate does not change.

## How Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest is worked out

Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest is simple interest for the number of days in the term:

Interest = amount deposited × yearly rate × number of days ÷ 365

The days are counted from the day the deposit starts up to the maturity date. Interest is not added to the deposit during the term, so it does not earn interest on itself until the deposit rolls over.

## When Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest is paid

All the interest on a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit is paid at maturity, at the end of the term. Nothing is paid monthly.

At maturity, withholding tax is deducted and the interest after tax is either paid out to your Kaskazi account or rolled over with the deposit, depending on what you have chosen.

## Withholding tax on Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest

Withholding tax of 15.0% is deducted from Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest before it is paid. Kaskazi Bank pays the tax to the tax authority on your behalf, and your maturity advice shows the interest earned, the tax deducted and the interest paid to you.

Tax is only ever deducted from interest. It never reduces the amount you deposited.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit interest examples

These examples start on Monday 2 November 2026. Figures are rounded to the nearest cent.

### Example 1: KES 100,000 for each term

This is what a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit of KES 100,000 opened on Monday 2 November 2026 earns for each term.

| Term | Maturity | Days | Interest earned | Tax at 15.0% | Interest after tax |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 months at 8.0% a year | Tuesday 2 February 2027 | 92 | KES 2,016.44 | KES 302.47 | KES 1,713.97 |
| 6 months at 8.75% a year | Monday 3 May 2027 | 182 | KES 4,363.01 | KES 654.45 | KES 3,708.56 |
| 12 months at 9.5% a year | Tuesday 2 November 2027 | 365 | KES 9,500.00 | KES 1,425.00 | KES 8,075.00 |

For the 3-month deposit: KES 100,000 × 8.0% × 92 ÷ 365 = KES 2,016.44.

The 6-month deposit would end on Sunday 2 May 2027, so it matures on the next working day, Monday 3 May, and earns interest for the extra day.

### Example 2: KES 200,000 for 12 months

You put KES 200,000 in a 12-month Kaskazi Fixed Deposit at 9.5% a year, from 2 November 2026 to 2 November 2027 (365 days).

- Interest earned: KES 200,000 × 9.5% × 365 ÷ 365 = KES 19,000.00
- Withholding tax at 15.0%: KES 2,850.00
- Interest after tax: KES 16,150.00

At maturity you have KES 216,150.00: your KES 200,000 back plus KES 16,150 interest after tax.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposit maturity on a weekend or public holiday

A working day is Monday to Friday, except public holidays.

If your Kaskazi Fixed Deposit's maturity date falls on a day that is not a working day, it matures on the next working day instead. It earns interest at the same rate for the extra days.

## Automatic rollover of a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit

Unless you tell Kaskazi Bank otherwise, your Kaskazi Fixed Deposit rolls over automatically at maturity. It starts again for the same term, at the rate for that term on the rollover date.

The amount that rolls over is your original deposit plus the interest after tax, so the new deposit earns interest on the interest from the first term.

The rate on the rollover date may be different from your original rate. If Kaskazi Bank's rate for your term has changed, the new deposit uses the new rate. Kaskazi Bank sends you a message before maturity to remind you that the deposit is about to roll over.

### Rollover example

Your KES 200,000 12-month Kaskazi Fixed Deposit at 9.5% a year matures on 2 November 2027 with KES 16,150 interest after tax, and you have given no instructions. KES 216,150 rolls over for another 12 months, at the 12-month rate on 2 November 2027. If that rate is still 9.5% a year, the new deposit earns 9.5% a year on KES 216,150.

## How to stop a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit rolling over

To stop the rollover or change what happens at maturity, tell Kaskazi Bank at least 2 working days before the maturity date. You can do this in the app, through the Kaskazi assistant or at any branch.

You can choose to:

- have the whole amount, deposit plus interest after tax, paid into your Kaskazi account;
- have the interest paid out and the original amount roll over; or
- roll over for a different term (3, 6 or 12 months) at that term's rate on the rollover date.

### Rollover deadline example

Your 3-month Kaskazi Fixed Deposit matures on Tuesday 2 February 2027. Two working days before is Friday 29 January 2027, because Saturday 30 and Sunday 31 January are not working days and Monday 1 February is only one working day before.

So you must give your instruction by the end of Friday 29 January. An instruction given on Monday 1 February is too late, and the deposit rolls over.

### If you miss the rollover deadline

If your Kaskazi Fixed Deposit has already rolled over and you need the money, you can still break the new deposit early. You lose half of the interest earned on the new deposit, which is very little in the first few days.

For example, if KES 216,150 rolls over for 12 months at 9.5% a year and you break it 1 day later, it has earned KES 56.26. You lose half, KES 28.13, and receive the rest after tax. Your KES 216,150 is returned in full.

## Breaking a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit early

You can take the money out of a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit before maturity. If you do, you lose half of the interest earned so far. Kaskazi Bank pays you the other half, after withholding tax, together with the full amount you deposited.

You never lose any of the money you deposited by breaking a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit early.

You can ask to break a deposit in the app, through the Kaskazi assistant or at any branch. The money is paid into your Kaskazi account.

### Early withdrawal example

You put KES 500,000 in a 12-month Kaskazi Fixed Deposit at 9.5% a year on 2 November 2026. On Friday 2 April 2027, after 151 days, you need the money.

- Interest earned so far: KES 500,000 × 9.5% × 151 ÷ 365 = KES 19,650.68
- You lose half: KES 9,825.34
- Interest paid before tax (the other half): KES 9,825.34
- Withholding tax at 15.0%: KES 1,473.80
- Interest paid to you after tax: KES 8,351.54

You receive KES 508,351.54: your KES 500,000 plus KES 8,351.54 interest after tax.

If you had kept the deposit for the full 12 months, the interest after tax would have been KES 40,375.00.

## No partial withdrawals from a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit

You can't take out part of a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit. To get any money out before maturity, you must break the whole deposit, and the early withdrawal rule applies to all of it.

If you think you might need part of your money early, consider splitting it across several deposits. Each one must be at least KES 50,000.

For example, instead of one deposit of KES 300,000 for 12 months, you could open three deposits of KES 100,000. If you need KES 100,000 after 151 days, you break only one of them. That one earns KES 3,930.14 so far, you lose half, and you receive KES 1,670.31 interest after tax on it. The other two keep earning 9.5% a year untouched.

## Adding money to a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit

You can't add money to a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit during its term. To deposit more, open a new Kaskazi Fixed Deposit of at least KES 50,000.

At maturity, you can roll over a larger amount by asking Kaskazi Bank, at least 2 working days before maturity, to add money from your Kaskazi account to the new deposit.

## Kaskazi Fixed Deposits and dormancy

A Kaskazi Fixed Deposit is not treated as inactive during the term you chose when you opened it, because leaving it alone is the point.

An automatic rollover, though, is done by Kaskazi Bank, so it does not count as a transaction started by you. If your deposit has rolled over automatically and you start no transaction on it for 12 months after its first maturity date, it becomes dormant. Giving a maturity instruction, breaking the deposit or adding money to it at rollover all count as transactions started by you.

A dormant Kaskazi Fixed Deposit keeps rolling over and earning interest. To take the money out, you first reactivate it at any branch with your ID or in the app with a selfie check. Reactivation is free.

## Changes to Kaskazi Fixed Deposit rates and terms

Kaskazi Bank may change its Kaskazi Fixed Deposit rates and these terms at any time. A change never affects the rate of a deposit already running; it applies to new deposits and to deposits when they next roll over.
