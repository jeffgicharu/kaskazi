# Kaskazi Personal Loan: terms and conditions

These terms apply to every Kaskazi Personal Loan from 1 October 2026. Together with your loan offer, they form the agreement between you and Kaskazi Bank Limited. Please read them before you accept an offer. If your offer and these terms ever disagree, the offer applies, because it was written for your loan.

Kaskazi Bank is invented, and these terms were written for a demonstration of a banking assistant. They are not a real contract and no real loan can be taken out under them.

## 1. Words used in the Kaskazi Personal Loan terms

In these Kaskazi Personal Loan terms:

- **we**, **us** and **our** mean Kaskazi Bank Limited.
- **you** and **your** mean the person who borrows. A Kaskazi Personal Loan is taken in one person's name.
- **loan amount** means the amount you borrow, as shown in your offer, before any fees or insurance are deducted.
- **net monthly pay** means the pay you receive each month after tax and the other deductions your employer makes by law. How we treat loan repayments your employer deducts is explained in section 3.
- **Kaskazi Base Rate** means the reference rate Kaskazi Bank sets and publishes. It is 11.5% a year on the date these terms take effect.
- **instalment** means each monthly payment you make towards the loan.
- **instalment date** means the day of the month on which your instalment is due, as shown in your offer.
- **working day** means Monday to Friday, except public holidays.
- **your account** means the Kaskazi Everyday Account into which the loan is paid and from which instalments are collected.

## 3. Who can take a Kaskazi Personal Loan

You can apply for a Kaskazi Personal Loan only if you meet every condition in this section. Meeting them all does not mean we must lend to you.

### Age

You must be at least 21 years old when you apply. You must also be no older than 65 when the loan ends, which means your last instalment must be due before your 66th birthday. If you are close to that age, the longest term we can offer you is the time left until then, even if it is less than 60 months. We check your age from your national ID card or passport.

### Net monthly pay

Your net monthly pay must be at least KES 25,000. If your pay varies from month to month, for example because of overtime or commission, we use the pay you can show regularly over your latest payslips, not the highest month.

### Salary history

You need at least 3 months of salary history. Either:

- your salary has been paid into a Kaskazi account for at least the last 3 months, or
- your salary is paid into an account at another bank, and you give us that bank's statements for the last 6 months, showing your salary arriving.

If you have recently moved your salary to Kaskazi and have fewer than 3 salaries with us, you can make up the history with statements from your previous bank covering the last 6 months.

### Affordability

All your monthly loan repayments, including the Kaskazi Personal Loan instalment you are applying for, must come to no more than 40% of your net monthly pay. We count repayments on loans with us and with any other lender, including loans your employer deducts from your payslip. For a loan your employer deducts, we add the deduction back to work out your net monthly pay and then count it as a repayment, so that every loan is treated the same way. Our guide "How we decide on a loan" gives worked examples.

### Account and documents

You must have, or open, a Kaskazi Everyday Account in your own name to receive the loan and pay the instalments. You must give us your 3 latest payslips with no gaps, your national ID card or passport, your KRA PIN and, if your salary is paid elsewhere, 6 months of statements from that bank. Every document must meet the standards set out in our guide "Applying for a loan: steps and documents".

## 4. How we decide on a Kaskazi Personal Loan application

A person in our lending team decides every Kaskazi Personal Loan application. The banking assistant can explain these terms, give you a quote and prepare your application, but it cannot approve or decline a loan, and nothing it tells you is an offer or a promise to lend.

Before deciding, we check your identity, your documents, your pay, your existing loans and your record with a credit reference bureau. We aim to decide within 2 working days of receiving all your documents and finding them in order. If we need another document or a clearer copy, the 2 working days start again from when we receive it.

We may:

- approve the amount and term you asked for
- offer a smaller amount or a different term that fits the affordability rule, which you are free to accept or turn down
- decline the application

If we decline, we tell you the main reason, unless the law prevents us or telling you would help someone commit fraud. You can ask for the decision to be reviewed. Our guide "How we decide on a loan" explains how.

## 5. Your Kaskazi Personal Loan offer and accepting it

If we approve your application, we send you an offer in the app. The offer shows:

- the arrangement fee and the credit life insurance, in shillings

Read the offer carefully. You accept it in the app or at a branch. The agreement starts when you accept. The offer is based on the information you gave us; if anything material changes before the money is paid, for example you lose your job, you must tell us and we may withdraw the offer.

## 6. Cancelling a Kaskazi Personal Loan before the money is paid

You can cancel your Kaskazi Personal Loan application at any time before the money is paid into your account, at no cost. Nothing has been deducted yet, so there is nothing to refund.

- **Before you accept the offer**, simply do not accept it, or ask us to close the application in the app, at a branch or through the assistant.
- **After you accept the offer**, the money is normally paid the same working day, so tell us straight away. If we receive your request before the payment has been made, we stop it and close the application, with no fee.

Once the money has been paid into your account, the loan can no longer be cancelled. You can still repay it in full early, as section 13 explains. In that case the arrangement fee and the credit life insurance are not refunded, and the early repayment fee applies because you would be repaying within the first 12 months.

## 7. Paying out your Kaskazi Personal Loan

We pay your Kaskazi Personal Loan into your Kaskazi Everyday Account the same working day the loan is approved and you accept the offer. If you accept on a day that is not a working day, or too late in the day for us to make the payment, we pay it on the next working day.

We deduct the arrangement fee and the credit life insurance before paying the loan to you, so the amount you receive is less than the loan amount. You repay the full loan amount, with interest.

We pay the loan only into a Kaskazi Everyday Account in your name. We do not pay it to another person, to another bank or to a mobile money wallet. Once it is in your account, you can move it as you would any other money.

## 8. Fees deducted from a Kaskazi Personal Loan at payout

### Arrangement fee

The arrangement fee is 2.5% of the loan amount, with a minimum of KES 2,000. The minimum applies to any loan of less than KES 80,000. The fee covers the work of assessing and setting up your loan. It is not refunded if you repay early.

### Credit life insurance premium

The credit life insurance premium is 0.5% of the loan amount for each year of the term. A part year counts as a full year, so a 6-month loan is charged for 1 year, a 13-month or 18-month loan for 2 years, and a 60-month loan for 5 years. The premium is paid once, at the start, and covers the whole term. Section 14 explains the cover.

## 9. How interest on a Kaskazi Personal Loan is calculated

Interest on a Kaskazi Personal Loan is charged at 15.5% a year on the reducing balance. Each month, interest is the monthly rate (15.5% divided by 12) applied to the balance you owe at the start of that month. Your instalment pays that month's interest first, and the rest reduces the balance. As the balance falls, the interest part of each instalment falls and the part that repays the loan grows.

Your instalment is worked out so that equal monthly instalments clear the loan by the end of the term:

instalment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r) to the power of −n)

where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. The instalment is rounded to the nearest shilling. Because of that rounding, your final instalment may be slightly higher or lower than the others, so that the loan is cleared exactly.

For example, a loan of KES 500,000 over 36 months has an instalment of KES 17,455, and 36 instalments come to KES 628,380.

Interest is charged on the full loan amount, including the part used to pay the fee and the insurance, because that is the amount you borrowed. If you repay between instalment dates, interest is added for the days since your last instalment.

We do not charge a higher rate on overdue amounts. If an instalment is missed, interest continues at your loan rate on the balance you owe, and the late payment fee in section 12 applies.

## 10. Changes to the Kaskazi Base Rate and your instalment

Your Kaskazi Personal Loan rate is the Kaskazi Base Rate plus a margin of 4.0 percentage points. The margin is fixed for the whole life of your loan. The Kaskazi Base Rate can change.

- If the Kaskazi Base Rate goes **up**, your rate goes up by the same amount and your instalment rises.
- If the Kaskazi Base Rate goes **down**, your rate goes down by the same amount and your instalment falls.

When the rate changes, we recalculate your instalment on the balance you then owe, over the months left in your term, so your loan still ends on the same date. We write to you in the app before the change takes effect, telling you the new rate, your new instalment and the date it starts. Instalments already paid are not affected.

If your new instalment would be hard for you to manage, tell us. We will look at what we can do, such as a longer term within the limits in section 16. You can also repay the loan early under section 13.

A change in the Kaskazi Base Rate is the only reason your interest rate can change. We do not change your margin.

## 11. Kaskazi Personal Loan instalments and instalment dates

You repay a Kaskazi Personal Loan in monthly instalments, collected from your Kaskazi Everyday Account on your instalment date. We set the instalment date to fall shortly after your usual pay day, and your first instalment is due about one month after the loan is paid out. Your offer shows the exact dates.

- Make sure your account has enough money in it on each instalment date.
- If an instalment date is not a working day, we collect on the next working day, and this does not count as late.
- If your pay day changes, for example because you change employer, you can ask us to move your instalment date. Moving the date may change the interest slightly, and we show you the effect before you agree.
- You can pay more than the instalment at any time. Anything extra is treated as an early repayment under section 13.

We send you a reminder in the app before each instalment date. You can see your balance, your instalment history and the date of your next instalment in the app at no charge. A printed statement costs KES 300 at a branch.

## 12. Late and missed Kaskazi Personal Loan instalments

If an instalment is not paid in full on its instalment date, it is a missed instalment.

- A late payment fee of KES 500 is charged for each missed instalment. It is charged once for that instalment, however long the instalment stays unpaid.
- We try to collect the missed amount again when money next comes into your Kaskazi Everyday Account. If only part of the amount is there, we collect what we can and the rest later.
- Interest continues at your loan rate on what you owe. We do not charge a higher rate.
- We contact you to find out what has happened and to agree how the arrears will be paid.

Missed instalments are recorded on your loan and are reported to credit reference bureaus, as section 18 explains. A missed instalment can make it harder for you to borrow in future, from us or anyone else.

If you know an instalment will be late, tell us before the instalment date. We cannot always waive the fee, but we can often agree a plan that avoids further missed payments.

## 13. Repaying a Kaskazi Personal Loan early

You can repay all or part of your Kaskazi Personal Loan early at any time.

### The early repayment fee

During the first 12 months after the loan is paid out, an early repayment fee of 2.0% of the amount you repay early applies. After the first 12 months, there is no early repayment fee. The fee is added to the amount you pay; it does not reduce the amount that goes off your balance. Paying your normal instalments is never an early repayment.

### Repaying in full

Ask us for a settlement figure in the app, at a branch or through the assistant. The figure shows the balance, interest up to the day you repay and, in the first 12 months, the early repayment fee.

For example, on a loan of KES 500,000 over 36 months with an instalment of KES 17,455, the balance on the day the 6th instalment is paid is KES 431,852. Settling that day costs KES 431,852 plus an early repayment fee of KES 8,637, a total of KES 440,489. On the same loan, the balance on the day the 14th instalment is paid is KES 332,429, and settling that day carries no fee.

### Repaying part of the loan

If you pay a lump sum, it reduces your balance straight away. In the first 12 months the fee is 2.0% of the lump sum, so a part payment of KES 100,000 carries a fee of KES 2,000. You then choose one of these:

- **keep the same instalment** and finish sooner, or
- **keep the same end date** and pay a lower instalment.

For example, on the KES 500,000 loan above, a part payment of KES 100,000 on the day the 6th instalment is paid takes the balance from KES 431,852 to KES 331,852. Keeping the same end date, the remaining 30 instalments fall to KES 13,413. Keeping the instalment of KES 17,455, the loan is cleared in 22 more instalments instead of 30, with a smaller final one.

### What is not refunded

The arrangement fee and the credit life insurance premium are not refunded when you repay early. When the loan is fully repaid, the credit life cover ends.

## 14. Credit life insurance on a Kaskazi Personal Loan

Every Kaskazi Personal Loan includes credit life insurance. The premium, 0.5% of the loan amount for each year of the term with part years counted as full years, is deducted when the loan is paid out.

### What the cover does

If you die before the loan is repaid, the insurance pays off the balance you owed on the date of your death. Your family and your estate do not have to repay that balance.

### What the cover does not do

- It does not cover losing your job, illness, or a drop in your income. Section 16 explains what to do if your income falls.
- It does not pay instalments that were already overdue before the date of death, or late payment fees. Those are claimed from your estate.
- It ends when the loan is fully repaid, whether at the end of the term or early.

The insurer's policy summary, given with your offer, sets out the cover and any exclusions in full. In this demonstration there is no real insurer and no real policy.

## 15. If a Kaskazi Personal Loan borrower dies

If a borrower dies, we ask a family member or the person handling the estate to tell us as soon as they can, at a branch or through the assistant, and to give us a copy of the death certificate.

From the day we are told:

- we stop collecting instalments and stop charging late payment fees on the loan
- we make the credit life insurance claim; the family does not have to deal with the insurer
- any instalments collected after the date of death are refunded to the estate once the claim is paid

When the insurance pays, the loan is closed. If any overdue instalments from before the date of death remain, we explain them to the person handling the estate and agree with them how they will be settled. We treat bereaved families with patience and do not pursue them personally for the loan.

## 16. If you lose your job or your income falls

Credit life insurance does not cover job loss, so if you lose your job, are suspended without pay, or your income falls, your Kaskazi Personal Loan instalments are still due. Please tell us as soon as you know, ideally before you miss an instalment. The earlier we know, the more we can do.

A member of our team will look at your situation with you. Depending on what has happened, we may agree:

- a reduced instalment for a period, with the difference repaid later
- a longer term, which lowers your instalment; the total term cannot go beyond 60 months, and you must still be no older than 65 when the loan ends
- a plan to clear any arrears in manageable amounts
- using any final pay, severance pay or savings to reduce or repay the loan, if you choose to

Any change is agreed with you in writing, and a person makes the decision; the assistant can take down your details and pass them on, but it cannot agree a change. Interest continues on what you owe during any arrangement, and a longer term means you pay more interest overall.

If you change jobs rather than losing one, your loan carries on as normal. Tell us your new pay day so that we can move your instalment date, and we suggest moving your salary to your Kaskazi Everyday Account so that instalments are collected without trouble.

## 17. Financial difficulty

If you are struggling to pay your Kaskazi Personal Loan for any reason, such as illness, a family emergency or other debts, talk to us. Being in difficulty is not a failing, and asking for help early protects your credit record far better than missing payments.

When you tell us you are in difficulty, we:

- listen and ask about your income and essential spending, without judgement
- give you time to think and do not pressure you to agree on the spot
- offer the options in section 16 where they suit you
- tell you clearly what any arrangement will cost and how it affects your credit record

You can ask a family member or another person you trust to speak to us on your behalf, if you tell us you agree to that.

## 18. Default and collections: how we treat you

### When a Kaskazi Personal Loan is in default

Your Kaskazi Personal Loan is in default if instalments stay unpaid after we have written to you about the arrears and given you time to pay or to agree a plan, and you have not done either. A loan can also be in default if we find that information you gave us to get the loan was false.

### What happens in default

If your loan is in default, we write to you before we take further steps. We may then:

- ask you to repay the whole balance, not only the arrears
- use money in your other Kaskazi accounts to reduce what you owe, after telling you that we will
- report the default to credit reference bureaus
- ask a collection agent to contact you for us, or, as a last resort, recover the debt through the courts

We will always try to agree a plan with you first. Even after a loan is in default, you can contact us to agree a way to repay.

### How we and anyone acting for us will treat you

- We contact you at reasonable times and in a respectful way.
- We never threaten you, shame you, or use abusive language.
- We never tell your employer, family, friends or neighbours about your debt to pressure you. We only speak to someone else if you have agreed to it.
- We never post about your debt on social media or anywhere public.
- We give you clear information about what you owe and how it was worked out.
- Any collection agent working for us must follow these same standards. If they do not, tell us and we will act.

### Credit reference bureaus

The law requires banks to share information about loans with credit reference bureaus, including loans that are being repaid on time and loans that are in arrears or in default. Before we report a missed payment or a default, we write to tell you and give you the chance to pay or to correct the information. If you think information we have reported about you is wrong, tell us and we will check it and correct it if it is.

## 19. Information you give us about your Kaskazi Personal Loan

Everything you tell us when you apply must be true and complete, and every document must be genuine. If we find that a document has been altered, or that information was deliberately false, we may decline the application or, if the loan has been paid, treat it as in default under section 18.

Please tell us promptly if your name, phone number or employer changes, or if your salary is no longer paid into your Kaskazi account. We keep records of your loan for 7 years after it is closed, and you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you; we respond within 7 days.

We may change these terms, for example to reflect a change in the law or to make them clearer. We tell you before any change that affects your loan takes effect. A change to these terms cannot change your margin or the fees already deducted from your loan. Changes to the Kaskazi Base Rate are covered by section 10.

If you are unhappy with anything about your Kaskazi Personal Loan, tell us in the app, through the assistant or at any branch. We give every complaint a reference, which starts KZC- followed by 6 digits. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 7 working days. A complex complaint can take up to 30 days, and we keep you updated if it does.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can ask for your complaint to go to the Customer Experience team, and then to the Head of Customer Experience. In a real bank, details of an outside complaints service would appear here. Kaskazi Bank is invented and is not regulated by anyone.

If something the assistant tells you seems to differ from these terms or your offer, the terms and your offer apply, and please tell us so we can correct it.
