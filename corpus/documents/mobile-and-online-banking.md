# The Kaskazi app and online banking

## About the Kaskazi app and online banking

The Kaskazi app (for smartphones) and Kaskazi online banking (in a web browser) let you run your accounts without visiting a branch. Both have the same features and the same security, and both include the Kaskazi assistant, which answers questions and can carry out some tasks for you. For phones without internet, there is also USSD banking.

Using the app, online banking and Kaskazi's part of USSD costs nothing (KES 0). You only pay the normal fee for any payment you make, such as a mobile money transfer, and those fees are the same in every channel. Kaskazi Bank is invented for a demonstration; the app store listing and the online banking address are not available in this demonstration.

## What you can do in the app and online banking

In the Kaskazi app and online banking you can:

- **See your money.** Check balances and transactions on all your Kaskazi accounts, cards and loans.
- **Move money.** Transfer between your own accounts and to other Kaskazi customers for free; send instant transfers and next-day transfers (EFT) to other banks; send money to mobile money wallets; and send money abroad.
- **Pay.** Pay bills (KES 30 each) and school fees (KES 0); set up, change and cancel standing orders.
- **Save.** Open a Kaskazi Goal Saver, set up to 5 goals, and open or manage a Kaskazi Fixed Deposit, including telling us not to roll it over.
- **Borrow.** Apply for a Kaskazi Personal Loan, a Kaskazi Salary Advance or a Kaskazi Credit Card, and upload the documents we ask for.
- **Control your cards.** Block and unblock a card, report it lost or stolen, and order a replacement.
- **Get statements.** Download statements for free.
- **Open accounts.** Open a new Kaskazi account in about 10 minutes.
- **Keep your details up to date.** Update your details and refresh your identity documents, which we ask for every 3 years.
- **Reactivate a dormant account** with a selfie check, free of charge.
- **Get help.** Ask the Kaskazi assistant, ask for a person, or make a complaint and follow its progress.

A few things still need a branch: payments above the daily digital limit of KES 500,000, same-day high-value transfers (RTGS), which start at KES 1,000,000, printed statements, and cash.

## Registering for the app and online banking

You can register for the Kaskazi app whether or not you already bank with us.

**If you already have a Kaskazi account**, download the app, choose to register and enter your national ID or passport number and your 13-digit account number. We send a one-time code to the phone number registered on your account. Enter it, take a selfie so we can match you to your ID, and create your sign-in details.

**If you are new to Kaskazi**, you can open an account in the app in about 10 minutes. You need:

- your national ID card or passport,
- your KRA PIN,
- a phone number registered in your name, and
- a selfie, taken in the app.

Students opening a Kaskazi Student Account also need a valid student ID.

**Online banking.** Once registered in the app, you can sign in to online banking in a web browser with the same details. The online banking address is not available in this demonstration.

**One profile, one phone number.** Codes always go to the phone number registered on your profile. If you change your number, update it at a branch or in the app before you switch, so codes keep reaching you.

## Signing in to the app and online banking

You sign in to the Kaskazi app with your app PIN or password. On a phone that supports it, you can also turn on fingerprint or face sign-in in the app's security settings, which is quicker and still secure.

**New phone or browser.** The first time you sign in on a new phone or computer, we send a one-time code to your registered phone number to confirm it is you.

**Choosing a good PIN or password.** Avoid your birth year, your ID number, your phone number or simple patterns. Use a PIN or password that you do not use for anything else, and never write it down next to your phone.

**Never share your sign-in details.** Kaskazi staff, agents and the assistant will never ask for your app PIN, password or a one-time code. Anyone who asks is not genuine.

## One-time codes

A one-time code is a short code we send by SMS to your registered phone number to confirm that you, and not someone else, are making a change. Each code is valid for 5 minutes and works only once.

**When we send a one-time code.** We send one when you:

- sign in on a new phone or browser,
- register for the app,
- unblock a card you blocked yourself,
- reset your PIN or password after being locked out, or
- make other sensitive changes, such as changing your phone number.

**If your code has expired.** After 5 minutes the code stops working. Ask for a new one on the same screen. Only the newest code works.

**If you get a code you did not ask for.** Someone may be trying to get into your account. Do not share the code with anyone, even someone who says they are from Kaskazi. Tell us straight away through the assistant, the app or a branch.

**Where to enter it.** Only type a one-time code into the Kaskazi app or online banking screen that asked for it. Never read it out to a caller, type it into a chat or enter it on a website you reached from a link.

## Automatic sign-out after 5 minutes

For your security, the Kaskazi app and online banking sign you out automatically after 5 minutes without activity. This protects your accounts if you leave your phone or computer unattended.

- **What happens.** After 5 minutes with no taps, typing or scrolling, the session ends and you return to the sign-in screen.
- **Unfinished payments are not sent.** A payment you had not confirmed before the timeout is not made. Sign in again and start it again.
- **Confirmed payments are safe.** A payment you had already confirmed carries on, and appears in your transactions.
- **Sign out yourself** when you finish, especially on a shared computer, and close the browser.

## Locked out after 5 failed sign-in attempts

If the wrong PIN or password is entered 5 times, your app and online banking access is locked. This stops someone guessing their way into your account. Your accounts and cards keep working: only digital access is locked.

**How to unlock it in the app:**

1. On the sign-in screen, choose to reset your PIN or password.
2. Enter your national ID or passport number.
3. Enter the one-time code we send to your registered phone number. It is valid for 5 minutes.
4. Take a selfie so we can match you to your ID.
5. Create a new PIN or password and sign in.

**If you cannot unlock it in the app,** for example because you no longer have your registered phone, visit any Kaskazi branch with your national ID card or passport. Branches are open 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday, and 9:00 am to 12:00 noon on Saturday, and are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

**If the failed attempts were not you,** someone may know or be guessing your details. Tell us straight away. You can still block your card yourself through the assistant if you are worried about it.

## The Kaskazi assistant

The Kaskazi assistant is a chat assistant built into the app and online banking. It is powered by artificial intelligence (AI), and it is available 24 hours a day, every day. You can type questions in your own words, in English or Swahili.

**What the assistant can do:**

- **Answer questions** about Kaskazi's accounts, cards, loans, fees, interest rates, limits and services, using the bank's own documents.
- **Look at your accounts** once you are signed in: balances, recent transactions, card status, loan balances and payment status.
- **Explain a charge**, for example why a fee appeared on your statement and which tariff line it comes from.
- **Do some tasks for you**, such as blocking or unblocking a card, setting up a transfer for you to approve, telling us not to roll over a fixed deposit, or logging a complaint.
- **Hand you to a person** whenever you ask, or when your situation needs one.

**What the assistant does not do.** It does not make lending decisions; applications are assessed by the bank. It does not give personal investment or tax advice. And it never asks for your PIN, password, one-time code or full card number.

## The assistant always asks before changing anything

The Kaskazi assistant never changes anything on your accounts without your clear agreement. Before any action, such as blocking a card, sending a payment, or changing a standing order, it shows you exactly what it is about to do and asks you to confirm.

**What a confirmation looks like.** The assistant shows a summary, for example: "Block your Kaskazi Debit Card now. While it is blocked, all payments on it will be declined, including ones you set up before. Confirm or cancel?" The card is identified only by the last few digits of its number. Nothing happens until you tap confirm.

**For payments,** the confirmation shows the recipient's name, the amount, the fee and when the money will arrive, exactly as the app's own payment screen does. The same limits apply: the daily digital limit of KES 500,000 and KES 50,000 to a new recipient in the first 24 hours.

**Some actions need a one-time code too.** Unblocking a card needs a one-time code sent to your registered phone number. You enter it on the app's own screen, not in the chat.

**If you change your mind,** cancel at the confirmation step. If you have already confirmed a payment, tell the assistant or ask for a person straight away; see "Transfers and payments: limits, fees and timings" for what can be done.

## Asking for a person

You can ask the Kaskazi assistant for a person at any time, about anything. Type "I want to talk to a person" or similar, and the assistant passes your conversation to our customer team with a summary, so you do not have to repeat yourself.

**When a person answers.** Our team staffs the chat from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, every day. During those hours we aim to reply within 5 minutes. Outside staffed hours, your conversation waits in the queue and a person replies when the team is next in.

**Card blocks never wait.** You can block your card yourself at any time, in the app or through the assistant, without waiting for a person.

**When the assistant hands over without being asked.** The assistant also passes the conversation to a person when it is not confident it has the right answer, and in sensitive situations: possible fraud or a scam, a bereavement, money worries or financial hardship, or when identity checks have failed more than once.

## How the assistant uses the bank's documents

The Kaskazi assistant answers questions from Kaskazi Bank's own published documents, such as this one, the tariff guide and the product terms. It does not make up figures.

- **Answers cite their sources.** Each answer names the document, and the section, it came from, so you can open it and check.
- **If the documents do not cover it,** the assistant says so rather than guessing, and offers to pass you to a person.
- **Your account information** comes from your accounts, not from the documents, and the assistant tells you which is which.
- **It can make mistakes.** If something does not look right, check the cited document or ask for a person. The documents and your statement are what count.

## What the assistant will never ask you for

The Kaskazi assistant, like every member of Kaskazi staff, will never ask you for:

- your card PIN, app PIN or password,
- a one-time code (you enter codes on the app's own screen, not in the chat),
- your full card number, or the security code on the back of the card, or
- to move money to a "safe account" or to pay a fee to unlock a refund.

If anyone in a chat, call or message asks for these, stop and tell us through the assistant or at a branch.

## USSD banking for phones without internet

USSD banking lets you use your Kaskazi account from any mobile phone, including phones without internet or a data bundle. You dial a short code from the phone number registered on your account and follow the menu. The USSD code is not available in this demonstration.

**What it costs.** Kaskazi Bank charges nothing for USSD sessions (KES 0). Your mobile network may charge for the session under its own terms. Payments made over USSD cost the same as in the app; for example, a mobile money transfer of KES 5,000 costs KES 35.

**What you can do on USSD:**

- check your balance and see a mini statement,
- transfer between your own accounts and to other Kaskazi accounts,
- send money to a mobile money wallet,
- pay bills and school fees, and
- block your card.

**Limits and security.** USSD uses your registered phone number and your PIN. The daily digital limit of KES 500,000 and the new-recipient limit of KES 50,000 in the first 24 hours apply, just as in the app. Never dial a code someone sends you in a message claiming to be from Kaskazi.

## Statements in the app

You can download a statement for any Kaskazi account in the app or online banking, free of charge (KES 0), as often as you like.

**How to get one.** Open the account, choose statements, pick the dates you need, and download the statement as a file you can save, print or share, for example with a landlord or an employer.

**Printed statements.** A statement printed at a branch costs KES 300 per statement. Business Account holders get a monthly statement free in the app, or can choose printed monthly statements for KES 300 a month.

**Statements for a loan application.** When you apply for a Kaskazi loan, we can see your Kaskazi statements directly, so you do not need to download them. Statements from another bank must cover 6 months, with every page included.

**Mini statements.** For a quick look at recent transactions, the app shows them straight away. At a Kaskazi agent, a mini statement costs KES 15.

## Card controls in the app

You can manage your Kaskazi debit and credit cards yourself in the app, in online banking or through the assistant, at any time of day.

- **Temporary block.** If you have misplaced your card, block it. Payments are declined while it is blocked, including subscriptions and other payments you set up before the block.
- **Unblock.** If you find the card, unblock it. You need a one-time code sent to your registered phone number.
- **Lost or stolen.** If the card is lost for good or stolen, report it as lost or stolen. The card is cancelled for good and cannot be unblocked. Order a replacement, which costs KES 500 and is ready at the branch you choose within 5 working days.
- **Disputes.** If you see a card payment you do not recognise, report it within 60 days of the transaction date. We acknowledge disputes within 24 hours.

## Keeping the app and your phone safe

Your phone is the key to your Kaskazi app, so protect it as you would your card.

- **Lock your phone** with a PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face, and keep its software up to date.
- **Download the Kaskazi app only from your phone's official app store.** Never install an app someone sends you a link to.
- **If your phone is lost or stolen,** tell us at once through online banking, the assistant on another device, or a branch, so we can stop app access on that phone. Block your cards too.
- **If your SIM suddenly stops working,** someone may have swapped it to receive your codes. Contact your network and tell us straight away.
- **Check your transactions** regularly and report anything you do not recognise.

## Fees for digital banking

Kaskazi charges nothing for its digital channels. You pay only the normal fee for any payment you make.

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Kaskazi app and online banking | KES 0 |
| USSD session | KES 0 from Kaskazi (your mobile network may charge) |
| Statement in the app | KES 0 |
| Printed statement at a branch | KES 300 per statement |

Payment fees, such as instant transfers to other banks (KES 30, KES 60 or KES 120 by amount) and mobile money transfers (KES 15 to KES 85 by amount), are set out in full in the "Fees and charges (tariff guide)".
