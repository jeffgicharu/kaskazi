# Kaskazi Credit Card: terms and conditions

These terms explain how the Kaskazi Credit Card works: who can have one, how your credit limit is set, when interest is charged, what you must pay each month and what each fee is for. They apply from 1 October 2026.

## About the Kaskazi Credit Card

The Kaskazi Credit Card lets you pay now and pay Kaskazi Bank back later. Unlike a debit card, it doesn't take money from your account at the moment you pay. Instead, each payment is added to your credit card balance, and once a month we send you a statement showing what you owe.

You can use the Kaskazi Credit Card:

- in shops in Kenya and abroad
- online and in apps
- for cash at ATMs, although cash withdrawals cost more (see the section on cash advances)

If you pay your full statement balance by the payment due date every month, you pay no interest on purchases. If you pay less, interest is charged on what you carry over.

The Kaskazi Credit Card is a form of borrowing. Only use it for amounts you are confident you can repay.

## Who can apply for a Kaskazi Credit Card

To apply for a Kaskazi Credit Card you need:

- a net monthly pay of at least KES 50,000
- a national ID card or passport
- a KRA PIN
- a phone number registered in your name
- a good enough credit record, which we check with licensed credit reference bureaus

Net pay is what you actually take home each month after tax and other deductions from your pay, not your gross salary. For example, if your gross salary is KES 70,000 but your take-home pay after deductions is KES 46,000, you don't meet the Kaskazi Credit Card's net pay requirement of KES 50,000.

If you are self-employed or your income varies, we look at your bank statements to work out a regular net monthly income. We use the same KES 50,000 test.

Meeting the requirements doesn't guarantee a Kaskazi Credit Card. We also look at the loans and cards you already have and how you have repaid them.

### Documents for a Kaskazi Credit Card application

Bring or upload:

- your national ID card or passport, which must not have expired
- your KRA PIN
- your latest payslips, or your bank statements if your income isn't a regular salary or isn't paid into Kaskazi

If your salary is already paid into your Kaskazi account, we can see it there, and you may not need to send payslips. Names must match on every document.

## Your Kaskazi Credit Card limit

Your credit limit is the most you can owe on your Kaskazi Credit Card at any time. A Kaskazi Credit Card limit is at least KES 30,000 and at most KES 500,000.

We set your limit within that range after looking at:

- your net monthly pay
- the loans, cards and other repayments you already have
- your credit record with credit reference bureaus
- how you have managed your Kaskazi accounts

We tell you your limit before you start using the card.

### Changing your Kaskazi Credit Card limit

You can ask for a higher or lower limit at any time in the app, through the assistant or at a branch. A higher limit needs a new assessment of your income and credit record, and may be refused. A lower limit takes effect straight away.

We will never raise your limit without your agreement. We may lower it if your circumstances change or you miss payments, and we will tell you when we do.

## Payments that would take you over your Kaskazi Credit Card limit

A Kaskazi Credit Card payment or withdrawal that would take your balance over your credit limit is declined. There is no over-limit fee, because we don't let you go over the limit with a purchase or withdrawal.

### Example: a payment that is declined

Your Kaskazi Credit Card limit is KES 100,000 and your balance is KES 96,000.

- You try to pay KES 5,000. Your balance would become KES 101,000, which is over your limit, so the payment is declined.
- You try to pay KES 4,000 instead. Your balance would become exactly KES 100,000. That is within your limit, so the payment goes through.

### When interest or fees take your balance over the limit

Interest and fees are added to your balance even if they take it over your limit. If that happens, new Kaskazi Credit Card payments and withdrawals are declined until a repayment brings your balance back below your limit. Your limit is freed up as soon as your repayment reaches your card account.

## Interest on the Kaskazi Credit Card

Interest on the Kaskazi Credit Card is 2.5% a month on balances carried over. That is 30% a year (2.5% × 12 months).

"Carried over" means the part of your statement balance you haven't repaid by the payment due date. If you pay your full statement balance by the due date, there is nothing carried over and no interest is charged on your purchases.

Interest is added to your balance and shown on your next statement. Cash advances are different: they are charged interest from the day you take the cash, whether or not you pay in full (see the section on cash advances).

### Example: carrying over part of a statement balance

Your Kaskazi Credit Card statement shows a balance of KES 40,000. By the due date you pay KES 10,000. You carry over KES 30,000.

If the KES 30,000 stays unpaid for the whole of the next month, the interest is 2.5% of KES 30,000 = KES 750. This is added to your balance on your next statement.

If you pay off part of the KES 30,000 during the month, the interest is lower, because it is worked out on what you still owe.

### New purchases when you carry a balance on the Kaskazi Credit Card

New purchases you make after a statement keep their interest-free period. If you pay the next statement in full by its due date, you pay no interest on those new purchases, even though you carried over a balance the month before.

## The interest-free period of up to 50 days

The Kaskazi Credit Card gives you up to 50 days without interest on purchases. The interest-free period runs from the day you make a purchase to the payment due date of the statement that purchase appears on.

The interest-free period applies only when you pay your full statement balance by the payment due date. It does not apply to cash advances.

Why "up to"? Each statement covers about a month of purchases, and the payment due date comes some days after the statement date. A purchase made on the first day of a statement period waits the longest before it is due, so it gets the full 50 days. A purchase made on the last day of a statement period gets the fewest days. So the number of interest-free days depends on when in the statement period you make the purchase.

### Worked example: interest-free days on the Kaskazi Credit Card

In this example, a statement period runs from 1 May to 31 May, and the payment due date for that statement is 20 June. Your own statement shows your actual dates.

| You buy on | Appears on the statement dated | Payment due | Interest-free days |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 May | 31 May | 20 June | 50 days |
| 15 May | 31 May | 20 June | 36 days |
| 31 May | 31 May | 20 June | 20 days |

If you pay the full balance on the 31 May statement by 20 June, none of these purchases is charged any interest.

If you buy something on 1 June, it falls into the next statement period and appears on the 30 June statement, with its own later due date.

### Tip: making the most of the interest-free period

If you are planning a large purchase and want the longest time to pay without interest, make it just after your statement date. Our app shows your statement date and your next payment due date.

### When you lose the interest-free period on the Kaskazi Credit Card

If you pay less than your full statement balance by the due date, interest of 2.5% a month is charged on the balance you carry over. You get the interest-free period back on later purchases as soon as you pay a statement in full by its due date.

## Your Kaskazi Credit Card statement

You get a Kaskazi Credit Card statement every month. Statements in the Kaskazi app are free. A printed statement from a branch costs KES 300.

Each statement shows:

- your statement date and payment due date
- every purchase, cash advance, fee, interest charge and repayment in the period
- your statement balance (the total you owe on the statement date)
- your minimum payment
- your credit limit and how much of it you have available

Check your statement as soon as it arrives. If you see a payment you don't recognise or a payment taken twice, report it within 60 days of the transaction date. Our document on disputing a card payment explains how.

## The minimum payment on the Kaskazi Credit Card

Each month you must pay at least the minimum payment by the payment due date. The minimum payment is the higher of:

- 5% of your statement balance, or
- KES 1,000

If your statement balance is lower than KES 1,000, the minimum payment is the full balance.

### Examples of the Kaskazi Credit Card minimum payment

| Statement balance | 5% of the balance | Minimum payment |
|---|---|---|
| KES 150,000 | KES 7,500 | KES 7,500 |
| KES 60,000 | KES 3,000 | KES 3,000 |
| KES 20,000 | KES 1,000 | KES 1,000 |
| KES 12,000 | KES 600 | KES 1,000 (5% is below KES 1,000) |
| KES 800 | KES 40 | KES 800 (the full balance, as it is below KES 1,000) |

### Example: paying only the minimum on the Kaskazi Credit Card

Your statement balance is KES 60,000. The minimum payment is 5% of it, KES 3,000. You pay KES 3,000 by the due date.

- You carry over KES 57,000.
- If it stays unpaid for the whole next month, interest is 2.5% of KES 57,000 = KES 1,425.

Paying only the minimum keeps your account in good standing and avoids the late payment fee, but it is the most expensive way to repay. Pay as much as you can above the minimum, and the full balance if you can.

## Late payment on the Kaskazi Credit Card

If we don't receive at least your minimum payment by the payment due date, a late payment fee of KES 1,000 is added to your balance. Interest of 2.5% a month is also charged on the whole balance you carry over.

### Example: a missed Kaskazi Credit Card payment

Your statement balance is KES 60,000 and the minimum payment is KES 3,000. You pay nothing by the due date.

- Late payment fee: KES 1,000
- Interest if the KES 60,000 stays unpaid for the whole next month: 2.5% of KES 60,000 = KES 1,500
- Added to your balance: KES 2,500 in all

Missing payments also affects your credit record. We report how you repay your Kaskazi Credit Card to licensed credit reference bureaus, and missed payments can make it harder to borrow in future. If we lower your credit limit or stop the card after missed payments, we will tell you.

If you are struggling to pay, tell us early, through the assistant, in the app or at a branch. We would rather agree a plan with you than add fees.

## Ways to pay your Kaskazi Credit Card

You can pay your Kaskazi Credit Card:

- from your Kaskazi account in the Kaskazi app; there is no charge for moving money between your own Kaskazi accounts
- by transfer from another bank; the other bank's charges and timings apply
- at any Kaskazi branch

Your payment counts on the day it reaches your Kaskazi Credit Card account. A payment from your Kaskazi account in the app arrives straight away. A transfer from another bank may take longer, depending on how and when the other bank sends it. An instant transfer usually arrives within minutes, but a standard bank transfer (EFT) can take until the next working day. Pay a few days before the due date if you are paying from another bank.

### Paying more than you owe on the Kaskazi Credit Card

If you pay more than your balance, the extra stays on your Kaskazi Credit Card as a credit balance and is used for your next purchases. You can ask us to move it back to your Kaskazi account at any time.

## Annual fee for the Kaskazi Credit Card

The Kaskazi Credit Card annual fee is KES 2,000. It is waived for the first year.

The first annual fee is charged when your card has been open for a year, and again each year after that. It is added to your balance and shown on your statement. Like a purchase, it is not charged interest if you pay that statement in full by the due date.

For example, if your Kaskazi Credit Card was opened on 10 November 2026, you pay no annual fee in the first year. The first KES 2,000 is charged on 10 November 2027.

If you close your Kaskazi Credit Card before the annual fee is due, the fee is not charged.

## Cash advances on the Kaskazi Credit Card

A cash advance is cash you take out with your Kaskazi Credit Card at an ATM, in Kenya or abroad. Cash advances cost more than purchases.

- Cash advance fee: 3% of the amount, with a minimum of KES 500
- Interest: 2.5% a month, charged from the day of the withdrawal, with no interest-free period

Interest on a cash advance starts straight away, even if you pay your full statement balance by the due date. To keep the cost down, repay a cash advance as soon as you can.

### Examples of the Kaskazi Credit Card cash advance fee

| Cash taken | 3% of the amount | Cash advance fee |
|---|---|---|
| KES 10,000 | KES 300 | KES 500 (the minimum) |
| KES 20,000 | KES 600 | KES 600 |
| KES 30,000 | KES 900 | KES 900 |

### Example: the full cost of a Kaskazi Credit Card cash advance

You take KES 20,000 in cash with your Kaskazi Credit Card.

- Cash advance fee: 3% of KES 20,000 = KES 600, added to your balance straight away
- Interest if the KES 20,000 stays unpaid for a whole month: 2.5% of KES 20,000 = KES 500
- Cost after one month: KES 1,100

If you need cash, a Kaskazi Debit Card withdrawal from your own account is much cheaper, and free at a Kaskazi ATM.

### Cash advances abroad and ATM owners' fees

When you take cash abroad with the Kaskazi Credit Card, the 3% currency conversion fee applies as well as the cash advance fee. The ATM's owner may also charge its own fee, which it must show on screen before you go ahead.

Cash advances count towards your Kaskazi Credit Card limit in the same way as purchases.

## Using the Kaskazi Credit Card abroad and in other currencies

You can use the Kaskazi Credit Card wherever the card network shown on the card is accepted. Any payment in a currency other than Kenya shillings has a currency conversion fee of 3% of the amount. This applies abroad and when you shop online with a business that charges in another currency.

The foreign amount is converted to Kenya shillings at the card network's exchange rate on the day the payment is processed, and the 3% fee is added.

### Example: a purchase abroad on the Kaskazi Credit Card

You book a hotel abroad. At the card network's rate, the bill comes to KES 15,000.

- Purchase: KES 15,000
- Currency conversion fee: 3% of KES 15,000 = KES 450
- Added to your balance: KES 15,450

If you pay your statement in full by the due date, no interest is charged on the purchase or the conversion fee.

If a shop or ATM abroad offers to charge you in Kenya shillings, the 3% fee does not apply, but the shop's own exchange rate is used, and it is often less favourable. Compare before you choose.

## PIN, contactless and online payments on the Kaskazi Credit Card

You choose your Kaskazi Credit Card PIN when you collect the card at the branch, and you can change it at any Kaskazi ATM or in the app.

If you enter the wrong PIN too many times in a row, the card is locked for PIN payments and withdrawals until you reset your PIN in the app (with a one-time code sent to your registered phone number) or at a branch with your ID.

Contactless payments have a limit for each payment. Above it, the card reader asks you to insert the card and enter your PIN.

For online payments, we may send a one-time code to your registered phone number to confirm the payment. Check the business name and amount in the message before you enter it. A one-time code works for 5 minutes.

## Lost or stolen Kaskazi Credit Card and fraud

If your Kaskazi Credit Card is lost or stolen, block it as lost or stolen in the app or through the assistant straight away, at any time of day or night. A card blocked as lost or stolen is cancelled for good. A replacement Kaskazi Credit Card costs KES 500 and is ready to collect within 5 working days at the branch you choose.

If you're not sure the card is lost, use a temporary block, which you can undo yourself with a one-time code.

If someone uses your Kaskazi Credit Card without your permission:

- before you report it, the most you lose is KES 5,000, unless you were careless with your PIN or a one-time code
- after you report it, you lose nothing (KES 0)

Our document on blocking, unblocking and replacing a card has the full steps.

## Disputed payments on the Kaskazi Credit Card

If you dispute a Kaskazi Credit Card payment, report it within 60 days of the transaction date. We acknowledge disputes within 24 hours. For disputes on amounts up to KES 20,000, we give you a provisional credit within 10 working days while we investigate. Investigations take up to 45 days.

While we investigate, keep paying at least the minimum payment on the rest of your balance. If the dispute is decided in your favour, we refund the disputed amount and any interest and fees charged on it.

## What Kaskazi Bank will never ask you for

Kaskazi Bank will never ask you for your Kaskazi Credit Card PIN, your full card number, the security code on the back of the card, a one-time code or your app password. We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account" or send anyone to collect your card.

Anyone who asks for these is a scammer, even if they seem to know your details. End the conversation and tell us. If you have already shared something, block the card in the app or through the assistant first.

## Closing your Kaskazi Credit Card

You can close your Kaskazi Credit Card at any time in the app, through the assistant or at a branch.

- Stop using the card and cancel any subscriptions or regular payments on it.
- Pay off the full balance. Interest continues on any balance carried over until it is paid.
- Payments approved before you asked to close the card, and interest and fees already due, still have to be paid.

Once the balance is zero, we close the card and confirm it to you. Cut the card through the chip and the card number.

### When Kaskazi Bank may stop or cancel the card

We may stop your Kaskazi Credit Card, lower your limit or ask you to repay the balance if you break these terms, miss payments repeatedly, if we reasonably suspect fraud, or if the law requires it. We will tell you why, unless the law prevents us.

## Changes to the Kaskazi Credit Card terms and complaints

We may change the interest rate, fees or these terms. We will tell you before a change takes effect, in the app and by message to your registered phone number. If you don't accept a change, you can close your card and repay the balance on the terms that applied before.

If you are unhappy with our service, tell us in the app, through the assistant, at a branch or through our contact centre (number not available in this demonstration). We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 7 working days, or within 30 days if the complaint is complex. Each complaint gets a reference starting "KZC-" followed by 6 digits.
