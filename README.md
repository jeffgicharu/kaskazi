# Kaskazi Bank

An AI platform for an invented Kenyan bank. Kaskazi Bank isn't real, and everything in it,
from its products and fees to its customers, is sample data.

- **Customer assistant:** answers from the bank's own documents with citations, does tasks
  through tools on a pretend core-banking service, asks for confirmation before anything changes
  an account, and hands over to a person.
- **Document desk:** reads payslips, IDs and statements, with a confidence for each field,
  checks and a human review queue.
- **Staff console:** handovers, reviews, an append-only audit trail, evaluations and monitoring.
